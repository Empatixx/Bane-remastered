title: Run stats
description: What the game counts during a run, how the gold reward is calculated from it at the end and which stats your profile remembers forever.
---

During every run the game quietly tracks how you play — how much you shoot and hit, how many you kill and what you collect.
At the end of the run it calculates a **gold** reward from it, which you spend in the lobby on weapon upgrades.

## How it works

### What is counted during a run

Stats carry over between floors and add up over the whole run.

| Stat | When it is added |
|------------|----------------|
| Shots | every fired projectile — a shotgun shot counts as many times as it has pellets |
| Hits | a hit on an enemy **with your bullet**; explosions, the pet, summoned helpers and poison do not count |
| Kills | every killed enemy (except the boss's small minions), even if a trap or the pet killed it; it is also counted by enemy type |
| Items | every coin, ammo, potion, weapon and artefact picked up |
| Coins | every coin picked up |
| Hits on the floor | how many times something hit you on the current floor (even a hit absorbed by a shield) |
| Untouched floors | +1 for every floor you leave (or finish by defeating the last boss) without being hit |

### Summary at the end of the run

The final screen appears after death, after a win or after choosing *Die* in the pause menu.

- **Accuracy** = hits ÷ shots (0% if you did not shoot).
- The **gold reward** has four rows. The first three grow with the floor reached and with the stat value — roughly
  *multiplier × floor × value*, with a slight acceleration at higher numbers:

| Row | Reward |
|-------|---------|
| Accuracy: N% | multiplier 2 × floor × accuracy in % (full only from 50 shots) |
| Kills: N | multiplier 2 × floor × number of kills |
| Loot: N | multiplier 4 × floor × number of items |
| Floor: N | 5 gold for every floor after the first |

**Example:** floor 3, accuracy 60%, 40 kills, 20 items → 383 + 254 + 252 + 10 = **899 gold**.

The result is written to the profile and the mission and the chosen challenge are evaluated — a completed challenge adds talents. If you
completed something, a note is shown right on the final screen.

### The final screen

First a skull slides in, then the heading and then the four rows one after another (an icon, a label and a counting-up "+N" of gold).
When the animation finishes, a blinking prompt asks you to continue — any key takes you back to the lobby.

### What the profile remembers forever

| Stat | Meaning |
|------------|--------|
| Gold | current gold and total gold earned |
| Talents | currency for [talents](/docs/player/upgrades) |
| Runs | the number of runs, wins and deaths |
| Kills and items | totals over all runs |
| Shots and hits | totals over all runs |
| Deepest floor | record |

## Tips and connections

- Missions track kills (also by enemy type), floor reached, coins, items and untouched floors — see [Run and progression](/docs/progression).
- You spend gold on weapon upgrade trees — [Guns](/docs/guns).
- Collecting anything pays off: the Loot row has a multiplier twice that of kills.
- Depth multiplies everything — one extra floor raises all three main rewards.

## Known inconsistencies

- A hit absorbed by a shield (*Shield Horn*) counts as a hit and ruins the untouched floor (see [Health, armor and damage](/docs/player/damage)).
- The floor you die on never counts toward untouched floors.
- Accuracy penalizes shotguns — every pellet is a separate "shot", and missing a few pellets is normal.

## Ideas for expansion

- A profile stats screen — the game collects them, but the player never sees them anywhere (except talents and gold in the lobby).
- More stats: run time, number of dodges, strongest hit, boss kills, coins spent.
