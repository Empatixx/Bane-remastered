title: Health, armor and damage
description: How enemies and traps hurt you, what armor is for, how invulnerability works, critical hits and all temporary effects on the character.

The character has two meters: **health** and **armor**. Armor works as an extra shield — hits take from it first
and only then from health. When health drops to zero, the run ends.

## How it works

### When something hits you

1. If you are **invulnerable** (after a recent hit or during a roll), the hit is ignored.
2. The **Kevlar** talent reduces damage by 10%; a fraction of a point is decided by chance. A hit for 1 deals nothing 10% of the time, and a hit for 3 deals only 2 in 30% of cases.
3. If you have an active **shield** from the artefact *Shield Horn*, the hit breaks it and deals no damage.
4. Damage is subtracted from **armor**; whatever does not fit into armor takes **health**. Some sources (lava, magma)
   **ignore** armor and go straight to health.
5. A hit sound plays. The first time you drop below 30% health, a warning signal sounds and the screen edges start pulsing red.
6. **1 second of invulnerability** follows and the character flashes briefly.
7. The hit counts in the stats — a floor where you were hit does not count as "untouched" (the mission *Untouched*).

### When you deal damage

The damage of each projectile is calculated like this:

1. **Base** — a random value from the weapon's range (including upgrades from the weapon tree).
2. **Talents** — *Sharpened Bullets* +20% (you always have it; in the challenge *Glass Cannon* even 2.4×) and *Dangerous Bullets* +25% are multiplied together (×1.5). A fraction of a point turns into a chance of an extra point — a hit for 1 deals 2 on every fifth hit with *Sharpened Bullets*.
3. **Critical hit** — double.
4. **Sneak attack** — the first shot from invisibility (*Phantom Cloak*) deals double and ends the invisibility.

**Critical hit chance** = 10% from the talent *Critical Hit* + the weapon's own chance (Sniper 20%) + a bonus from the
weapon tree. The upgrade *Deadeye* makes every fifth shot critical. A critical hit is decided for each
projectile separately — with a shotgun every pellet has its own chance (only *Deadeye* crits all pellets at once).

A hit enemy flashes white briefly and a damage number flies up above it. Explosions, plasma and chain lightning deal
half damage (at least 1) — see [Guns](/docs/guns).

### Healing and recovery

- Health / armor potion: small +1, large +4 (up to the maximum; you cannot pick it up when the meter is full).
- The talent *Armor Regeneration*: +1 armor after clearing a room (the challenge *Bane* turns it off).
- The artefacts *Armor Refill* and *Blood Chalice* (heals 50%).
- Nothing recovers between floors.

### Death

When health drops to 0, the character stops and the final run screen appears. In the pause menu you can end the run
at any time by choosing *Die*.

## Tips and connections

- The talents *Kevlar*, *Critical Hit*, *Sharpened Bullets*, *Dangerous Bullets*, *Hero's Heart*, *Titan's Heart* and
  *Improved Armor*: [Talents](/docs/player/upgrades).
- The red screen edges and other effects: [Interface](/docs/player/hud).
- Armor only protects against "normal" hits — against lava and magma rely on the roll, not on armor.
- A shotgun with a high critical chance sometimes deals huge "jackpot" shots.

## Known inconsistencies

- The **shield from Shield Horn** absorbs the hit, but the game still treats it as a hit: it plays the damage sound, gives you
  invulnerability and ruins the "untouched" floor for the mission *Untouched*.
- The same applies when **Kevlar** knocks a hit for 1 down to zero — it deals nothing, but it counts as a hit.
- The **red screen flash** on a hit appears only on enemy touch, projectiles, spikes, flame traps or lava — arrows from traps, explosions,
  poison, Golem attacks, Leaper impacts and Mimics hurt you without a flash.

## Ideas for expansion

- Damage numbers above the character (enemies have them).
- More effects on the player: burning, bleeding, slowed shooting; poison that works even after leaving the puddle.
- Resistances against specific sources (e.g. a talent or artefact "poison immunity").
