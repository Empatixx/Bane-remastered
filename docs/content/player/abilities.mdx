title: What the player can do
description: An overview of everything the character can actively do — movement, dodge, shooting, artefact, interaction with the surroundings — and of passive helpers.
---

The character has no spells or special abilities with cooldowns — the only such action is the dodge. Everything
else is handled by weapons, one-use artefacts and interaction with items around you.

<Callout type="info">
The special attacks you see on enemies (the Smash impact, explosion on death, poison cloud, healing allies, homing
projectiles…) are abilities of **enemies**, not of the player — they are described on the [Enemies](/docs/enemies) page.
</Callout>

## How it works

### Active actions

| Action | Key | Limit | More |
|------|---------|---------|------|
| Walking | WASD | surfaces slow it down, freezing stops it | [Movement and dodge](/docs/player/movement-dodge) |
| Dodge with invulnerability | Space | 1.26 s cooldown; disabled in the challenge *Two Left Feet* | [Movement and dodge](/docs/player/movement-dodge) |
| Shooting | left button | not during a dodge or freeze | [Guns](/docs/guns) |
| Reloading | R | pointless with infinite ammo | [Guns](/docs/guns) |
| Switching weapons | 1–9, wheel | 2 slots, 3 with *Additional Gun* | [Guns](/docs/guns) |
| Dropping a weapon | Q | you throw the weapon toward the cursor | [Guns](/docs/guns) |
| Using the artefact | F | one slot, the artefact is used up | [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items) |
| Interaction | E | you must stand next to the object | below |
| Big map | Tab | | [Interface](/docs/player/hud) |

### Interacting with the surroundings

When you stand next to an object you can do something with, a label appears above it. Pressing the Interact key
(E by default) performs the action. The label always shows the key you actually have set — the table
lists the default key.

When you stand next to several objects at once (for example two weapons lying side by side), the label shows
only on the **nearest** one and the key uses exactly that one. Move a little and the prompt switches
to the other object.

| Object | Where | Label | What happens |
|--------|-----|-------|-----------|
| Portal | lobby | Press E to enter the portal | starts a new run |
| Scientist | lobby | Press E to upgrade | opens / closes the talent menu |
| Board | lobby | Press E to read the board | missions and challenges |
| Weapon table | lobby | Press E to upgrade weapons | weapon upgrade trees for gold |
| Stairs | dungeon | Press E to enter next floor | descend to the next floor |
| Weapon on the ground | dungeon | | you take it; with full slots you drop the one you hold for it |
| Artefact on the ground | dungeon | | swaps with the artefact in your slot |
| Goods in the shop | shop | Press E to buy | you buy with coins |
| Slot machine | shop (with the talent Gamble Machine) | Press E to spin | a spin costs 5 coins |

Coins, ammo and potions are picked up automatically when you walk over them. Weapon chests open on touch.

### Passive helpers

- **Talents** — permanent upgrades from the lobby: [Talents](/docs/player/upgrades).
- **Pet** — the talent *Pet*, bites enemies and collects loot: [Talents → Pet](/docs/player/upgrades#pet).
- **Weapon upgrades** (e.g. *Deadeye*): [Guns](/docs/guns).

## Tips and connections

- Characters and objects in the lobby (Scientist, board, weapon table, portal): [Run and progression](/docs/progression).
- The shop and the slot machine: [Dungeon and world](/docs/dungeon).
- The artefact is the only "ultimate" you have — save it for a boss or a crowded room.

## Known inconsistencies

- Weapons and artefacts lying on the ground have no label of their own — you only recognize them by a yellow outline.

## Ideas for expansion

- In-run upgrades ("boons" after rooms) as a natural place for active abilities.
- A rechargeable ability instead of a one-use artefact.
- Candidates: an attack dash, a grenade on the right button, deflecting projectiles (parry), a short shield with a cooldown.
