title: Movement and dodge
description: How fast the character moves, what slows it down and how the dodge roll works, during which you are invulnerable.
---

The character does not move in "jumps" — it takes a moment to get running, and after you release the keys it coasts a little.
The main defense is the **dodge roll**: a short fast move during which nothing can hurt you.

## How it works

### Walking

- You reach full speed in about half a second to one second; after you release the keys you stop in under 2 seconds.
  Turning to the opposite direction takes longer than just stopping.
- Base speed is about 6.4 tiles per second, with the talent *Swiftwind Steps* (which you always have) about 7 tiles per second.
- The character's animation follows the direction you walk in (not the aim direction).
- In water you leave ripples behind you.

### What changes speed

| Influence | Speed | Note |
|------|----------|----------|
| Water | 55% | |
| Swamp | 45% | |
| Cobweb | 50% | cobwebs on the ground and also the ones spat by spider enemies |
| Ice | 100% | speed does not change, but the character slides — starting and stopping take over 3× longer |
| Berserk (artefact *Berserk Pot*) | 130% | 15 s |
| Talent *Swiftwind Steps* | 110% | always active |
| Freeze (frozen barrel) | — | for 1.5 s you cannot control movement, you only coast on momentum |

Slowdowns from surfaces do not stack — only one applies (water takes priority over swamp and swamp over cobweb).
Berserk and the talent multiply with them.

### Dodge (roll)

| Property | Value |
|-----------|---------|
| Key | Space |
| Roll length | 0.36 s |
| Speed | about 12 tiles per second (almost 2× faster than walking) |
| Distance covered | about 4 tiles |
| Cooldown | 1.26 s from the start of the roll |
| Invulnerability | for the whole roll |

Dodge rules:

- The roll goes in the direction you are moving. When you stand still, you roll toward the cursor.
- You cannot dodge during another roll, during the cooldown, when frozen, or in the challenge *Two Left Feet*, which disables the dodge.
- During the roll you **do not shoot**, surfaces do not slow you and you are immune to all damage.
- With a roll you can safely fly over **lava**, the **Golem's laser** and a **frozen barrel explosion**.
- A special animation plays during the roll, a short **swish** sounds (two variants alternate) and bluish "shadows"
  stay behind the character.
- After the roll a white bar appears below the character and shortens — when it disappears, you can dodge again.

The dodge works in the lobby too.

## Tips and connections

- Invulnerability from the roll and after a hit is the same effect — see [Health, armor and damage](/docs/player/damage).
- Surfaces and traps: [Dungeon and world](/docs/dungeon).
- Cobwebs are spat by the spider boss and the *WebSpitter*: [Enemies](/docs/enemies).
- *Berserk Pot*: [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- The challenge *Two Left Feet* (no dodge): [Run and progression](/docs/progression).
- On ice start braking earlier — the coasting distance is much longer than on a normal floor.

## Known inconsistencies

- **Reloading** is also paused during the roll — if you dodge in the middle of a reload, it finishes only after the roll.
- Neither Berserk nor *Swiftwind Steps* affects the length or speed of the roll.

## Ideas for expansion

- Talents for the dodge: shorter cooldown, a double roll, a "perfect dodge" with time slow on a close escape.
- Turning the character by aim direction (like in Enter the Gungeon).
