title: Interface (HUD)
description: Everything you see on the screen in the game and in the lobby — health and armor meters, weapon and ammo, artefact, coins, map, arrows to enemies and screen effects.

The interface shows the state of the character, the weapon and the surroundings. Most elements are in the corners of the screen so they do not block
the view; labels, price tags and damage numbers are shown right in the world above objects.

## How it works

### In the game

| Element | Where | What it shows |
|-------|-----|------------|
| Health meter | top left | a fill based on remaining health; when you lose health a lighter "trail" stays for a moment and slowly catches up, when you heal it is greenish |
| Armor meter | below the health meter | the same for armor, gray-blue color |
| Boss meter | top center | the boss's health and its name in red; it disappears after its death |
| Weapon | bottom right | the icon of the weapon you are holding |
| Ammo | bottom right below the weapon | "magazine/reserve", e.g. 12/120 |
| Rounds in the magazine | in the weapon slot | one icon per round (for shotguns, shotgun shells); a fired round "empties" |
| Infinite ammo | by the ammo | with *Infinity Quiver* an animated ∞ is shown instead of numbers and the rounds shimmer gold |
| Artefact | left of the weapon | the icon of the artefact in the slot; an empty slot is not shown |
| Coins | bottom left | the number of coins you carry |
| Minimap | top right | nearby rooms, the current one highlighted, unvisited ones fainter |
| Big map | Tab, full screen | can be moved with the mouse; close it with Esc or Tab; the artefact *Reveal Map* reveals the whole floor |
| Arrows to enemies | screen edges | a semi-transparent arrow to every living enemy off screen; nearby arrows merge |
| Dodge cooldown | below the character | a white bar that shortens until you can dodge again |
| Wave | center | "Wave N/M" — only in rooms with several waves of enemies |
| Interaction labels | above an object | "Press E to …" when you stand in range |
| Price tags | above goods in the shop | the price; on a sale in green with the text "SALE x%" |
| Damage numbers | above an enemy | flying −N (damage) or +N (healing) |
| Cursor | mouse | a crosshair; while reloading it changes to a "Reload" cursor; in the pause menu, on the map and on the final screen a normal arrow |

### Screen effects

| Effect | When | What it looks like |
|-------|------|------------|
| Red flash | a hit (touch, projectile, trap, lava) | the screen edges turn red and fade within about half a second |
| Pulsing red edges | health below 30% | constantly, a slow pulse every 3 s |
| Blur and darkening | pause | the image blurs and darkens |
| Time slow | *Hourglass of Ash* | a smooth start and end of the effect |
| Camera shake | shots from stronger weapons, explosions, lightning | the image shakes |

### In the lobby

| Element | What it shows |
|-------|------------|
| Health and armor meters | the same as in the game |
| Counters bottom left | the number of talents and the gold in your profile |
| Talent menu | see [Talents](/docs/player/upgrades) |

### Final run screen

A skull, a heading and four rows (Accuracy, Kills, Loot, Floor) with gold rewards — see [Run stats](/docs/player/stats).

## Tips and connections

- Where the meter values come from: [Health, armor and damage](/docs/player/damage); ammo and weapons: [Guns](/docs/guns).
- The minimap and rooms: [Dungeon and world](/docs/dungeon); artefacts: [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- The arrows on the edges help you find the last enemy that blocks the room's doors.

## Known inconsistencies

- The red flash on a hit is missing for arrows from traps, explosions, poison and boss attacks (see [Health, armor and damage](/docs/player/damage)).

## Ideas for expansion

- A meter for the remaining duration of effects (Berserk 15 s, invisibility 5 s …) — right now you do not know when an effect ends.
- A numeric display of health and armor, not just a fill.
- The number of the current floor and an overview of the weapons in all slots.
- An indicator of the active challenge during the run.
