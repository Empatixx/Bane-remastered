Talents are **permanent character upgrades** that stay with you across all runs. You buy them in the lobby from the Scientist
("Press E to upgrade") with the currency **Talents**, which you earn by completing missions and challenges.

## How it works

- Talents are divided into **3 trees of 5 nodes** — Utility, Offensive and Defensive. Each tree is a simple
  chain: you can buy the next talent only when you own the previous one.
- **The first talent of every tree is free** from the start.
- **The other talents cost by row:** row 2 costs 2, row 3 costs 3, rows 4 and 5 cost 5 talents each. A whole tree costs 45 talents.
- After buying, an unlock sound plays and the talent applies from the next run.
- Some challenges change or disable talents (see below).
- A talent's quality (Common / Rare / Epic) is only the color of the frame — it does not affect the price.

Talents are obtained as rewards from missions and challenges (see [Run and progression](/docs/progression)).

## Contents

The order in the tables matches the order in the tree (1 = root).

### Utility (left column)

| # | Talent | Quality | Cost | In-game description | What it really does |
|---|--------|---------|------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Swiftwind Steps | Common | free | Increases movement speed by 10% | walking speed +10% |
| 2 | Discount | Common | 2 | The shop prices will have a chance to be reduced | in every shop one random table gets a chance of a discount: 23% for −25%, 5% for −50%, 5% for −75% (the price tag shows the subtracted percentage, e.g. "−75%") |
| 3 | Trap Master | Rare | 3 | All traps have a 30% chance to be immobilized | every spike or flame trap has a 30% chance, when the floor is created, to be permanently disabled and harmless |
| 4 | Additional Gun | Epic | 5 | Adds an additional slot for a weapon | 3 weapon slots instead of 2 |
| 5 | Gamble machine | Common | 5 | Unlocks the gamble machine in the shop | a slot machine stands in the shop; without the talent it is not there |

### Offensive (middle column)

| # | Talent | Quality | Cost | In-game description | What it really does |
|---|--------|---------|------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Sharpened Bullets | Common | free | Increases all damage by 20% | damage +20%; a fraction of a point is decided by chance (a weapon for 1 deals 2 on every fifth hit) |
| 2 | Additional Ammo | Common | 2 | Increases ammo capacity by 10% | maximum ammo reserve +10% (e.g. Uzi 200 → 220) |
| 3 | Critical Hit | Rare | 3 | 10% chance to deal double damage | +10% chance of a critical hit for double damage (for a shotgun, for each pellet separately) |
| 4 | Dangerous Bullets | Epic | 5 | Increases all damage by 25% | another +25% damage, together with Sharpened Bullets ×1.5 |
| 5 | Pet | Common | 5 | Unlocks a pet that will help you in battle | a pet in the lobby and on every floor, see below |

### Defensive (right column)

| # | Talent | Quality | Cost | In-game description | What it really does |
|---|--------|---------|------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Hero's Heart | Common | free | Increases initial health by 20% | max health 10 → 12 |
| 2 | Improved Armor | Common | 2 | Increases initial armor by 20% | armor 5 → 6 |
| 3 | Armor Regeneration | Rare | 3 | +1 armor after clearing a room | +1 armor after every cleared room (up to the maximum) |
| 4 | Titan's Heart | Epic | 5 | Increases initial health by 25% | max health 12 → 15 |
| 5 | Kevlar | Common | 5 | Reduces damage taken by 10% | damage taken −10% on average; a fraction of a point is decided by chance (a hit for 1 deals nothing 10% of the time, a hit for 3 deals only 2 in 30%) |

**Fully upgraded character:** 15 health, 6 armor, 3 weapon slots, damage about ×1.5, 10% critical hit chance.

### Pet

The pet is a small companion that follows you everywhere.

| Property | Value |
|-----------|---------|
| Bite | 1 damage, up to 5× per second |
| Attention range | about 8 tiles around itself |
| Speed | about 8 tiles per second toward loot and enemies (faster than you); when following you, it speeds up with distance |
| Stops | about 1.5 tiles from you |
| Teleports to you | when it gets farther than ~8.5 tiles away |

The pet first goes for **loot** (coins and other things you can pick up — not goods in the shop), then for
**enemies**, and when there is nothing, it stays near you. It remembers targets it cannot reach and ignores them.

### Talent menu

- You open it at the Scientist with the E key; close it with Esc or E again. While it is open, the character stands still.
- On the board the root is at the top and below it are three tree columns: Utility, Offensive, Defensive.
- A talent's frame shows its quality and state: owned, available or locked. Locked icons are faded.
- The links between owned talents glow orange and light pulses run along them; the others are gray.
- You move the canvas by dragging the mouse or with the WASD keys.
- Clicking a talent opens a panel on the right with the icon, name, description, cost (e.g. "Cost: 2 talents") and a button
  *Buy* / *Bought* / *Can't buy*.
- Top right is the counter "Talents: N".

## Tips and connections

- Talents affect [movement](/docs/player/movement-dodge), [damage](/docs/player/damage),
  [guns](/docs/guns) and the shop and traps ([Dungeon and world](/docs/dungeon)).
- Challenges change talents: *Glass Cannon* sets max health to 1 but doubles damage; *Pistol Only* allows
  only the pistol; *Two Left Feet* disables the dodge; *Bane* disables *Armor Regeneration* — see [Run and progression](/docs/progression).
- Talents are not the same as **weapon upgrades** — those are bought with gold at the weapon table ("Press E to upgrade
  weapons"), see [Guns](/docs/guns).
- *Critical Hit* adds to the weapon's own chance — with the Sniper you have 30%.

## Known inconsistencies

- **Prices:** an older version of the game had prices 20 / 60 / 140 / 300 / 500; now 2 / 3 / 5 / 5 by row apply.
- **Discount** gives a discount only with a 33% chance in total and only on one table in the shop.

## Ideas for expansion

- Multi-level talents, branching trees and more trees.
- New talents: shorter dodge cooldown, a second artefact slot, starting with a random weapon, healing after a floor,
  more frequent potions, a stronger or second pet.
- The option to sell or reset talents (respec).
