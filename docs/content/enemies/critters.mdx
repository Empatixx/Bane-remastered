---
title: Basic enemies
description: Rat, Snake, Bat and Ghost – the most common enemies, who chase you on all floors.
---

Four animal enemies that appear on **all floors**. Snake and Ghost come in every theme; Rat and Bat come in
every theme except Ice and Lava, where they are replaced by [their own versions](/docs/enemies/ice-lava). All of them go straight at you
and hurt you on touch; they differ in speed, toughness and one small ability.

## Stats

Base values – they grow with every floor (see
[Spawning and enemy strength](/docs/enemies/spawning#strength-by-floor)).

| | Rat | Snake | Bat | Ghost |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| HP | 12 | 8 | 9 | 7 |
| Contact damage | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Speed | 45 % | 42 % | 77 % | 32 % (dash 153 %) |
| Size | elongated | medium | medium | medium |
| Ability | none | poison trail | none | dash |

## How it works

### Rat

The toughest of the basic enemies, medium speed, no ability. Rats are also summoned by the boss
[Sewer ogre](/docs/enemies/bosses/ogre) – those rats have the base stats (12 HP, 2 damage) and drop
nothing. In the main menu, rats and bats run around as decoration.

### Snake – poison trail

The snake leaves a continuous **poison trail** behind it:

- each drop lasts 5 s (it fades during the last second),
- it hurts **only you** – 1 damage on touch, at most once per second because of the invulnerability after a hit,
- you get poisoned (green outline),
- it does not bother other enemies.

The snake is most dangerous when you circle around it – the trail gradually closes off your space.

### Bat

The fastest regular enemy (77 % of your speed). No ability, it pushes with pure speed.

### Ghost – dash

The ghost moves in a 3-second cycle: for **1 second** it dashes at about 153 % of your speed (faster
than you) and leaves shadows behind, then for 2 seconds it drifts slowly. It does the dash **right after
materializing**. The start of every dash is accompanied by a ghostly **whoosh** – even when you cannot
see the ghost, you know it has started moving.

## Tips and connections

- You cannot outrun the ghost during the dash – step aside or use dodge ([Player](/docs/player)), then
  catch up with it while it is slow.
- The snake's trail works the same as the poison cloud of the Bloated spider ([Spiders](/docs/enemies/spiders))
  and the poison barrels in the [dungeon](/docs/dungeon).
- The missions "kill N rats / bats / ghosts / snakes" – see [Progression](/docs/progression).

## Known inconsistencies

- The ghost has no warning before the dash – the whoosh sounds only at the moment it launches – and its
  first dash comes as soon as it materializes.

## Ideas for expansion

- Gradually replace the basic enemies with stronger versions – on floor 5 the rat is just a "bullet sponge".
- A Ghost with ghostly behavior – passing through walls or becoming transparent – and a warning before the dash.
- Themed variants: a snake with poisonous mud for Moss/Sewer, a bat with a fiery trail for Lava.
- None of them shoots – of the regular enemies only Slime, Red slime and Eye watcher shoot.
