---
title: Spawning and enemy strength
description: How many enemies appear where, which ones come on which floor, how they grow stronger and what drops from them.
---

Enemies do not appear pre-placed – they materialize only when you enter a combat room. Which kinds
come depends on the floor and the dungeon theme; how strong they are depends on the floor and the
chosen challenge.

## How it works

### Rooms and waves

Combat starts when you enter a room (at least 2 tiles from the edge). The doors close with bars and
open only when no enemy is left in the room – including the boss's helpers and enemy projectiles that
are still flying.

- **Number of enemies** – one for every 100 tiles of room area, at least 5, at most 12. This is also
  limited by the number of places where enemies can appear.
- **Regular room** – one wave.
- **Ambush** – 3 waves, each about 70 % of the size of a regular wave (at least 4 enemies). The next
  wave comes 1.5 s after the previous one is cleared, and "wave N/3" is shown on the screen. After the
  last wave a **weapon chest** appears.
- **Boss** – the boss appears in the middle of the room, its name and HP bar show at the top and the
  boss music starts. After its death the screen shakes, an **artefact chest** appears and a ladder
  down one floor. On floor 5, **victory** comes instead of the ladder.

### Choosing enemies

Each enemy in a wave is drawn with equal probability from all kinds that belong to the given floor
and theme. At the start of a run, all seven themes (Spider, Stone, Sewer, Moss, Ice, Lava, Temple)
are shuffled randomly and floors 1–5 get the first five – each theme appears at most once in a run
and none is tied to a specific floor.

## Contents

### Regular enemies by floor

| Floor | Theme | Who can appear | Kinds |
|---|---|---|--:|
| 1–2 | any except Spider | Rat, Snake, Bat, Ghost, Slime, Red slime | 6 |
| 1–2 | Spider | the same + Red / Green / Purple spiderling, Bloated spider, Stilt spider | 11 |
| 3–5 | any except Spider | Rat, Snake, Bat, Ghost, Slime, Red slime, Demon eye, Eye watcher | 8 |
| 3–5 | Spider | the same + all five spiders | 13 |

### Bosses by theme

Each theme has one boss of its own, who travels with it to any floor:

| Theme | Boss |
|---|---|
| Spider | Spider king |
| Stone | Stone golem |
| Sewer | Sewer ogre |
| Moss | King slime |
| Ice | Argus |
| Lava | Hellhound |
| Temple | Mimic |

### Strength by floor

Regular enemies **and bosses** grow stronger with the floor: **+12 % HP and +8 % contact damage per floor**
(rounded). Boss helpers, boss attacks and enemy projectiles (always 1 damage) stay the same.
Speed does not change. The values of bosses by floor are in the overview of [Bosses](/docs/enemies/bosses).

| Enemy | HP on floor 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 | Damage on floor 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Rat | 13 / 15 / 16 / 18 / 19 | 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 3 |
| Snake | 9 / 10 / 11 / 12 / 13 | 3 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 4 |
| Bat | 10 / 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 | 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 3 |
| Ghost | 8 / 9 / 10 / 10 / 11 | 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 3 |
| Slime, Red slime | 10 / 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 | 1 |
| Demon eye | – / – / 15 / 16 / 18 | – / – / 4 / 4 / 4 |
| Eye watcher | – / – / 5 / 6 / 6 | 1 |
| Red / Purple spiderling | 3 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 5 | Red 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 3, Purple 1 |
| Green spiderling, Bloated spider | 6 / 6 / 7 / 7 / 8 | Green 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 3, Bloated 1 |
| Stilt spider | 8 / 9 / 10 / 10 / 11 | 1 |

### Challenges

Some challenges strengthen absolutely all enemies – including the boss, its helpers and enemy projectiles.

| Challenge | Enemy HP | Enemy damage |
|---|--:|--:|
| Horde | × 1.6 | × 1.3 |
| Bane | × 1.6 | × 1.3 (and armor does not regenerate) |
| others | unchanged | unchanged |

### Elite enemies

There are none in the game. The only "stronger version" is the boss enrage phase (see [Bosses](/docs/enemies/bosses)).

### Drops

Enemies randomly drop a **coin** or an **ammo box** (for your guns). The game has a safeguard against
bad luck – the longer nothing has dropped, the higher the chance:

| Kills since the last drop | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Drop chance | 33 % | 50 % | 67 % | 83 % | 100 % |

Boss helpers and King slime drop nothing (and also give no bonus coins from Midas). Kills count toward
the missions in [Progression](/docs/progression).

## Tips and connections

- An ambush is hard, but the reward is a weapon chest – see [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- On a Spider floor almost half of the pool is spiders; the other themes have the same pool.
- The Horde and Bane challenges turn bosses into real bullet sponges (Argus has 192 HP on floor 5, over 300 with Horde).
- Room types, themes and bars are described in [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).

## Known inconsistencies

- The number of enemies does not grow with the floor – it depends only on the room size.
- The room does not open while enemy projectiles are flying in the air. With King slime this can take a
  while after its death.
- An enemy can materialize right next to you – the spawn spot ignores your position (you are protected
  only by the fact that a materializing enemy does not deal damage for 2 s).
- All bosses except King slime count toward the drop safeguard as a regular kill.
- According to the story, floor 5 should always be Temple with the final boss; in the game the theme of floor 5 is random.

## Ideas for expansion

- More enemies on higher floors (for example a maximum of 12 + the floor number).
- Rare enemies – different selection chances instead of an even draw.
- Enemies of their own for each theme, so that the floors differ in regular enemies too.
- A safe distance from the player for spawn spots.
