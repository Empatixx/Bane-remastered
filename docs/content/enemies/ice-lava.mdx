The Ice and Lava themes have enemies of their own. In these two themes the plain Rat, Bat and Slime
do not appear at all: each of them is replaced by a themed version, and the Lava theme adds a worm
on top. Snake, Ghost, Red slime, Demon eye and Eye watcher still come in every theme.

## Stats

Base values - they grow with every floor (see
[Spawning and enemy strength](/docs/enemies/spawning#strength-by-floor)).

## How it works

### Chill (Ice)

A hit from a Frost rat, a Snow slime snowball or a Snow slimeling **chills** you: for 2.5 s you walk
at half speed. If you are chilled **3 times in a row**, you are **frozen** for 1 s and cannot move or
shoot. A frozen player cannot move or shoot, so avoid the third chill.

### Frost rat

A Rat with a cold bite. It runs straight at you and every bite that lands chills you.

### Icicle bat

A Bat that also rains icicles. When you are within about 6.5 tiles of it, it drops an icicle **every
3.5 s** on the spot where you stand. A shadow shows on the floor for 0.9 s, then the icicle lands and
hurts for 1 damage in a small area. Move out of the shadow, or use dodge.

### Snow slime

- Like the Slime it crawls slowly and shoots, but its volley is made of **snowballs** that chill.
- When it dies, it **breaks into 2 Snow slimelings**: small, 4 HP, quick to kill, and they chill on touch.

### Ember rat

A slow Rat until it is hit. The first hit makes it **flare up**: it burns brighter and runs
**2.4 times faster**. Kill it in one blow or keep your distance.

### Flame bat

- It circles you for about 3 s, then flares up (0.5 s wind-up) and **swoops** through the place where
  you stand (0.55 s dive, 3.4 times faster than normal).
- When it dies, it leaves a pile of **ash**. The ash **rises again** after 2 s as a new Flame bat unless
  you shoot it first (the ash has 3 HP).

### Magma slime

- It takes turns every 3 s between **molten** and **crusted**.
- Molten: fast and open to hits.
- Crusted: slowed to 40 % and it **shrugs off your hits**, so do not waste ammo on it.
- Every 2.8 s it lobs a ball of magma at you. A shadow shows where it will land, the flight takes 1 s,
  and the blast does 1 damage in a small area.

### Lava worm

- It shows a hole in the floor (0.7 s), rises, spits **3 magma shots** (one every 0.7 s), sinks and
  comes up somewhere else, 3 to 5 tiles from you.
- It can only be hurt **while it is up**. While it is underground it cannot be hit.

## Tips and connections

- Every attack that lands from above (icicle, magma ball) shows a shadow first. Dodge protects against
  them - see [Player](/docs/player).
- Fight the Lava worm while it is up: save a reload for the moment it rises.
- Ice themes end with the boss [Argus](/docs/enemies/bosses/argus), Lava themes with the
  [Hellhound](/docs/enemies/bosses/hellhound).
- Themes and their hazards: [Dungeon themes](/docs/dungeon/themes).

## Known inconsistencies

- The missions "kill N rats / bats / slimes" count only the plain Rat, Bat and Slime, so in Ice and Lava
  rooms the themed versions do not count toward them.

## Ideas for expansion

- A snow variant of the Snake or the Ghost, so that no plain animal is left in these themes.
- A lava version of the Red slime that leaves a puddle of fire.
