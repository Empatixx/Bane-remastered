---
title: Argus
description: A levitating eye that fires rings of projectiles, shoots from seven eyes, surrounds itself with a shield of eyes and teleports.
---

Argus is a giant floating eye. It hardly moves – instead of walking it **teleports** and floods the
room with projectiles. It is protected by orbiting eyes that block your shots.

> "The scientist's eye. He built it to watch the subjects. It got out of hand."

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 120 base, 134–192 on floors 1–5 |
| Contact damage | 2 (3 on floors 4–5) |
| Speed | almost stationary |
| Size | giant |
| Where | any floor, only the Ice theme |
| First attack | after 2 s |
| Pause between attacks | 1.7 s (0.9 s when enraged) |
| Helpers | Argus eye (4, 6 when enraged) |

## How it works

Argus alternates three attacks and teleports after every third one. Your distance does not matter.

| Attack | Chance |
|---|---|
| Volley | medium |
| Barrage | medium |
| Shield | high when it has no eye; low when some are missing; never when it has all of them |

## Contents

### Volley – pulsing rings

- A 0.5 s wind-up is announced by a rising ghostly **chord**, then 5 pulses (7 when enraged) every
  0.4 s. Each pulse gives a hollow **boom**.
- Each pulse is a ring of 10 projectiles (14 when enraged), turned relative to the previous one – together
  they form a spiral. Projectile: 81 % of your speed, 1 damage.

### Barrage – fire from the eyes

- A 0.6 s wind-up (aiming) is announced by the same **chord** as Volley, then 14 shots (22 when
  enraged) in quick succession – each gives a short **squeak**.
- Each projectile comes from one of seven eyes on the body and aims at you with a slight spread. When
  enraged, two projectiles fly at once.

### Shield – orbiting eyes

- A 0.6 s wind-up comes with a ringing **melody**, then it tops up the **Argus eyes** to 4 (6 when enraged).
- The eyes orbit Argus along an ellipse at a distance of about 3 tiles.
- Argus eye: 4 HP, 1 contact damage. They **block your shots** and hurt on touch.
- When Argus dies, the eyes die too.

### Teleport

- After every 3 attacks Argus vanishes with a sucking **whoosh** (0.35 s), appears elsewhere – 4 to 7.5 tiles
  from its old spot, but no closer than 2 tiles to you – and materializes again (0.35 s). Then it has only 0.8 s until the next attack.
- You can hurt it even while it vanishes and appears.

### Enrage (from half HP)

Argus lets out a piercing **screech** and turns red, the pause between attacks drops to 0.9 s, Volley
has more pulses and projectiles, Barrage has more shots and double projectiles, and the shield has 6
eyes instead of 4.

## Tips and connections

- Shoot down the shield eyes before you start shooting at Argus – otherwise they absorb most of your shots.
- After a teleport you have only a moment – watch where it appears.
- The eyes count toward clearing the room, but drop nothing.
- Thematically it is related to the [Eyes](/docs/enemies/eyes).

## Known inconsistencies

- Argus has no close-range attack – the choice of attacks does not depend on how close you are.
