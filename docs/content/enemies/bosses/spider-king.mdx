Spider king is the boss of the **Spider** theme – it waits on whichever floor gets this theme and
appears nowhere else. It is fast, so the main threat is its touch – and on top of that it keeps
hatching new spiderlings and covering the room with webs.

> "Steh. He keeps the wound near the surface closed. Killing him = opening the way down."

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 75 base, 84–120 on floors 1–5 |
| Contact damage | 2 (3 on floors 4–5) |
| Speed | 69 % of the player |
| Size | large |
| Where | any floor, only the Spider theme |
| Enrage | no |
| Helpers | Red, Green and Purple spiderling |

## How it works

Between attacks it keeps chasing you. Its two abilities run at the same time, each on its own timer.

### Hatching spiderlings

- Every **2.5 s** it hatches one spiderling, in the order Red → Green → Purple → Red ...
- The hatching comes with the **cracking of an egg**, a squelch and clicking.
- Spiderlings are active at once, have base stats and drop nothing (Red 3 HP, Green 5 HP and heals the
  others on death, Purple 3 HP and 1 damage). See [Spiders](/docs/enemies/spiders).
- **The number is not limited** – if you do not kill them, 24 per minute will pile up.
- It does not hatch while spitting a web or when it is frozen.

### Web

- Every **5 s** it stops and briefly casts (about 0.45 s) – you hear a rising **hiss and clicking**.
- Then it spits a web at you with a wet **whip sound**. The web flies out quickly, slows down and
  **stays on the ground**.
- When you stand in a web, you move at **half speed**. The web does not deal damage.
- Webs disappear only when the boss dies.

## Contents

| Ability | How often | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Touch | – | 2 damage |
| Hatching | every 2.5 s | 1 spiderling (Red → Green → Purple) |
| Web | every 5 s | a permanent area, slows you to 50 % |

## Tips and connections

- Do not stand in webs – Spider king is fast and a slowed player cannot get away from it.
- Clear the spiderlings continuously, or they will overwhelm you; Green spiderlings heal other
  spiderlings, but not the boss.
- Spiderlings count toward clearing the room, but they die on their own when the boss dies.
- Boss rules and rewards: [Bosses](/docs/enemies/bosses).

## Known inconsistencies

- Spider king has no spiderling limit (other bosses with helpers have one) – with passive play the
  fight can get out of control.
- Webs never disappear on their own and pile up (12 per minute of fighting).

## Ideas for expansion

- An enrage phase – for example a jump at the player (like Stilt spider) or barricading the room with webs.
- A spiderling limit and a duration for the webs.
