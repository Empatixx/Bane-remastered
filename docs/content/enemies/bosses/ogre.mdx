A huge, slow giant from the sewers with the most HP of all bosses. It attacks with waves of rocks up
close and sends rats at you from a distance.

> "A temple worker who dug the drainage. The first one the wound took."

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 130 base, 146–208 on floors 1–5 (the most of all bosses) |
| Contact damage | 2 (3 on floors 4–5) |
| Speed | 9 % of the player (13 % when enraged) |
| Size | giant |
| Where | any floor, only the Sewer theme |
| First attack | after 2.4 s |
| Pause between attacks | 3 s (1.8 s when enraged) |
| Helpers | Rat (at most 6) |

## How it works

The ogre walks slowly after you, and after a pause it winds up and attacks – all of its attacks are
instant. Every wind-up has its own sound, so you can recognize the attack by ear too. When you are
close (within about 6 tiles), it prefers Jump and Slam; at a distance it prefers Throw. When there are
6 or more helpers in the room, it no longer summons rats.

| Attack | Chance when close | Chance when far | With 6+ rats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jump | high | low | unchanged |
| Slam | high | low | unchanged |
| Throw | low | high | never |
| Roar | medium | medium | never |

## Contents

### Jump – stomp

- A 0.5 s wind-up comes with a **snort** and a swing (the ogre pushes off), then **3 concentric rings of 16 rocks each** – a dense wall of 48 rocks. Each rock
  deals 2 damage and flies at 75 % of your speed.

### Slam – club strike

- A 0.45 s wind-up comes with a rising **whoosh of the club**, then from the spot where the club landed
  a **fan of 2 × 11 rocks** shoots at you.

### Throw – throwing rats

- A 0.4 s wind-up comes with a short **grunt**, then it releases **2 rats** next to itself toward you.

### Roar – roar

- A 0.3 s wind-up is a real **roar**, then **3 rats** appear around it, spread out in a fan.

The rats have the base Rat stats (12 HP, 2 damage, 45 % of your speed) and drop nothing. A rat is not
summoned into a wall.

### Enrage (from half HP)

The ogre lets out a long, deep **roar**, turns red, speeds up by 35 % and the pause between attacks
drops to 1.8 s. The attacks themselves do not change.

## Tips and connections

- Keep your distance – from afar only the rats threaten you, up close it is three rings of rocks.
- Kill the rats continuously so that the ogre does not fill the room.
- The rocks are the same as with the [Stone golem](/docs/enemies/bosses/golem); the rats are described in
  [Basic enemies](/docs/enemies/critters).

## Known inconsistencies

- Throw and Roar do almost the same thing (2 vs. 3 rats).
- The Jump attack is not actually a jump – the ogre stomps in place.
- Enrage does not change the attacks, only the pace (other bosses add projectiles when enraged).

## Ideas for expansion

- Jump as a real jump at the player, and in the enrage a fourth ring or more rats.
- Throw could really throw the rats in an arc (with a landing like Stilt spider) or throw a puddle of mud.
