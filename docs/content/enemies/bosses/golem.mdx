A huge stone golem that moves slowly but controls the whole room: with a shockwave of rocks, boulders,
a rain of rocks from the sky and a laser that follows you. Mini golems are also born from the falling
rocks.

> "A statue of a guardian from the temple. Brought to life, without orders, it guards out of habit."

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 110 base, 123–176 on floors 1–5 |
| Contact damage | 2 (3 on floors 4–5) |
| Speed | 9 % of the player (12 % when enraged) |
| Size | giant |
| Where | any floor, only the Stone theme |
| First attack | after 2 s |
| Pause between attacks | 2.2 s (1.3 s when enraged) |
| Helpers | Mini golem (at most 4) |

## How it works

The golem walks slowly after you, and after a pause it stops, winds up and attacks. When you are close
(within about 5 tiles), it picks Slam more often; at a distance it prefers Throw and Laser.

| Attack | Chance when close | Chance when far |
|---|---|---|
| Slam | high | low |
| Throw | low | high |
| Rain | medium | medium |
| Laser | low | high |

## Contents

### Slam – shockwave

- A 0.45 s wind-up comes with a **grinding of stone** and rumbling, then the blast: **16 rocks** in a circle around the golem (75 % of your speed, 2 damage).
- **When enraged**, a second ring follows after 0.35 s and fills the gaps of the first.

### Throw – boulder

- A 0.45 s wind-up comes with a deep **growl** and a swing, then it throws a **large boulder** straight at you (61 % of your speed, 2 damage).

### Rain – rain of rocks

- A 0.4 s wind-up announces it with **rumbling and crumbling rock**, then for 2.8 s about **23 rocks** fall from the sky (one every 0.12 s).
- Before each rock, a **shadow** shows on the ground for 0.7 s. The rock hits an area of about 2 × 2 tiles –
  2 damage. Dodge protects you. Every landing can be heard.
- The first 3 rocks aim straight at you, so that you have to move; the others fall anywhere within ~5 tiles.
- Each landing has a **16 % chance** to spawn a Mini golem (if there are not too many yet and the spot is free).

### Laser – rotating beam

- 1 s of charging – the golem aims its eye at you and a rising **wail** is heard.
- For 1.9 s a blue beam shines across the whole room (only a wall stops it). It slowly turns after you,
  faster when enraged. While it shines, it **hums** loudly. A hit = 2 damage (because of the invulnerability after a hit, at most once per second).

### Mini golem

A small stone chaser: 6 HP, 1 damage, 47 % of your speed. It drops nothing and dies together with the golem.

### Enrage (from half HP)

With a deep stone **roar** the golem turns red and speeds up by 35 %, the pause between attacks drops to 1.3 s, Slam has two rings and the laser
turns faster.

## Tips and connections

- When close, expect Slam – after the wind-up jump away or stand in a gap between the rocks.
- The laser turns slowly – circle the golem in a steady direction and the beam will not catch you.
- During Rain, watch the shadows, not the golem.
- Dodge protects against rock landings and the laser ([Player](/docs/player)).
- [Sewer ogre](/docs/enemies/bosses/ogre) uses the same rocks.

## Known inconsistencies

- The laser has no warning line during charging (Hellhound has one before its beams) – only an animation.
- The limit of 4 Mini golems is counted together with all helpers in the room.
- An older description of the game listed a completely different golem (healing, armor, dash) – today's golem is a new boss.
