---
title: Hellhound
description: A three-headed hound of hell with a lunge, flame beams, a fiery howl and magma that spreads from the walls.
---

A three-headed dog from the depths of lava. Unlike the other bosses, it is **fast** and actively runs
you down. Throughout the whole fight, glowing magma also spreads from the walls of the room and
gradually pushes you toward the middle.

> "The guardian of the bottom. ... It does not guard the wound from you – it guards you from what is under it."

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 110 base, 123–176 on floors 1–5 |
| Contact damage | 2 (3 on floors 4–5) |
| Speed | 59 % of the player (74 % when enraged) |
| Size | giant |
| Where | any floor, only the Lava theme |
| First attack | after 2.2 s |
| Pause between attacks | 2.4 s (1.3 s when enraged) |
| Helpers | none – magma instead |

## How it works

Between attacks the hound chases you. When you are close (within about 5.5 tiles), it prefers flame
beams; at a distance it prefers the lunge.

| Attack | Chance when close | Chance when far |
|---|---|---|
| Lunge | medium | high |
| Breath | high | medium |
| Howl | low | low |

## Contents

### Lunge

- A short wind-up (about 0.3 s) with a deep **growl**, then it charges straight at you at huge speed – about **3.8× faster than
  you** (almost 5× when enraged) – for up to 1.4 s. Touch = 2 damage.
- **Hitting a wall**: when it hits a wall, it is **stunned for 1.7 s** – that is the best time to attack.
- If it does not hit anything, it stops after 1.4 s.

### Breath – flame beams

- A 0.45 s wind-up: the hound audibly **inhales** and **dotted orange lines** lead from the heads,
  showing where the beams will go.
- For 1.8 s, 2 beams (3 when enraged) burn across the whole room with a **roar of flames**. They slowly
  turn after you (faster when enraged). A hit = 1 damage (at most once per second).

### Howl – fiery howl

- A 0.3 s wind-up is the **howl**, then a ring of **14 hot embers** (20 when enraged) flies out of the
  middle head. The embers are slow (47 % of your speed), deal 1 damage and go out after 3 s.

### Magma (constant)

- 3 s after the fight starts, magma begins to spread from the walls – up to a depth of **3 tiles from the walls**, from the wall inward.
- Every 0.45 s (0.28 s when enraged) it floods another tile, over and over.
- Fresh magma glows up for 1 s (it is harmless), then it burns and disappears after 16 s (it cools for the last 1.2 s).
- When you stand on a glowing tile, you take **2 damage straight to HP – armor does not protect you**. Dodge does.
- The edges of the arena gradually become unusable and the fight moves to the middle.

### Enrage (from half HP)

The hound growls and **howls**, turns red, speeds up by 25 %, the pause between attacks drops to 1.3 s,
it has 3 beams instead of 2 and turns them faster, howls out 20 embers and the magma spreads faster.

## Tips and connections

- Bait the lunge so that the hound hits a wall – the stun is your window for damage. But make sure you
  are not standing in magma by the wall.
- Before the beams, watch the dotted lines and step aside.
- Dodge protects from the beams and the magma ([Player](/docs/player)).
- The Lava theme also has its own lava surfaces that burn independently of the boss – see [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).

## Known inconsistencies

- The lunge has no warning line for its direction, only a short animation – at its speed it is the
  hardest attack in the game to read.
- The story places it on floor 4, but in the game it can appear on any floor with the Lava theme.
- According to the story it is a guardian that does not want to kill – this does not show in the game in any way.

## Ideas for expansion

- A warning line before the lunge.
- Bring in the story: in the enrage it "offers" the player a retreat or leaves the area around the ladder free.
