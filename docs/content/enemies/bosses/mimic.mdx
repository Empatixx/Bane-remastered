title: Mimic
description: A living chest that jumps, bites, spits slime, scatters fake coins and closes into a bulletproof box.

A chest with a mouth full of teeth. Mimic jumps around the room, bites, spits slimes and scatters fake
coins around you that snap shut when you step on them. Now and then it closes up and is completely
invulnerable for a while.

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 115 base, 129–184 on floors 1–5 |
| Contact damage | 2 (3 on floors 4–5) |
| Speed | 32 % of the player (42 % when enraged) |
| Size | large |
| Where | any floor, only the Temple theme |
| First attack | after 2.2 s |
| Pause between attacks | 2.6 s (1.5 s when enraged) |
| Helpers | Saliva slime (at most 4) |

## How it works

Mimic walks after you and picks an attack after a pause. When you are close (within about 5 tiles), it
bites; at a distance it prefers to jump. When there are 4 or more helpers in the room, it no longer
spits, and when 8 or more lures are lying around, it no longer scatters them.

| Attack | Chance when close | Chance when far | When not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hop | medium | high | – |
| Bite | high | very low | – |
| Spit | medium | medium | 4+ helpers |
| Lure | medium | medium | 8+ lures |
| Rest | low | low | – |

## Contents

### Hop – jump

- A 0.2 s wind-up comes with the **creaking of a lid**, then it jumps straight at you (about 2× your speed) for 0.45 s.
- On landing, a ring of **14 coins** flies out of it (84 % of your speed, 2 damage, 1.8 s).

### Bite

- A wind-up of about 0.3 s – a creak and the **snap of teeth** – then a short fast lunge.
- At the end, a fan of **7 teeth** shoots from the mouth at you – **the fastest enemy projectile in the game**
  (118 % of your speed), 2 damage.

### Spit – spitting slime

- A wind-up of about 0.3 s comes with a **bubbling gurgle**, then it spits out a **Saliva slime** (and a burst of spit that does no damage).
- Saliva slime has 5 HP, jumps at you, and after its first death it forms a puddle for 10 s and rises with full HP (see
  [Slimes](/docs/enemies/slimes#saliva-slime--puddle-and-revival)).

### Lure

- A wind-up of about 0.4 s comes with the **jingle of coins**, then it places **5 lures** around you (2 to 4 tiles from you) – gold coins on the ground.
- A lure lasts 9 s. When you get close to it (about half a tile), it snaps shut and deals **2 damage**
  – **even if you are dodging**.

### Rest – box

- A 0.3 s wind-up ends with the **slam of the lid and the click of a lock**, then it **closes for 2.2 s**: it stands in place and is **immune to any damage**
  (projectiles, explosions, poison).
- It is the only defensive attack among the bosses – use it to dodge the remaining projectiles and to kill slimes.

### Enrage (from half HP)

Mimic rattles and growls, turns red, speeds up by 30 % (the jumps are faster too) and the pause between attacks drops to 1.5 s. The attacks
themselves do not change.

## Tips and connections

- Do not pick up coins from the ground during the fight – they may be lures. Real coins are described in
  [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- You cannot outrun the teeth from Bite, you have to dodge to the side.
- When it closes up, do not shoot at it – kill the Saliva slimes.
- After it is defeated, all lures and Saliva slimes disappear, including the puddles.

## Known inconsistencies

- Lures hurt even during a dodge, while the other area attacks of bosses are avoided by a dodge.
- It is the only boss without a story.
- Enrage does not change the attacks, only the pace.
