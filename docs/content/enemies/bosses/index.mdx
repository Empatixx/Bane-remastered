Every floor ends with a boss room. When you enter it, the doors close, the boss materializes in the
middle, its name and HP bar appear at the top, and the boss music starts. Bane has **seven bosses** –
each of the seven themes has exactly one of its own. The themes are shuffled randomly in every run, so
any boss can be waiting on any floor.

## How it works

### Common rules

- **Grows with the floor** just like regular enemies: **+12 % HP and +8 % contact damage per floor**.
  Its attacks and helpers do not change. The Horde and Bane challenges make it stronger too (HP × 1.6,
  damage × 1.3).
- **Materializing** takes 1.5 s, during which the boss does not deal damage and cannot be hit.
- **Contact damage** is 2 for all bosses on floors 1–3 and 3 on floors 4–5.
- Bosses cannot be pushed by a crowd and do not keep their distance.
- **Helpers** summoned by a boss are active immediately, have base stats, drop nothing and do not count
  toward kill statistics – but the room does not open while they are alive.
- **Enrage** (Stone golem, Argus, Sewer ogre, Hellhound, Mimic): from half HP the boss turns red, speeds
  up, shortens the pauses between attacks and makes some attacks stronger. Spider king and King slime
  have no enrage.
- It picks attacks randomly based on distance, but **never the same attack twice in a row**. A wind-up
  is visible before every attack. See [Behavior and movement](/docs/enemies/ai#how-bosses-decide).
- **Every attack has its own sound**, which for attacks with a wind-up plays right at its start – the
  sound tells you what is coming even when you are looking elsewhere. Entering the enrage is announced
  by a roar or a screech. You hear a boss from farther away than regular enemies (see
  [Attack sounds](/docs/enemies#attack-sounds)).

### After a boss is defeated

1. All helpers die and the webs, beams, falling rocks, magma and lures disappear.
2. The HP bar disappears, the normal music returns and the screen shakes.
3. An **artefact chest** appears in the middle of the room.
4. On floors 1–4 a **ladder** to the next floor appears; on floor 5 **victory** comes.
5. The defeat counts toward the "defeat boss X" missions.

## Tips and connections

- The enrage starts at half HP – save strong items for the second half of the fight.
- Keep the helpers under control; some bosses summon more, but only until there are too many.
- The boss room, themes and ladder are described in [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon), the artefact chest in
  [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- The "Boss" mission and the Bane challenge ("defeat the last boss") are in [Progression](/docs/progression).

## Known inconsistencies

- **The final boss is missing** – victory comes after defeating any boss on floor 5.
- The story assigns bosses to floors (1: Spider king, 2: King slime / Ogre, 3: Golem / Argus,
  4: Hellhound), but in the game the boss depends only on the theme, and the themes are random per floor.
- King slime is the only boss that drops nothing and does not count toward the drop guarantee; the
  others do.
