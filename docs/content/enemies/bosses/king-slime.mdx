King slime is a giant version of the regular [Slime](/docs/enemies/slimes). It slowly crawls after you
and from the very first second pours out purple projectiles in two patterns at once – a classic bullet
hell. It is the boss of the **Moss** theme and can wait on whichever floor gets the Moss theme.

> "What was left of the subjects who dissolved."

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 90 base, 101–144 on floors 1–5 |
| Contact damage | 2 (3 on floors 4–5) |
| Speed | 10 % of the player |
| Size | giant |
| Where | any floor, only the Moss theme |
| Enrage | no |
| Drop | none |

## How it works

Both kinds of fire use the purple projectile: small, 79 % of your speed, 1 damage, flies until it hits a wall.

### Circular volley

Every **2 seconds** it fires 25 projectiles evenly in a circle. It fires the first volley right after
materializing. Every volley is announced by a deep **squelch**.

### Armageddon – rotating spiral

Roughly 7 projectiles per second, each turned a bit further – this makes a continuous spiral that
turns all the way around in about 3.8 s. Together with the circular volleys it creates a dense net of
projectiles. The spiral comes with a quiet **bubbling** – it plays only on every third projectile, so
it is not unbearable.

## Contents

| Attack | How often | Projectiles | Damage |
|---|---|--:|--:|
| Circular volley | every 2 s | 25 | 1 per projectile |
| Spiral | ~7 times per second | 1 | 1 |
| Touch | – | – | 2 |

In a minute of fighting it fires about 1,150 projectiles.

## Tips and connections

- Unlike the regular Slime, it does not keep its distance – it goes straight at you, but very slowly.
- The spiral is regular – find a gap and move with it.
- The Midas artefact turns its projectiles into coins ([Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items)).
- Projectiles slow down in webs ([Dungeon](/docs/dungeon)).

## Known inconsistencies

- It is the only boss that drops nothing and does not count toward the drop guarantee.
- The spiral is perfectly regular; an older description of the game promised that it would gradually spread out.
- After its death the room opens only after all of its projectiles land, which can take a while.

## Ideas for expansion

- Enrage and wind-ups, so that it does not attack at full power from the first second.
- Split into smaller slimes as it loses HP.
