In Bane, several enemies wait for you in every combat room, and a boss waits at the end of every
floor. The game has **23 kinds** in total: 13 regular enemies, 3 helpers that bosses summon, and 7
bosses. Most enemies run straight at you and hurt you on touch. Some shoot, jump or leave poison
behind.

## How it works

### The life of an enemy

1. **Appearing** – when you enter a combat room, the doors close and enemies start to materialize at
   different spots in the room. See [Spawning and enemy strength](/docs/enemies/spawning).
2. **Materializing** – the enemy slowly fades in for 2 seconds (a boss for 1.5 s). During that time it
   does not move, does not hurt you and cannot be hit.
3. **Fighting** – then it starts to chase you and goes around obstacles. See [Behavior and movement](/docs/enemies/ai).
4. **Getting hit** – a hit enemy briefly turns red (0.3 s).
5. **Death** – the death animation plays. Some enemies do something when they die: they fire
   projectiles, release a poison cloud or heal their relatives.
6. **Reward** – after death a coin or an ammo box may drop (see
   [Drops](/docs/enemies/spawning#drops)).

### Contact damage

Enemies hurt you when you touch them – but they do not push you away physically, and you can walk
through them. A hit takes your armor first, then your health, and then you get **1 second of
invulnerability**. An enemy that is still materializing, dying or frozen does not deal damage. When you
are invisible (the Phantom artefact), enemies do not hurt you on touch at all.

### Attack sounds

Enemies and bosses make a sound **only when they attack**: on the wind-up, a shot, a jump, when
summoning helpers or when a boss becomes enraged. Walking, touching and getting hit make no sound. If
an attack has a wind-up, the sound plays at its very start – so you often hear an attack before you
see it.

- The volume depends on how far the enemy is **from you** (not from the center of the screen). It is
  at full volume up to about 3 tiles, then fades, and from about 14 tiles you no longer hear it. You
  hear bosses from farther away – full volume up to about 6 tiles, and they go silent only around 24.
- The sound is panned slightly left or right depending on which side the enemy is on.
- Every playback has a slightly different pitch, and the same sound plays at most three times at once,
  so a crowd of slimes does not sound like a machine gun.
- The volume of these sounds is controlled by the effects volume setting.

You can find exactly what each enemy sounds like on its page.

### Speed

Enemy speed in the tables is given as a percentage of your speed (100 % = the player). In general, the
bigger the enemy, the slower it is.

## Tips and connections

- **Dodge** protects you from jump landings, falling rocks, beams and magma. See [Player](/docs/player).
- **Ice ammo** slows an enemy to half speed, and after 4 hits it freezes it – a frozen enemy stands
  still, does not attack and does not deal damage. See [Guns](/docs/guns).
- **Red slime** reacts to the number of hits, not to damage – rapid-fire guns make it fire a lot of
  projectiles when it dies.
- **Phantom** (invisibility) makes regular enemies stand still and stop shooting. **Time slow** slows
  down all enemies and their projectiles, but not you. **Midas** gives bonus coins for kills.
  See [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- **Traps and poison puddles** in the [dungeon](/docs/dungeon) hurt enemies too.
- Missions like "kill N rats / bats / slimes / ghosts / snakes" and "defeat boss X", plus the Horde and
  Bane challenges (stronger enemies), are in [Progression](/docs/progression).

## Known inconsistencies

- Bosses (except King slime) count toward the guaranteed drop system as a normal kill, while King slime
  does not – boss behavior is inconsistent here.
- Red spiderling and Purple spiderling have no special ability; in an older version of the game, Red
  spiderling healed itself when hit and Purple spiderling teleported behind the player.
- Older descriptions of the game claim that enemies do not get stronger with the floor – in fact,
  regular enemies and bosses both gain 12 % HP and 8 % damage per floor.

## Ideas for expansion

- **Elite enemies** with random traits (fortified, furious, regenerating), with a chance that grows
  with the floor.
- **The final boss "The Heart"** on floor 5 (Temple theme), following the game's story – today floor 5
  has an ordinary boss from the pool.
- **Themed enemies**: of the 13 regular enemies, 5 are tied to the Spider theme, and the other themes
  have no enemies of their own. Ideas include an ice slime that leaves ice patches, a lava bat with a
  fiery trail, and so on.
