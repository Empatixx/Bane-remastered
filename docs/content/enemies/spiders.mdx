title: Spiders

Five spider enemies belong to the **Spider** theme. The floor themes are shuffled randomly in every
run, so the spider floor can come right at the start, but just as well only on floor 5 – and the
spiders come with it. Their boss is [Spider king](/docs/enemies/bosses/spider-king), who hatches
spiderlings during the fight.

## Stats

HP on floor 1 (the base value in brackets). Like all regular enemies they gain 12 % HP and 8 %
damage per floor – see [Spawning](/docs/enemies/spawning#strength-by-floor).

| | Red spiderling | Green spiderling | Purple spiderling | Bloated spider | Stilt spider |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| HP | 3 (3) | 6 (5) | 3 (3) | 6 (5) | 8 (7) |
| Damage | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 (touch and landing) |
| Speed | 46 % | 46 % | 55 % | 41 % | 15 % + jumps |
| Size | small | small | small | medium | medium |
| Ability | none | heals spiderlings on death | none | poison cloud on death | jump at the player |

## How it works

### Red spiderling

A small fast chaser with 3 HP and no ability. Spider king hatches it first.

### Green spiderling – healer

When it dies, it heals all living spiderlings (red, green and purple) within about 1.5 tiles by
**+1 HP** (up to the maximum) and a green number shows above them. It does not heal Bloated or Stilt
spiders. The healing is accompanied by a quiet ringing **melody**.

### Purple spiderling

The fastest spider, but it deals only 1 damage. No ability.

### Bloated spider – poison cloud

On death it releases a **poison cloud** for 1.4 s:

- the burst is accompanied by the **hiss** of escaping gas,
- it hurts you for 1 while you are inside,
- it **also hurts enemies** in the cloud (1 per second) – you can use it against other spiders.

### Stilt spider – jumper

It walks slowly on its own, but attacks with a jump:

1. **Creeping** – it walks after you. It can jump for the first time after 1.4 s, then always after 2.4 s, and only when you are
   at a distance of about 2.5–6.5 tiles.
2. **Crouching** – for 0.5 s it crouches in place and an accelerating **clicking chatter** sounds. That is your warning.
3. **Jump** – for 0.5 s it flies to the spot where you were at the end of the crouch. It does not hurt you by touch during the flight.
4. **Landing** – a dull **thud**. If you are closer than about 1.75 tiles to the landing spot and do not dodge,
   you take damage.
5. **Rest** – it stands for 0.8 s, then creeping again.

## Tips and connections

- Kill Green spiderlings last, or conversely quickly one after another, so the others cannot heal in time.
- Kill Bloated spider in the middle of a group – its cloud hurts the others too.
- Against Stilt spider it is enough to change direction after the crouch – it lands where you were.
- The poison cloud and the snake's trail ([Snake](/docs/enemies/critters#snake--poison-trail)) work the same as
  the poison barrels in the [dungeon](/docs/dungeon).
- Spider king hatches Red → Green → Purple spiderlings over and over.
- Dodge protects you from the landing of the jump ([Player](/docs/player)).

## Known inconsistencies

- Stilt spider has no shadow or circle on the ground showing where it will land – Stone golem has one for the falling rocks.
- The healing range of Green spiderling cannot be seen on the ground, so it is hard to judge.
- Red spiderling and Purple spiderling have no ability of their own; in an older version of the game Red
  healed itself when hit and Purple teleported behind the player.

## Ideas for expansion

- Give Purple spiderling its teleport behind the player back, so that it has an identity of its own.
- A shadow at the landing spot of Stilt spider.
