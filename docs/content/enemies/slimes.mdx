Slimes crawl very slowly, but they shoot. Slime and Red slime are regular enemies on all floors (Red slime in all themes, Slime in all but Ice and Lava,
where it is replaced by the [Snow slime and Magma slime](/docs/enemies/ice-lava)); Saliva slime is a helper of the boss [Mimic](/docs/enemies/bosses/mimic). Their big
brother is the boss [King slime](/docs/enemies/bosses/king-slime).

## Stats

| | Slime | Red slime | Saliva slime |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Kind | regular | regular | Mimic's helper |
| HP | 9 | 9 | 5 (+ one revival) |
| Contact damage | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Speed | 7 % | 7 % | 34 %, only while jumping |
| Floors | all | all | only in the Mimic fight |
| Ability | volley of 5 projectiles, keeps its distance | projectiles on death | jumps, rises from a puddle |

The HP of regular slimes grows with the floor (see [Spawning](/docs/enemies/spawning#strength-by-floor)).

## How it works

### Slime – volley and distance

- Every **2 seconds** it fires 5 green projectiles evenly in a circle. It fires the first volley right after materializing.
- Every volley is accompanied by a squelchy **splat**.
- Green projectile: small, 79 % of your speed, 1 damage, flies until it hits a wall.
- The only regular enemy that **keeps its distance**: when you are closer than about 4.5 tiles, it
  stops, and when you are closer than 3 tiles, it backs off (see [Behavior and movement](/docs/enemies/ai)).

### Red slime – retaliation for hits

- It counts how many times you hit it with a projectile or a helper.
- On death it fires **twice as many red projectiles as the hits it took** – half in a spiral, half in a
  regular circle. Killing it with one strong blow = 2 projectiles, "nibbling" it with a shotgun or
  submachine gun = dozens of projectiles.
- The burst on death is accompanied by a loud **smack** – when you hear it, expect a shower of projectiles.
- Otherwise it just slowly crawls after you (it does not keep its distance).
- The red projectile has the same properties as the green one.

### Saliva slime – puddle and revival

- Mimic spits it out with the Spit attack (only when there are fewer than 4 helpers in the room). It is
  active at once, without materializing, and drops nothing.
- It moves by jumps: it stands for a while, then jumps straight at you (about 0.4 s), then rests for roughly 0.5–0.8 s.
- **First death**: instead of dying, it melts into a puddle. For 10 s it is a puddle – it cannot be hit and does not attack. At the end
  it merges again and rises with full HP.
- **The second death** is final.
- When Mimic dies, all Saliva slimes die too, including the puddles.

## Tips and connections

- Against Red slime use your strongest single shot, not a rapid-fire gun ([Guns](/docs/guns)).
- You have to go after Slime – it will not come to you on its own.
- Slime projectiles slow down in webs ([Dungeon](/docs/dungeon)).
- The mission "kill N slimes" counts only the ordinary Slime ([Progression](/docs/progression)).

## Known inconsistencies

- Red slime does not show in any way how many hits it has collected – you do not know how many projectiles will fly out of it.
- The Slime volley is always rotated the same way.
