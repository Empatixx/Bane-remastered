title: Enemy projectiles
description: All enemy projectiles and area attacks – how fast they fly, how much damage they deal and who uses them.
---

Besides contact, enemies hurt you with projectiles and area attacks. Regular shooters (slimes, Eye
watcher) have weak projectiles that deal 1 damage, while bosses have stone and sharp projectiles that
deal 2.

## How it works

- A projectile flies straight at a constant speed until it hits a wall, you or a solid object. Then
  an impact animation plays.
- Some projectiles have a limited flight time – after that they disappear on their own.
- Enemy projectiles **fly through other enemies**.
- They slow down in webs.
- Every projectile glows in its own color, so it is visible even in the dark.
- A hit takes your armor first, then HP, just like contact, and gives you 1 s of invulnerability.
- Projectile damage does not grow with the floor, only with the Horde and Bane challenges.

Speed is given as a percentage of your speed.

### Other attacks

| Attack | Damage | How it works | Who |
|---|--:|---|---|
| Web | 0 | shoots out quickly, slows down and stays on the ground; slows you to 50 % | Spider king |
| Falling rock | 2 | a shadow shows on the ground for 0.7 s, then it lands in an area of about 2 × 2 tiles | Stone golem (Rain) |
| Laser | 2 | a rotating beam across the whole room (up to the wall) | Stone golem (Laser) |
| Flame beam | 1 | 2–3 rotating beams across the whole room | Hellhound (Breath) |
| Magma | 2, **ignores armor** | glowing tiles by the walls, each lasts 16 s | Hellhound |
| Lure | 2 | a fake coin on the ground that snaps shut when you step on it; lasts 9 s | Mimic (Lure) |
| Poison cloud | 1 | 1.4 s, also hurts enemies | Bloated spider (on death) |
| Poison trail | 1 | lasts 5 s, hurts only you | Snake |
| Jump landing | like a spider | hits the area around the landing spot (about 1.75 tiles) | Stilt spider |

Thanks to the 1 s of invulnerability after a hit, beams and trails can realistically hurt you at most
once per second.

## Tips and connections

- The **Midas** artefact turns enemy projectiles into coins – see [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- **Dodge** protects you from landings, beams and magma (but not from Mimic's lures). See [Player](/docs/player).
- Eye watcher's homing projectile turns smoothly – a sharp dodge to the side shakes it off.
- Mimic's teeth are the only enemy projectile faster than you – you cannot outrun them, you have to dodge to the side.
- Webs on the ground in the [dungeon](/docs/dungeon) also slow down enemy projectiles.

## Known inconsistencies

- Spider king's webs never disappear on their own – during a long fight they pile up until the boss dies.
- The green, red and purple projectiles look and fly the same – you cannot tell by looking which one is more dangerous.

## Ideas for expansion

- Tell the small projectiles apart by size or speed according to how dangerous they are.
- New projectile patterns: a wave, a boomerang, a projectile that splits after a moment.
- More homing projectiles – today only Eye watcher has them.
