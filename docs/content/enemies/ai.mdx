---
title: Behavior and movement
description: How enemies find you, how they go around obstacles and how bosses decide what to do.
---

Enemies in Bane have a single goal – you. Right after they materialize, they know where you are and
take the shortest path to you around walls and objects. Bosses also alternate attacks in a fixed
pattern with a telegraph and an enrage phase.

## How it works

### Whom enemies follow

Always only the player – never your pet. If you are invisible (the **Phantom** artefact):

- regular enemies stand still,
- Eye watcher stops shooting and homing projectiles fly straight,
- bosses behave as if you were far away and pick ranged attacks.

Area attacks (falling rocks, jump landings, beams) still hit you even when you are invisible.

### Pathfinding

Enemies "sense" the distance to you within a radius of about 18 tiles (across a whole room). The path
map is calculated separately for each enemy size: a large enemy (rat, golem, stilt spider) is sent only
through gaps it can really fit through, while a small one can go through a narrow crack. If an enemy
sees your path as clear for the width of its body, it walks straight to you; otherwise it walks
straight to the farthest point of its path that it can reach in a straight line, so it turns smoothly
and does not zigzag between tiles. The map is recalculated whenever you move (at most 10 times per
second), so you cannot shake an enemy off by hiding around a corner. An enemy whose only path is
narrower than itself tries to squeeze through the path made for smaller enemies.

### Steering and spacing in a crowd

Exactly which way an enemy goes at a given moment is decided by its steering (context steering): it
scores each of 16 directions around itself by how well it leads to the target, and subtracts points
from directions where a wall or another enemy is close ahead (it looks ahead about a third of a
second of walking). It picks the best direction, and when a wall is right in front of it, it slows
down instead of running into it. It steps aside for another enemy only when that enemy stands right in
its way and is not going the same direction; behind one that goes the same way, it simply follows. It
does not back off or jump away. It changes direction and speed smoothly over a fraction of a second,
so it turns in an arc and does not twitch.

### Spots around the player

So that enemies do not push each other for one spot at your side, each one has its own spot around you.
There are 16 spots in a circle around the player, each so close that an enemy standing on it touches
you (and hurts you). An enemy that comes within about 3.5 tiles takes the nearest free spot on the
side it is coming from, where its body fits next to the others. It walks there and stands until you
get away from it (more than about 5.5 tiles, for example when you run away). An enemy that gets no
spot because it is crowded around you slowly circles you about two tiles away and waits for one to
free up. About 4–6 enemies fit around you, depending on their size. Shooting enemies do not take
spots; they keep their own distance.

Enemies do not collide with each other physically (they pass through one another); they keep their
distance only through their steering and the spots around you. They collide normally with walls,
objects and furniture.

### Getting stuck

If an enemy still gets stuck (for example in a crowd), it avoids the direction it was pushing for 0.6 s
and tries to go around the obstacle from the side. If it gets stuck again, it tries the other side.

### Facing

Enemies turn to face you. The exception is those that are in the middle of a dash – they keep the
direction of the attack.

### Time slow

The time slow artefact slows down all enemies, their attacks and their projectiles, but not you.

### States that change behavior

| State | When it happens | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Materializing | on appearing | invisible → visible, cannot be hit, does nothing |
| Rooted | attack wind-up, spitting a web | stands still, but the attack keeps running |
| Frozen | 4 hits with ice ammo | stands still, does not attack, does not deal damage |
| Chilled | a hit with ice ammo | half speed |
| Dash | boss attacks, slime jump | flies in a fixed direction |
| Closed | Mimic in the Rest attack | immune to damage |
| Hit | every hit | red outline for 0.3 s |
| Poisoned | poison puddle | green outline, 1 damage per second |

### How bosses decide

Stone golem, Argus, Sewer ogre, Hellhound and Mimic share the same pattern:

1. **Movement** – the boss follows you (Argus only hovers) and waits for the pause between attacks to end.
2. **Choosing an attack** – random, but influenced by your distance and the number of helpers. **It never
   uses the same attack twice in a row.**
3. **Wind-up** – the boss stops and plays an animation that tells you what is coming.
4. **Attack** – a shot, a beam, a rock rain, a dash...
5. **Recovery** – a short pause, then back to movement.
6. **Enrage** – when the boss drops to half HP, it turns red, speeds up, shortens its pauses and makes
   some attacks stronger.

Spider king and King slime are simpler: their abilities run in a loop on timers, with no wind-ups and
no enrage.

## Tips and connections

- Slime is the only regular enemy that runs away from you – you have to go after it.
- Invisibility (Phantom) is the best way to get rid of crowd pressure, but it does not protect you from the bosses' area attacks.
- Ice ammo and freezing take an enemy out of the fight completely, including its contact damage. See [Guns](/docs/guns).
- Walls and solid objects in the [dungeon](/docs/dungeon) are obstacles that enemies have to go around.

## Known inconsistencies

- Enemies have no perception range – they see you right after materializing, even through walls.
- Outside a radius of about 18 tiles, enemies walk straight at you, even if a wall is in their way.
- An enemy that fits nowhere (for example a giant stilt spider in a narrow corridor) can still get stuck.
- Boss attacks depend on the place where the boss appeared, not on the run seed – so with the same seed
  they may not repeat the same way.

## Ideas for expansion

- A perception range and "waking up" enemies when they see you.
- Group behavior – surrounding, flanking – instead of everyone taking the same path.
- More enemies that keep their distance or circle (Eye watcher, Demon eye).
- Convert Spider king and King slime to the same pattern with wind-ups and enrage as the other bosses.
