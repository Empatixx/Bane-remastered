Two enemies that join the game from **floor 3** in any theme. They widen the pool of regular enemies
from 6 to 8 kinds. Thematically they belong to the boss [Argus](/docs/enemies/bosses/argus) – according
to the story, the "Scientist's Eye".

## Stats

| | Demon eye | Eye watcher |
|---|--:|--:|
| HP (floor 3 / 4 / 5) | 15 / 16 / 18 | 5 / 6 / 6 |
| Contact damage | 4 | 1 |
| Speed | 64 % of the player | stationary |
| Size | medium | medium |
| Ability | none | homing projectile |

## How it works

### Demon eye

A fast, hard-hitting chaser with no special ability. With a speed of 64 % of the player and damage of 4
on the late floors, it is the most dangerous melee enemy in the whole game.

### Eye watcher – turret

- It stands in place (only a crowd can push it a little).
- Every **3 seconds** it takes a breath (a short attack animation, about 0.4 s, with a rising **buzz** –
  that is your warning) and with a short **pop** fires one **homing projectile**.
- The projectile: large, glows yellow, flies at 62 % of your speed, deals 1 damage and disappears after 5 s.
- The projectile turns after you smoothly, not sharply – a sudden dodge to the side or a dodge roll shakes it off.
- When you are invisible (Phantom), it does not shoot and projectiles already fired fly straight.

## Tips and connections

- Eye watcher has only 4–6 HP – kill it first, before it floods the room with homing projectiles.
- Running away from Demon eye does not help; keep it at a distance with a gun that deals big damage, or with freezing.
- More about the homing projectile is in [Enemy projectiles](/docs/enemies/projectiles).
- From floor 3 these two enemies make up a quarter of the regular pool – see [Spawning](/docs/enemies/spawning).
- Invisibility and dodge are described in [Player](/docs/player).

## Known inconsistencies

- Eye watcher can appear in the middle of the room, although as a turret it would make more sense by a wall.
