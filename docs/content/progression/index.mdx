Bane is played in **runs**: you descend from the lab through a portal into the dungeon, fight through five floors
(or die) and return to the lab. Between runs you spend what you earned on permanent
upgrades for your character and weapons. This section describes the whole loop, the currencies, missions, challenges, the lab,
the menus and the story.

## How it works

### The game loop

1. **Loading** — an animated title and a spinning icon.
2. **Main menu** — Play / Settings / Exit. In the background is a live crypt with rats and bats running around.
3. **Lab (lobby)** — you walk freely around the room and spend meta currencies at the stations:
   - **Scientist** → the talent tree, for **talents**,
   - **Weapon table** → weapon upgrades, for **gold**,
   - **Quest board** → missions (rewarded in talents) and the choice of **challenge**,
   - **Portal** → starts a run.
4. **Run** — floors 1 to 5. Each floor has rooms with enemies, a shop and a boss. When you defeat the boss, a
   **ladder** to the next floor appears. The boss on floor 5 = **victory**.
5. **End of run** — death, giving up (Die in the pause menu) or victory → a stats screen
   (Game Over / Victory!), gold is added, missions advance and the challenge is evaluated.
6. Any key → back to the **lab**.

### What carries over between runs

| What | How you get it | What you spend it on |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | Reward on the end-of-run screen (accuracy, kills, loot, floor) | Weapon upgrades at the Weapon table |
| **Talents** | Completed missions on the Quest board, first completion of a challenge | The talent tree at the Scientist |
| **Coins** | Collecting coins in the dungeon | Shop, slot machine — **lost at the end of the run** |
| Stats | Automatically at the end of every run | Not shown anywhere yet |

## Known inconsistencies

- The talent and gold top-up in the lab switches the economy off completely — whatever you buy is refilled the next time you enter.
- The game keeps stats (number of runs, wins, kills, deepest floor...) but does not show them anywhere.
- You cannot get from a running run back to the menu or the lab other than by dying or using the Die button.
- Most of the story is not in the game yet — see [Story](/docs/progression/story).

## Ideas for expansion

- A profile / run history screen with stats and the seed.
- A "Give up and return to the lab" option in the pause menu, without having to "die".
