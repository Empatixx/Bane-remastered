title: Currencies and meta progression
description: The game's three currencies (coins, gold, talents), where they come from, what they are spent on and what permanently strengthens the character.
---

Bane has **three separate currencies**. Coins are used only within a single run, while gold and talents are carried
to the lab and spent on permanent upgrades.

## How it works

| Currency | Source | Spent on | Survives a run? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coins** (banecoin) | Coins from enemies, crates, chests, flowerpots, artefacts | Shop, slot machine | **No** — lost |
| **Gold** | Rewards from the end-of-run screen | Weapon upgrades at the Weapon table | Yes |
| **Talents** | Claimed missions, first completion of a challenge | The talent tree at the Scientist | Yes |

Coins are a purely in-run economy — see [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items). The number of
coins collected does count toward the *Gold Digger* and *Hoarder* missions, though.

### Gold

- **Earning:** the sum of four rewards from the end-of-run screen — the calculation is in [End of run](/docs/progression/run-end).
- **Spending:** at the **Weapon table** — each weapon has its own upgrade tree. A node costs 100 gold in the first row and every further row costs 100 more (100, 200, 300...); a mutation node costs **800**.
- **Rerolling** a weapon's tree gives you back all the gold you spent and charges a fee of **100**. In detail: [Guns](/docs/guns).
- When the shape of the weapon trees changes in the game, the old trees are reset and you get your gold back.

### Talents

- **Earning:** missions (1–3 talents by difficulty, boss missions 2) and challenges (3–6, only the first time). See [Missions](/docs/progression/missions), [Challenges](/docs/progression/challenges).
- **Spending:** at the **Scientist** in the lab. The price grows with the row in the tree: **2 / 3 / 5 / 5** talents (rows 2 to 5), and the whole tree costs **45**.
- The tree has 3 branches of 5 talents each. They are bought in order (each requires the previous one). **You get the first talent of each branch for free.**

### Test top-up

To try out upgrades, the lab can be started in a "rich" mode, which tops talents up to 1000 and gold up to 5000.
In the normal game nothing is topped up — talents and gold have to be earned.

## Tips and connections

- In the lab you see the counters: talents in orange, gold in yellow.
- The Scientist comments on how many talents you have — see [The lab](/docs/progression/lobby).
- Challenges can suppress some talents (dodging, armor regeneration, HP) — see [Rules](/docs/progression/rules).

## Known inconsistencies

- A talent's quality (Common / Rare / Epic) is only visual — the price depends on the row in the tree.
- Coins from a run are lost completely.
- Bought talents cannot be returned.

## Ideas for expansion

- Multi-level talents, so there is something to spend on even after the tree is bought out.
- Converting some of the leftover coins to gold (for example 10%), so you do not have to spend everything blindly.
- The option to redistribute talents (respec), maybe for gold.
