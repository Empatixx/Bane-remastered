Missions are the main source of **talents**. On the Quest board in the lab (the **Tasks** tab) there are always three,
each of a different kind. They are completed gradually over runs, and after completing one you claim the reward by clicking the ticket.

## How it works

- There are always **3 missions** on the board, no two of the same kind.
- Progress is not counted during the run but only **at its end** — from the run's stats and the floor reached.
- Missions are counted in two ways:
  - **cumulative** — progress adds up across all runs (kills, bosses, total coins, items, floors without a hit),
  - **best run** — only the best single run counts (floor reached, coins in one run).
- You **claim** a completed mission by clicking the ticket → the talents are added and a new mission (of a different kind than the other two) is rolled in its place right away.
- When you complete a mission in a run, the end-of-run screen shows "1 mission ready on the board" / "N missions ready on the board".
- Most kinds have **3 difficulty tiers**; a harder tier = a higher goal and more talents (1 / 2 / 3).
- The roll order is fixed for your profile — restarting the game does not reroll your missions.

## Contents

| Mission | Description in the game | Goal (tier 1 / 2 / 3) | Reward (talents) | Counting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crypt Cleaner | Defeat N enemies | 50 / 120 / 250 | 1 / 2 / 3 | cumulative |
| *enemy* Hunter (for example "rats Hunter") | Defeat N rats / bats / slimes / ghosts / snakes | 10 / 25 / 50 | 1 / 2 / 3 | cumulative kills of that type |
| *boss* Slayer | Defeat the *boss* | 1 | **2** | cumulative kills of that boss |
| Deep Delver | Reach floor N | 2 / 3 / 4 | 1 / 2 / 3 | best run |
| Gold Digger | Collect N coins in a single run | 40 / 80 / 140 | 1 / 2 / 3 | best run |
| Hoarder | Collect N coins | 150 / 300 / 600 | 1 / 2 / 3 | cumulative |
| Scavenger | Pick up N items | 15 / 30 / 60 | 1 / 2 / 3 | cumulative |
| Untouchable | Clear N floors without taking damage | 1 / 2 / 3 | 1 / 2 / 3 | cumulative floors without a hit |

**Hunted enemies** for the Hunter mission: rats, bats, slimes, ghosts, snakes.

**Bosses for the Slayer mission:** each biome has exactly one boss, so the boss waits on the floor with its biome:

| Boss | Biome |
|---|---|
| Spider king | Spider |
| Stone golem | Stone |
| Sewer ogre | Sewer |
| King slime | Moss |
| Argus | Ice |
| Hellhound | Lava |
| Mimic | Temple |

A run goes through five of the seven biomes, so the boss you are looking for appears in a given run with a probability of about 5 : 7.

"Coins" in the Gold Digger and Hoarder missions are coins collected in the dungeon, not gold.

## Tips and connections

- Claiming: [Quest board](/docs/progression/quest-board). Spending talents: [Currencies](/docs/progression/currencies).
- Enemies and bosses: [Enemies](/docs/enemies).
- **Untouchable:** a floor counts only when you leave it by the ladder (or win). A floor where you die does not count — even if nothing hit you on it.
- Gold Digger and Hoarder count coins collected, not your balance — spending in the shop does not cost you progress.

## Known inconsistencies

- The name of the enemy-hunting mission starts with a lowercase letter and is in the plural ("rats Hunter" instead of "Rat Hunter").
- The boss mission depends on chance: the biomes of the floors are shuffled randomly in every run and a run goes through only five of seven, so in some runs you do not meet the boss you are looking for at all.
