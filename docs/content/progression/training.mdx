title: Training crypt
description: The intro training before the lab. It has ten rooms, and each one teaches one thing. A guide walks with you, death returns you to the start of the room, and training can be skipped or repeated.
---

On the **first launch**, the **Play** button does not send you straight to the lab but to the training crypt.
The crypt is ten small rooms in a row. Each one teaches one thing, and the gate to the next room
opens only when you pass it. You play as **Elian**, the youngest novice of the Order of the Seal. All the way you are guided by the wounded **brother Aldric**, the only other brother who survived that night (see [Story](/docs/progression/story)). He gives advice in speech bubbles above his head.

The first time you press **Play**, the intro cinematic plays first (rendered directly by the game engine; you can skip it with Esc, space or Enter). After its fade to black, training starts in the dark: the crypt slowly lights up, you appear in a column of purple light that sparks are drawn into, and brother Aldric walks up to you and speaks once he stands next to you.

## How it works

### Lessons

| # | Lesson | Task | Keys |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Movement | Walk the winding corridor to its end. The keys blink on the floor. | **W A S D** |
| 2 | Shooting | Shoot 3 flowerpots. You see your ammo at the bottom right, **R** reloads. | mouse |
| 3 | Loot | Shoot a barrel (coins and ammo drop), take the shotgun from the table (E) and switch weapons (1/2). | **E**, **1**, **2** |
| 4 | Dodge | Jump over a strip of spikes with a roll. Nothing can hurt you during the roll. | **Space** |
| 5 | Health | Kill a rat, then drink a potion. Armor takes hits before hearts. | — |
| 6 | Traps and surfaces | Cross the room: arrow traps, water slows you, lava burns, ice slides, spikes | **Space** |
| 7 | Cover | An Uzi lies on the table by the entrance (E). Beat the slime and the rats. You can flip the table over and hide behind it. | — |
| 8 | Artefact | A Ring of Death lies on the table in the middle: pick it up (E), which lures 3 rats, and use it (F) | **E**, **F** |
| 9 | Trial | An M4 lies on the table by the entrance. The entrance closes behind you and a wave comes: 3 rats and a bat. After it, the entrance opens again. | — |
| 10 | Exit | E at the portal sends you straight to the lab | **E** |

### Rooms

The first lesson is a wide winding corridor, the dodge is a narrow corridor over spikes and the traps lesson is a long passage.
The other rooms are halls built symmetrically like the treasure rooms in the dungeon:
- there is a pillar in each corner,
- torches hang above the pillars and banners on both sides of the center,
- cobwebs hang in both upper corners,
- in the halls with loot, health and the portal there is a carpet in the middle.

Each hall also has its own feature:
- shooting is an armory with sword racks and armor,
- loot has crates and candelabras,
- health has fire bowls,
- cover is a library,
- the artefact lies between four candelabras,
- the trial is lit by four fire bowls,
- the portal stands between two angel statues.

The entrances are in the middle of the walls, and the corridors between the halls turn, because the halls lie at different heights. Vertical chains hang
in the corners of the corridors. Above three rooms (health, artefact, traps) there is an alcove behind bars in the middle of the upper wall,
with water and a pipe, and with lava in the traps room. The crypt has no minimap and the camera follows the player freely.

### Hints

- The keys that a lesson teaches **blink on the floor**: W A S D at the start, Space before the spikes, E and F at the artefact.
- Short labels are placed by the keys on the floor (*Move*, *Roll*, *take*, *use*). Everything else is said by the guide in bubbles. There is no panel or minimap on the screen.
- When you complete a lesson, a sound plays, a green *Done!* appears above the player and the gate opens.
- The player starts with only a pistol and no artefact. Weapons and the artefact always lie on a table (the same as in the shop): the shotgun in lesson 3, the Uzi in 7, the artefact in 8 and the M4 in 9.
- If the player drops a weapon (Q) and is left with an empty hand, the game puts the other weapon in the hand by itself.
- Ammo is refilled by itself during training, but you still have to reload (R).

### Death

Every room is a checkpoint. When you die, the screen briefly fades to dark and you appear at the start of the room
with full health and armor. The lesson is set up again and you lose nothing. After your **third death** in the same
room, the guide gives you advice.

### Skipping and repeating

- **Esc → Skip Training** in the pause menu skips training. The game first asks if you are serious.
- Both finishing and skipping are saved to the profile, so next time **Play** leads straight to the lab.
- You can repeat training any time: in the lab at the **book on the table** on the left with the **Training** sign (E, *Train again*).

## Tips and connections

- Bought upgrades and perks do not apply in training, so everyone plays it the same.
- Controls in detail: [Controls](/docs/player/controls). Traps and surfaces: [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).
- For development: `BANE_START=training` starts the game straight in the crypt and `BANE_LESSON=n` starts from lesson n.
