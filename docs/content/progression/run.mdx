---
title: Run flow and floors
description: What happens from entering the portal through the individual floors to the last boss.
---

A run is one descent into the dungeon. It has **5 floors**, and each ends with a boss. Whoever defeats the boss of floor 5
wins. Whoever dies earlier returns to the lab with what they managed to earn.

## How it works

### Start of the run

Entering the portal starts a new run on floor 1:

- Every run has its own random **seed** — the floor layout and loot differ every time.
- If you have a **challenge** selected on the Quest board, its [rules](/docs/progression/rules) apply for the whole run.
- Talents (more HP, armor, speed...) and weapon upgrades from the Weapon table take effect right away.

### Each floor

- **Biome**: at the start of the run, all seven biomes (Spider, Stone, Sewer, Moss, Ice, Lava, Temple)
  are shuffled randomly and the floors get five of them — no biome repeats in one run and none is
  tied to a specific floor. Each biome has its own boss, so the boss of a floor is random too.
- **Floor size** is rolled again for every floor: 4–7 combat rooms, 0–2 treasure rooms,
  0–1 altar and 1–2 ambushes.
- Rooms with enemies, a shop (unless the challenge forbids it), other special rooms and a boss. In detail: [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).
- The talent **Discount** gives a discount in the shop, **Trap Master** removes some of the traps, **Pet** brings a pet.
- The talent **Gamble machine**: a slot machine stands in the shop – without the talent it is not there.
- A minimap (small in the corner, large on Tab) and a HUD with health, armor, coins, weapons and the artefact.

### What happens during a floor

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| You clear a room | With the talent **Armor Regeneration** +1 armor (up to the maximum; some challenges turn this off) |
| The next wave starts | A "wave X / Y" banner (only in rooms with several waves) |
| You survive an ambush | A chest with a weapon |
| You enter the boss room | Boss music plays |
| You defeat the boss | Screen shake, an **artefact chest**; on floors 1–4 a **ladder** appears, on floor 5 **victory** |

### Descending to the next floor

Press **E** at the ladder. You take your health, armor, coins, all weapons, the artefact
and the run stats to the next floor. A floor where nothing hit you counts as "untouched" (for the Untouchable mission).

### The end

- **Victory**: the boss on floor 5 is defeated.
- **Death**: health drops to 0, or you choose **Die** or **Main Menu** in the pause menu.

Both lead to the same screen — see [End of run](/docs/progression/run-end).

## Contents

| Floor | Biome | End of floor |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Random from all seven, no repeats | Biome boss → ladder |
| 5 | Random (from the remaining ones) | Biome boss → **Victory!** |

### Difficulty by floor

Applies to regular enemies and to bosses.

| Floor | Enemy HP | Enemy damage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ×1.12 | ×1.08 |
| 2 | ×1.24 | ×1.16 |
| 3 | ×1.36 | ×1.24 |
| 4 | ×1.48 | ×1.32 |
| 5 | ×1.60 | ×1.40 |

The Horde and Bane challenges add their own multiplier on top.

### What is counted during the run

| Stat | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Shots and hits | Accuracy on the end-of-run screen |
| Kills (total and by enemy type) | Gold reward, missions |
| Items picked up | The "Loot" row, the Scavenger mission |
| Coins collected | The Gold Digger and Hoarder missions |
| Floors without a hit | The Untouchable mission |

## Tips and connections

- Biomes and bosses: [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon), [Enemies](/docs/enemies).

- Coins do not carry over to the next run — spend them in the shop before you die.

## Known inconsistencies

- According to the story, floor 5 should always be the temple (Temple) with the final boss **The Heart**. In the game, the biome of floor 5 is random and the final boss is just the ordinary boss of that biome.
- After victory an artefact chest appears, but the Victory screen comes up immediately — the chest cannot be opened.

## Ideas for expansion

- A fixed floor 5 = Temple and the final boss The Heart.
- A "breather" between floors: a choice of reward or a shop before descending.
- Optional difficulty with stacking modifiers (Heat), not just one challenge.
