The Quest board is a board in the lab, a little to the left of the Weapon table by the top wall. According to the story, these are
the Scientist's working notes. Here you find your **missions** and the choice of **challenge**.

## How it works

- Walk up to the board ("Press E to read the board") and press **E**. You close it with **Esc** or **E** again.
- While the board is open, the lab stands still and the background is blurred.
- There are two tabs at the top, switched by clicking:

| Tab | Contents | What a click on a ticket does |
|---|---|---|
| **Tasks** | 3 active missions | A completed mission → you claim the talents and a new one takes its place. The Scientist says "Talents earned!" and a coin clinks. |
| **Challenges** | All 6 challenges | Selects / switches / cancels the challenge, the portal changes color and a click sounds. |

### The ticket

Each ticket shows:

- the name (for the active challenge, a green "Active"),
- a short description,
- a progress bar — **red** until completed, then **green**,
- progress as a number (for a challenge 0/1 or 1/1),
- the reward "+N" with a talent icon (missing for an already completed challenge).

The tickets are pinned in three columns, slightly crooked. They are highlighted on mouse-over and completed ones have a different paper look.

## Contents

- The **Tasks** tab — a list of all kinds of missions: [Missions](/docs/progression/missions).
- The **Challenges** tab — a list of all challenges: [Challenges](/docs/progression/challenges).

## Tips and connections

- Check the board after every run — completed missions are **not claimed by themselves**.
- A change on the board is saved immediately ([What the game remembers](/docs/progression/saves)).
- Other stations in the lab: [The lab](/docs/progression/lobby).

## Known inconsistencies

- If you forget about the board, the talents for completed missions lie there unclaimed and nothing warns you about them.
