title: Run rules
description: The modifiers with which a challenge changes a whole run — HP, damage, allowed weapons, shops, dodging and armor regeneration.
---

Every run is played under a set of rules. Normally they are all neutral; only a selected
[challenge](/docs/progression/challenges) changes them. The rules apply from entering the portal until the end of the run.

## How it works

The rules affect three things:

1. **You** — they can cap your maximum HP, raise your damage, leave you only one weapon, turn off
   dodging or armor regeneration. A rule can only **turn off** a talent, it never gives you a talent you do not have.
2. **Enemies** — they can raise their HP (including bosses) and damage (by touch and by projectiles).
3. **The dungeon** — they can forbid shops.

For enemies, values are rounded to whole numbers and nothing drops below 1 — for example damage 2 × 1.3 = 2.6 → **3**. Your damage multiplier is added to the multiplier from talents, and a fraction of a point turns into a chance for an extra point — see [Perks](/docs/artefacts-items/perks).

## Contents

| Rule | Normally | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Max HP cap | no cap | The player's maximum health does not exceed the given value |
| Player damage | ×1 | Multiplies all your damage |
| Enemy HP | ×1 | Multiplies the health of enemies and bosses |
| Enemy damage | ×1 | Multiplies the touch and projectile damage of enemies |
| Single weapon | no restriction | You can use only one specific weapon |
| Shops | yes | A shop is generated on the floors |
| Dodging | yes | You can dodge |
| Armor regeneration | yes | The Armor Regeneration talent works |

Which challenge changes what: the table in [Challenges](/docs/progression/challenges).

## Known inconsistencies

- Enemy buffs (Horde, Bane) affect only touch damage, jumps and regular projectiles. Some special attacks (lasers, flames, falling rocks, magma) probably stay weaker than they should be.
- Rules cannot be combined — only one challenge can be active.

## Ideas for expansion

- New rules: faster enemies, more enemies per wave, no healing, more expensive shop, no artefacts.
- Stacking several rules at once with a growing reward (a Heat-like system from Hades).
