Game text is in English. The story is told as in The Binding of Isaac: almost no text during
a run, short lines from characters and bosses, the truth bit by bit. The in-game lines are in `rust/app/src/lore.rs`
(bosses, the whisper of the wound), `systems/scientist.rs` (the Scientist, Aldric) and `scenes/epilogue.rs` (the ending). The ghosts of bosses and the rifts with whispers are in `systems/souls.rs`.

## The story

For four hundred years, the people of Velmor prayed in the temple on the hill. The brothers of the Order of the Seal kept watch
so that at least one voice was always praying, and what sleeps under the altar kept sleeping.

One night the brothers fell asleep at their prayers, one after another. **Elian**, the youngest novice, was the last
awake. Then he fell asleep too. A wound (**Bane**) opened under the temple, the things that crawled out slaughtered the order, and only Elian and the wounded
**brother Aldric** escaped into the crypt. **Elian never told anyone that he had fallen asleep.**

The player is Elian. In training, Aldric teaches him to fight and sends him to the **Scientist**, whose portal leads down.
Elian wants to close the wound and bring the brothers back. The wound offers him exactly that, only deeper.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What he wants** | to close the wound and bring the brothers back |
| **The lie** | "It wasn't my fault, something attacked us." |
| **What he needs** | to admit it and let them go |

## How it is told

- **Intro cinematic:** a day in the temple, night, the brothers falling asleep one by one, Elian kneeling alone before the altar and finally falling asleep. The wound, the monsters, the escape into the crypt. The last line: "Elian never told anyone that he had fallen asleep."
- **The whisper of the wound:** at the start of every floor from the second on, a small rift opens in the floor next to Elian, whispers from a bubble above it ("Deeper. I'll give them back.", "You wanted to sleep. So sleep.") and closes again.
- **The bosses are people of the temple.** On entering, you see their name and who they were. When someone who was a person falls (Teodor, Cassian, The Faithful), their ghost appears next to Elian out of sparks (a priest or a pilgrim, translucent purple). In a bubble it says its last words, raises its hands and dissolves into golden light. For the others (the angel, the eye, the dog, the chest) a caption speaks. The last words reveal bit by bit who was on watch that night:

| Boss | Who it was | Last words |
|---|---|---|
| Spider king | **Teodor**, the scribe | "...who was on watch... that night...?" |
| King slime | **The Faithful**, the people of Velmor | "WE PRAYED ALL DAY  YOU ONLY HAD THE NIGHT" |
| Sewer ogre | **Cassian**, the gatekeeper | "...the prayers stopped... I ran to the nave... the boy..." |
| Stone golem | **The Angel** above the altar | "I SAW YOU CLOSE YOUR EYES" |
| Argus | **The Eye** of the wound | shows a small kneeling figure with a bowed head, asleep |
| Hellhound | **Oswin's dog** | stops fighting, sniffs you and lies down |
| Mimic | **The Offering Box** | "...more... more... MORE..." |

- **The lab:** brother Aldric keeps a vigil between five candles by the training book. He blames himself ("I fell asleep at my prayers that night. God forgive me.") and speaks of the defeated bosses as brothers.
- **Death screen:** "Subject 41. Attempt N. Recorded." (Elian is the Scientist's 41st volunteer.)

## The ending

For now it comes after the first win of the **Bane** challenge, as a scene in the engine with the same cinematic frame as the intro
(`BANE_START=epilogue`, `BANE_CHOOSE=close|open`). It is a hall of temple stone deep below it, with a rift
at the top. Elian arrives along a carpet (his own walk, back to the camera). Out of sparks, Teodor, Oswin, Cassian and one of the Faithful appear before the rift. The wound speaks, the rift
grows stronger and the ground trembles. Elian kneels as he did that night and begins to nod off. Night falls across the hall and the brothers are kneeling in prayer again: Elian
remembers that he was the one who fell asleep. A choice:

- **Let them go:** the brothers dissolve one by one in golden sparks, the rift closes from the edges to the middle, a warm light turns on and Elian falls asleep. The portal in the lab goes dark, Aldric: "It's quiet. Truly quiet."
- **Stay:** Elian stands up and walks into the rift among them. The brothers vanish behind him, purple swallows everything. The Scientist writes down "Subject 41, lost."
