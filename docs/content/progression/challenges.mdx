title: Challenges
description: Six optional run handicaps that are chosen on the Quest board and reward talents once.

A challenge is a voluntary handicap for a run. You choose it on the Quest board (the **Challenges** tab), it changes the
[rules](/docs/progression/rules) of the run and for the first successful completion you get talents.

## How it works

- Click a ticket to select a challenge; click the selected one to cancel it, click another to switch. **At most one** is active at a time — you can tell by the green "Active" label.
- The selection **applies to all further runs** until you cancel it.
- The selected challenge **colors the portal** in the lab with its color, so you see what awaits you already at the entrance.
- At the end of a run: if you have never completed the challenge before and you reached the goal, you get talents — **only once in the whole game**. The end-of-run screen shows "*Name* complete! +N talents".
- **Goal:** for most challenges it is enough to **reach** the target floor (even if you die on it). The challenge with the goal "Beat the last boss" requires victory.
- You can play a completed challenge again, but there will be no reward (it disappears from the ticket).

## Contents

| Challenge | Description in the game | What it changes | Goal | Reward | Portal color |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glass Cannon | One heart, double damage. Reach floor 3 | max. 1 HP, your damage ×2 | floor 3 | **4** talents | red |
| Pistol Only | No gun but the pistol. Reach floor 3 | pistol only | floor 3 | **3** | golden yellow |
| Horde | Tougher, harder hitting enemies. Reach floor 4 | enemy HP ×1.6, enemy damage ×1.3 | floor 4 | **4** | green |
| Merchant's Strike | No shops. Reach floor 4 | no shops | floor 4 | **3** | blue |
| Two Left Feet | No dodging. Reach floor 3 | you cannot dodge | floor 3 | **3** | orange |
| Bane | Horde without armor regen. Beat the last boss | like Horde + armor regeneration does not work | **victory** (floor 5) | **6** | dark red |

In total, you can get **23 talents** from challenges.

## Tips and connections

- Dodging and HP: [Player](/docs/player); the pistol: [Guns](/docs/guns/pistol).
- **Glass Cannon:** with 1 HP armor decides — the talents Improved Armor and Armor Regeneration pay off here.
- **Merchant's Strike:** you have nothing to spend coins on, so it is an ideal run for the Gold Digger mission.
- **Bane** is key according to the story — completing it is meant to unlock the true ending ([Story](/docs/progression/story)).

## Known inconsistencies

- Completing the **Bane** challenge has no story effect yet (the true ending does not exist).
- The enemy buff in Horde and Bane does not apply to some special attacks — see [Run rules](/docs/progression/rules).

## Ideas for expansion

- Combining several challenges at once with a growing reward (a Heat-like system from Hades).
- Descriptions as Scientist notes (for example Horde: "I fed it a little. Let us see what grew.").
- New challenges: "No Artefacts", "Speedrun" (a time limit per floor), "Bullet Sponge" (enemies ×3 HP, player ×2 damage), "Poverty" (enemies drop no coins), "One Gun" (the first weapon picked up stays forever).
