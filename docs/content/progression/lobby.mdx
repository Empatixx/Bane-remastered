The lab (lobby) is the Scientist's workroom above the wound called Bane. You return here after every run. You walk
around as in the dungeon (movement, dodging, pet), but you **do not shoot** and there are no enemies.
Calm lab music plays.

## How it works

When you arrive in the lab:

- You stand in the middle of the room. At the top center is the **portal**, colored by the selected challenge, with swirling particles.
- Around you are the stations: the **Scientist**, the **Quest board**, the **Weapon table**.
- In the HUD you see your weapons including upgrades from the Weapon table; with the **Pet** talent a pet stands next to you.
- Counters for talents (orange) and gold (yellow).

### Controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| WASD, Space | Movement, dodge |
| **E** at a station | Opens / closes the station panel; at the portal it starts a run |
| Esc | Closes an open panel; otherwise pause (Resume / Settings / Main Menu / Exit Game) |

While a panel or the pause menu is open, the lab stands still.

After you buy a talent or a weapon upgrade, the Scientist says "Excellent choice!"; after you claim a mission, "Talents earned!".

### What the Scientist says

The Scientist talks on his own when you are nearby (up to about 5–6 tiles). Between sentences he pauses for at least **7 seconds**
and does not talk while another bubble is showing. When you go far away (about 12 tiles) and come back, he greets you again.
What he says depends on your progress, which the game remembers in the profile.

1. **One-time messages** — when something new happened in the last run (deepest floor, first boss kill,
   a win...), the Scientist says it first of all, even before the greeting. He says each message **only once**;
   what you do not manage to hear (for example if you walk straight into the portal) waits for the next visit. At most
   4 messages wait at a time, the most important first: win → first boss → new floor → challenge → deaths → kills.
2. **A greeting** on arrival, alternating between two sentences depending on where you are.
3. Then he alternates:
   - when you have no talents → "No talents? Go loot!"
   - when you have at least 100 talents → "Talents to spend!"
   - otherwise one of the hints; sentences that you "unlock" by progressing are added to the general hints.

### Decorations

2× weapon rack, 4× torch, 2× bones, 2× cobweb, 2× armor stand, crate, 2× chains,
4× stone, 2× bookcase, a table with a map and **3 breakable flowerpots**.

## Tips and connections

- The portal color shows the selected challenge — [Challenges](/docs/progression/challenges).
- The Scientist and the merchant in the dungeon talk with the same bubbles — [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).
- From the lab you get to the main menu through Esc → Main Menu; you open settings straight from the pause menu (Esc → Settings).

## Known inconsistencies

- The game remembers killed bosses only since the version with the Scientist's lines; with an older profile the Scientist therefore comments on the "first" kill of a boss even if you had already beaten it earlier.
- "Excellent choice!" is the only reaction to any purchase.
- The pet sits next to you; according to the story it should be a wolf waiting by the portal.

## Ideas for expansion

- More Scientist reactions to purchases (the story suggests for example "Cleaned it myself. Mostly.").
- A lab that changes with progress — trophies of defeated bosses, new decorations after a win.
- A practice range with a dummy for trying out weapons and their upgrades.
- The map table could show profile stats or run history.
