Bane is a 2D top-down roguelike shooter. The player is a test subject whom the Scientist sends through a portal
into the **Bane** — a wound in the world whose floors rearrange themselves after every descent.

This wiki describes what is in the game and how it should work: weapons, enemies, artefacts, dungeon floors
and the whole run. It is an overview of the content and a place for ideas on what to add.

<Callout type="info">
Pages have a similar structure: **How it works** → **Stats / Contents** → **Tips and connections**
→ **Known inconsistencies** → **Ideas for expansion**. The last two sections are where to look for what is left to do.
</Callout>
