Perks are permanent talents that you buy outside a run from the Scientist in the lobby (see [Progression](/docs/progression) for the talent tree and buying). This page describes how they show up **during the game**.

## How it works

- Talents apply at the start of a run (maximum health and armor - the current values rise by the added difference) and then on every floor (gun slots, ammo, traps).
- Some challenge talents modify or limit things - they change damage, the health cap, allow only the pistol, or turn off dodging or armor regeneration.

<Callout type="info">
The first talent of each branch is **free and always unlocked**. So every player always has Swiftwind Steps (+10% speed), Sharpened Bullets (+20% damage) and Hero's Heart (+20% health).
</Callout>

## Contents

| Talent | Tree | Effect in the game |
|---|---|---|
| Swiftwind Steps | Utility, free | movement speed +10% |
| Discount | Utility | a chance of a discount on one table in the [shop](/docs/artefacts-items/shop) - most often -25% |
| Trap Master | Utility | each trap has roughly a 30% chance of being disabled - spikes retracted, flames out, no damage |
| Additional Gun | Utility | +1 gun slot |
| Gamble Machine | Utility | the [slot machine](/docs/artefacts-items/slot-machine) stands in the shop - without the talent it is not there |
| Sharpened Bullets | Offensive, free | damage +20% |
| Additional Ammo | Offensive | maximum ammo reserve +10% |
| Critical Hit | Offensive | 10% chance of double damage (for the shotgun separately for each pellet) |
| Dangerous Bullets | Offensive | damage +25% (multiplies with Sharpened Bullets) |
| Pet | Offensive | wolf companion - see [Pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) |
| Hero's Heart | Defensive, free | max health +20% (10 -> 12) |
| Improved Armor | Defensive | max armor +20% (5 -> 6) |
| Armor Regeneration | Defensive | +1 armor after clearing a room |
| Titan's Heart | Defensive | max health +25% (12 -> 15) |
| Kevlar | Defensive | damage taken -10% on average; the leftover fraction of a point is decided by chance |

### Kevlar in practice

10% is taken off a hit and the decimal part is rounded by chance - up with a probability equal to its size. So a hit for 1 takes **nothing** in 10% of cases, a hit for 2 takes 2 in 80% of cases and only 1 in 20%, a hit for 3 takes 3 in 70% of cases and only 2 in 30%. On average every hit is one tenth weaker, whether it is big or small.

### Damage in practice

Bonuses from talents are added into a single multiplier - with Sharpened Bullets x1.2, with both talents x1.5 - and the decimal part of the result is rounded by chance, just like with Kevlar. So a gun with 1 damage deals 2 on every fifth shot with Sharpened Bullets, and on every second shot with both talents. Then comes, if applicable, x2 for a critical hit and x2 for the first shot from [Phantom Cloak](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/phantom-cloak). Artefacts (Ring of Death, Bone Turret, Thunder Idol, ghosts from Grave Lantern) and the wolf **do not use** talents.

## Known inconsistencies

- Because the first talents are free, "+20% health / damage" are in fact the base values of every player.

## Ideas for expansion
