The wolf is a loyal companion you get with the **Pet** talent (the last talent of the offensive tree). It appears next to you at the start of every floor and in the lobby, collects loot for you and bites enemies.

## How it works

- The wolf **has no health** - enemies cannot hurt or kill it.
- At every moment it chooses what to do, in this order:
  1. **Collecting** - if there is an item you can use in its surroundings (about 8 x 8 tiles), it runs for it.
  2. **Attacking** - otherwise it pounces on an enemy in the same area.
  3. **Following** - otherwise it stays near you.
- The wolf avoids walls and finds its way. It forgets an item or enemy it has no path to.
- When it gets farther than roughly 8-9 tiles from you or cannot reach you, it **teleports** back to you.

### Movement

| Situation | Speed |
|---|---|
| running to an item or enemy | fast - about a quarter faster than the player |
| close to you (within 1.5 tiles) | stands still |
| following you | roughly your speed |
| catching up from far away | speeds up to full speed |

### Attack and collecting

- When the wolf touches an enemy, it bites for **1 damage**, at most **once per second** (1 damage per second). It does not attack enemies that are still spawning.
- Whatever the wolf runs over, you collect - coins go to your wallet, ammo to your guns, potions to you. It takes only what you can use.
- In the lobby the wolf just follows you.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Health | immortal |
| Damage | 1 per bite, at most 1 bite per second |
| Speed | about 1.25x player speed |
| Search range | area of about 8 x 8 tiles |
| Teleport to player | beyond roughly 8-9 tiles of distance |
| Stops near the player | within 1.5 tiles |

## Known inconsistencies

- **The wolf prefers collecting over attacking** - in a fight it runs for coins instead of biting. The original version of the game had it the other way around (attack has priority).
- **The wolf forgets unreachable targets permanently** - an enemy that was behind a wall for a moment is invisible to it until the end of the floor.
- **The wolf's damage does not grow** with the floor or with talents.
- According to the story, the wolf should "sit by the portal" in the lobby - in the game it just appears next to the player.
