---
title: Hourglass of Ash
description: Slows all enemies and their projectiles to 35% speed for 6 seconds.
---

Hourglass of Ash slows time for everything that belongs to the enemy. You, your projectiles and your allies keep moving at normal speed.

## How it works

- After you use it, enemy time slows to **35%** within a fraction of a second (0.3 s) and returns just as smoothly at the end.
- What is slowed:
  - the movement of enemies and their projectiles,
  - animations, attacks and abilities of enemies and bosses,
  - spawning of new enemies and waves in rooms,
  - traps and hazardous environment.
- **Running at normal speed:** you, your projectiles, the wolf, ghosts from Grave Lantern and the turret from Bone Turret.
- Using another hourglass during the effect extends the slowdown to its full length without a new ramp-up.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 6 s |
| Enemy speed | 35% |
| Ramp-up / ramp-down | 0.3 s |

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - really everything they do is slowed.
- [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon) - it also slows the wave countdowns in rooms.
- It combines very well with [Thunder Idol](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/thunder-idol), [Bone Turret](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/bone-turret) and [Grave Lantern](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/grave-lantern) - allies are almost three times faster than the slowed enemies.
- Best against bosses with dense projectile patterns - you can then walk through the gaps between projectiles easily.

## Known inconsistencies

- There is no visual indication of the slowdown (no screen tint or slowed sound) - you notice it only from how enemies move.

## Ideas for expansion

- A sepia or desaturated screen filter and slowed sounds for the duration of the effect.
- A variant that stops time completely for a shorter time.
