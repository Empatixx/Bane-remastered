Berserk Pot is a potion of fury that makes you much faster for 15 seconds. You recognize it by the changed particles trailing behind you.

## How it works

- After you use it, your movement speed rises by 30% for 15 seconds.
- The speed-up multiplies with the Swiftwind Steps talent (+10%) and with the slowdown in water, in a swamp or in a cobweb.
- It has no effect on damage, fire rate or dodge.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 15 s |
| Movement speed | +30% |

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - movement and surfaces.
- [Perks](/docs/artefacts-items/perks) - with the Swiftwind Steps talent you are about 43% faster than the base.
- Good for escaping an encirclement, running through a room with traps, or dodging boss bullet-hell patterns.

## Known inconsistencies

- **The effect continues after death** - it is the only timed artefact that is not cancelled on death.
- The name promises fury, but it adds only speed.
- There is no indicator of the remaining time (this applies to all timed artefacts).

## Ideas for expansion

- A real berserk: more damage or faster shooting, at the cost of more damage taken.
