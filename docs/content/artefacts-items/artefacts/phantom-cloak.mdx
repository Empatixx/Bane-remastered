title: Phantom Cloak
description: Makes you invisible to enemies for 5 seconds; the first shot from hiding deals double damage.
---

Phantom Cloak wraps you in shadow. Enemies stop seeing you and you have a moment to reposition or prepare a devastating first strike.

## How it works

- After you use it, you turn into a shadow and for **5 seconds** enemies cannot see you - they stop chasing you, aiming at you and using targeted abilities. The same applies to bosses.
- **Ambush attack** - the first shot during the cloak deals **double damage**, for all of its projectiles (including all Shotgun pellets). With that shot the cloak **disappears immediately**. Further projectiles from the burst no longer have the bonus.
- If you do not shoot, the cloak expires on its own after 5 seconds.
- Invisibility does not protect you from projectiles already in flight or from contact with an enemy.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 5 s or until the first shot |
| Damage of the first shot | x2 |

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - they lose their target and stand still.
- [Guns](/docs/guns) - the bonus multiplies with talents and a critical hit, so with the Railgun or Sniper it is a huge opening blow. Crit + cloak = quadruple damage.
- Traps and hazardous environment can see you from farther away.
- It is useful even without shooting - for escaping or running across a room.

## Known inconsistencies

- There is no sound for putting on or breaking the cloak, and no special indication of the boosted hit.
- Dodge does not cancel the cloak - they can be combined (it is not certain whether this is intended).

## Ideas for expansion

- A sound and effect on the ambush attack (e.g. a purple damage number).
- A variant where even a kill does not cancel the cloak but extends it.
