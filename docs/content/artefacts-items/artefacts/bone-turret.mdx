Bone Turret summons a bone turret at your feet that aims on its own and fires bone shards at the nearest enemies.

## How it works

- The turret rises from the ground (half a second), then actively fires for **12 seconds** and finally crumbles (about half a second).
- It aims at the **nearest enemy** within a distance of roughly 9 tiles and turns to follow it.
- Each shard deals **2 damage**, regardless of your talents.
- The turret has no health - enemies ignore it and it cannot be destroyed.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 12 s active |
| Range | about 9 tiles |
| Damage | 2 per shard |
| Fire rate | about 3 shots per second (about 5.7 damage/s, about 68 total) |
| Shard speed | fast (like gun projectiles) |

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - it does not target enemies that are still spawning or dying.
- Place it in the middle of the room where it can reach as many enemies as possible - the turret stays in place.
- With [Hourglass of Ash](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/hourglass-of-ash) it fires at slowed enemies at full speed.
- [Pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) and [Grave Lantern](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/grave-lantern) - other allies.

## Known inconsistencies

- The turret does not check whether it can see the target - it fires even through walls (the shards break on the wall).
- The shots have no sound.
- Damage talents do not affect it.

## Ideas for expansion

- Enemies could attack the turret (drawing attention away) - that would give the artefact tactical depth.
- A shot sound and aiming only at visible targets.
