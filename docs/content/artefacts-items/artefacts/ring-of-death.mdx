title: Ring of Death
description: Instantly fires 50 projectiles in a circle around the player.
---

Ring of Death is an emergency button for when you are surrounded. After you use it, a ring of 50 projectiles bursts out of you in all directions at once.

## How it works

- All 50 projectiles are created at once and fly away from you in all directions at the speed of a regular player projectile.
- Each projectile deals **2 damage**, regardless of your talents.
- The ring turns slightly more than a full circle, so in one direction (roughly a sixth of the circle) the projectiles fly twice as dense.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Number of projectiles | 50 |
| Damage per projectile | 2 (up to 100 total) |
| Projectile speed | fast (like gun projectiles) |
| Duration | instant |

## Tips and connections

- Best in [Ambush rooms](/docs/dungeon), where enemies come from all sides.
- Against one big target (a boss) it pays to stand right next to it - more projectiles hit it.
- [Guns](/docs/guns) - the projectiles look and behave like regular player projectiles.

## Known inconsistencies

- **The direction of the denser band does not depend on the mouse** - it is determined by your position on the floor, so it is practically random for the player.
- **The ring overshoots the full circle** (about 430 degrees) - part of the circle is covered twice.
- The name suggests a spiral, but all projectiles fly out at once as a circle.
- **Damage talents (Sharpened Bullets, Critical Hit) and Phantom Cloak do not affect it.**

## Ideas for expansion

- A real spiral - projectiles would fly out one after another with a small delay.
- An even circle, or a denser band in the aiming direction.
- Damage that grows with the floor or talents.
