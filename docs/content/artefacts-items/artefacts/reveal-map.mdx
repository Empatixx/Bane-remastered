title: Reveal Map
description: The starting artefact that reveals all rooms of the current floor on the map.
---

Reveal Map is the artefact you start every run with. After you use it, you see the whole floor on the minimap and the big map - including the shop, the Treasure room, Sacrifice and the boss arena.

## How it works

- Press **F** and all rooms of the floor are marked as discovered.
- The effect applies only to the **current floor** - the next floor is new and unexplored again.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Effect | reveals all rooms of the floor |
| Duration | instant, lasts until the end of the floor |
| Source | start of every run; can also be found again |

## Tips and connections

- [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon) - the minimap and room types.
- Use it right at the start of the floor if you want to head straight to the shop or the Treasure room. If you plan to find a better artefact, keep it until then - when you pick up a new one, it drops to the ground anyway.
- It can drop again from a chest, the shop or the slot machine - only the artefact you currently hold is excluded.

## Known inconsistencies

- Compared to other artefacts it is weak; as a starting item that makes sense, but from a chest after a boss it is a disappointment.
- It does not reveal chests, traps or anything hidden - only rooms.

## Ideas for expansion

- Highlight special rooms (Treasure, Sacrifice, Shop) on the map with a special icon.
- Reveal chests and traps too.
