title: Midas Coin
description: Turns all enemy projectiles into coins and gives 2 extra coins per kill for 6 seconds.
---

Midas Coin turns everything it touches into gold - including the projectiles flying at you right now. It is both a defensive and an economic artefact.

## How it works

1. **Golden touch** - all enemy projectiles in the air at that moment vanish and a **coin** with a golden flash stays in their place.
2. **6 seconds of wealth** - every enemy you kill during that time drops **2 extra coins**. The regular drop (see [Drops](/docs/artefacts-items/drops)) is not affected.

All coins from this artefact disappear after 10 seconds if you do not pick them up.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Transformation | all enemy projectiles at the moment of use |
| Bonus duration | 6 s |
| Bonus per kill | +2 coins |

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - strongest against bosses with dense projectile patterns: one press saves your life and makes you richer. King Slime and summoned minions do not give bonus coins.
- You spend the coins in the [shop](/docs/artefacts-items/shop) or the [slot machine](/docs/artefacts-items/slot-machine).
- The [Pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) helps you collect the coins before they disappear.

## Known inconsistencies

- It transforms only projectiles that are flying at the moment of use - not new projectiles.
- With a large number of projectiles, so many coins are created that you cannot collect them before they disappear.

## Ideas for expansion

- A variant where every new enemy projectile turns into a coin for the whole 6 seconds.
- Coins from the transformation that do not disappear.
