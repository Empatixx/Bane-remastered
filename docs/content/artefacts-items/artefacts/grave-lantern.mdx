title: Grave Lantern

Grave Lantern calls three green-glowing ghosts that become your allies for 12 seconds and throw themselves at the nearest enemies.

## How it works

- **3 ghosts** appear around you.
- Each ghost flies after the **nearest enemy** (with no range limit). When there is none, it circles around you.
- On touching an enemy, it bites for **2 damage**, then waits half a second.
- Ghosts pass through walls and enemies cannot hurt them.
- They gradually fade away during the last second.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Number of ghosts | 3 |
| Duration | 12 s |
| Damage | 2 per bite, 2 bites per second (4 damage/s per ghost, 12 damage/s all three) |
| Speed | slightly slower than the player |
| Light | green glow |

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - a ghost bite works the same as a wolf bite.
- With [Hourglass of Ash](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/hourglass-of-ash) the ghosts run at full speed while enemies are slowed.
- Ghosts light up their surroundings - in dark rooms they also help you see.
- [Pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) - a similar ally, but permanent.

## Known inconsistencies

- All three ghosts often fly at the same enemy instead of splitting the targets.
- Ghosts can fly off through a wall after an enemy in another room, off screen.
- They have no sound.

## Ideas for expansion

- Splitting targets between the ghosts.
- Limiting the range to the current room.
- Spooky sounds on summoning and biting.
