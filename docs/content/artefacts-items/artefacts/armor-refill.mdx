title: Armor Refill

---

Armor Refill is a simple defensive artefact - after you use it, your armor is full again.

## How it works

- Press **F** and armor is refilled to the maximum.
- The maximum armor is 5, with the Improved Armor talent 6.
- Using it at full armor wastes the artefact - the game does not stop you.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Effect | armor to maximum (up to +5, or +6) |
| Duration | instant |

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - armor absorbs hits before health.
- It works as a stronger version of the large armor potion (+4) from the [shop](/docs/artefacts-items/shop). The Armor Regeneration talent gives +1 armor after every cleared room ([Perks](/docs/artefacts-items/perks)).
- It pays off most right before a boss or in the middle of a tough fight when armor is at zero.

## Known inconsistencies

- Using it has no sound or visual effect - you notice the change only on the armor indicator.

## Ideas for expansion

- A sound and a blue flash on use.
- A temporary "overcharge" of armor above the maximum, so it is not just a better potion.
