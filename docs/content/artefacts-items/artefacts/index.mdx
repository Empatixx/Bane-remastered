Artefacts are single-use magic items. Each has a strong instant or time-limited effect - from healing through summoning allies to slowing time. You can carry only one at a time.

## How it works

- You have **one slot** for an artefact. Every run starts with the **Reveal Map** artefact.
- An artefact on the ground hovers, and when you stand on it, it has a yellow outline. On the ground it **never disappears**.
- **Pick up (E)** - the artefact moves into the slot. If you already have one, **the old one drops to the ground** and you can take it back later.
- **Use (F)** - triggers the effect and empties the slot. There is no recharging - the artefact is consumed.
- Timed effects run only for a set time. If you use the same timed artefact again, the countdown just restarts (effects do not stack).
- On death, active effects are cancelled.
- The icon of the held artefact is shown in the HUD; an empty slot is not shown.

### Where to get artefacts

| Source | Condition | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Run start | always | Reveal Map |
| Chest after a boss | after defeating the boss on each floor | 100% |
| Shop - Attack table | always if there is no gun left that you do not have; otherwise 50% | 50% and more |
| Pentagram (Sacrifice) | the goods are the Attack category (1/3) | roughly 1/6 and more |
| Slot machine | rare prize | about 1 in 150 per spin |
| Swapping | picking up a new artefact drops the old one | - |

A random artefact is chosen evenly from all except the one you currently hold - no rarities or weights.

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - Phantom Cloak hides you from them, Hourglass of Ash slows their movement, attacks and spawning.
- [Guns](/docs/guns) - Infinity Quiver removes ammo use, Phantom Cloak doubles the damage of the first shot.
- [Player](/docs/player) - Shield Horn, Armor Refill and Blood Chalice keep you alive, Berserk Pot speeds you up.
- Good combinations: Hourglass of Ash + Bone Turret or Grave Lantern (allies run at full speed, enemies do not), Phantom Cloak + a strong single-shot gun.
- An artefact you do not need right now can be left on the ground - it will not disappear and you can come back for it.

## Known inconsistencies

- **No rarities** - Reveal Map has the same chance to drop as Thunder Idol.
- **Berserk Pot continues after death** - it is the only timed effect that is not cancelled on death.
- **An artefact has no name or description in the game** - you see only the icon, so a new player does not know what they are picking up.
- **Artefact damage does not grow** with the floor or talents - Ring of Death, Bone Turret and Thunder Idol are much weaker on floor 5 than on floor 1.
- The original version of the game had only 5 artefacts, the current one has 13.

## Ideas for expansion

- Rarities and weighted selection, so strong artefacts are rarer.
- A name and short description of the artefact while standing over it.
- **Cursed Mirror** - reflects the next enemy projectile back.
- **Frost Bell** - freezes nearby enemies (freezing already exists in the game with frost barrels).
- **Poison Censer** - leaves a poison trail behind the player.
- **Magnet Charm** - pulls in all pickups in the room.
