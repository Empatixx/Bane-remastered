Shield Horn creates a magic aura around you that catches the whole next hit - no matter how strong.

## How it works

- After you use it, a glowing shield appears around you. It lasts **until something hits you** - it has no time limit.
- The next hit deals zero damage. It also absorbs attacks that would otherwise go through armor.
- The hit still behaves like a hit: you hear the damage sound, see a flash and get a short invulnerability (1 s).
- If you are already invulnerable (after a previous hit), the shield is not used up.
- After the hit, the aura disappears.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hits blocked | 1 |
| Duration | until the first hit |

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - the shield protects both health and armor.
- Most valuable against strong one-off boss attacks or explosions.
- A second Shield Horn used while the shield still holds adds nothing - shields do not stack.

## Known inconsistencies

- A blocked hit plays the damage sound and flash as if you were really hurt - there is no clear "shield broke" feedback.
