---
title: Infinity Quiver
description: Infinite ammo with no reloading for all guns for 8 seconds.
---

Infinity Quiver gives you an endless magazine for 8 seconds. You shoot without a break and without reloading.

## How it works

- After you use it, any ongoing reload is cancelled and for the duration of the effect:
  - shots and bursts do not use up ammo,
  - an empty magazine does not matter - you keep shooting,
  - the reload key does nothing.
- It applies to any gun, even if you switch in the meantime.
- The gun indicator in the HUD turns golden and shows a shimmering infinity symbol.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 8 s |
| Effect | no ammo use, no reloading |

## Tips and connections

- [Guns](/docs/guns) - strongest with guns that have a small magazine or a long reload (Minigun, RPG, Railgun).
- Ideal for a boss fight or for quickly clearing an Ambush room.
- Fire rate does not change - it pays to hold the fastest-firing gun.

## Known inconsistencies

- After the effect ends, the magazine stays in the state it was in before use - nothing is refilled.

## Ideas for expansion

- A fire rate bonus (e.g. +50%) to make the effect more noticeable.
- Refilling the magazine after the end.
