title: Blood Chalice

Blood Chalice is a chalice of blood that heals you at once and awakens bloodlust for a while - every kill then heals you.

## How it works

1. **Instant healing** - heals half of max health (rounded up; with 10 max health, +5). Blood splashes around you.
2. **Bloodlust for 10 seconds** - you get +1 health for every enemy killed. During the whole time, drops of blood rise from you and you pulse red.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Instant healing | 50% of max health |
| Bloodlust | 10 s |
| Healing per kill | +1 health |

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - apart from potions, the only source of healing in the game.
- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - summoned minions do not count toward healing; King Slime does.
- You get the most out of it when you use it in a room full of weak enemies or at the start of a wave.
- It goes well with the [Sacrifice](/docs/artefacts-items/shop) pentagram - sacrifice health and then heal with the chalice.

## Known inconsistencies

- Healing is not shown as a floating number - you see it only on the health indicator.
- A second use during bloodlust just restarts the countdown.
