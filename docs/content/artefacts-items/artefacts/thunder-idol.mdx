title: Thunder Idol

Thunder Idol sends a lightning bolt that jumps from enemy to enemy. It is the strongest instant offensive artefact in the game.

## How it works

- The lightning strikes the **nearest enemy** within a distance of roughly 7 tiles.
- From there it jumps to the nearest not-yet-hit enemy within roughly 5 tiles - and so on, up to **6 targets**.
- Each enemy hit takes **6 damage**.
- The screen shakes on a hit and a bright flash with sparks erupts at the hit locations.
- If nobody is in range, the artefact is used up for nothing.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Damage | 6 per target (up to 36 total) |
| Range of the first strike | about 7 tiles |
| Range of a jump | about 5 tiles |
| Max. number of targets | 6 |

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - it does not hit enemies that are still spawning or already dying.
- The lightning damage is the same kind as a barrel explosion.
- Best against a group of enemies standing close together. With [Hourglass of Ash](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/hourglass-of-ash) it is easier to get enemies into range.
- Before using it, make sure an enemy is nearby.

## Known inconsistencies

- **Using it with no enemy in range wastes the artefact.**
- There is no thunder sound.

## Ideas for expansion

- Return the artefact to the slot when there is nothing to strike.
- Damage falling with each jump (6 -> 5 -> 4 ...) in exchange for more targets.
- A short stun of the enemies hit.
