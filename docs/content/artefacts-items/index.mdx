During a run in Bane you meet three kinds of loot: small **pickups** (coins, ammo, potions) that are collected on their own, single-use **artefacts** with a strong active ability, and **guns**. You spend coins in the shop or gamble them away in the slot machine. Everything you carry - health, armor, coins, guns and your artefact - goes with you to the next floor.

## How it works

1. **Pickups** - coins, ammo boxes, and health or armor potions. You collect them by stepping on them (or your [pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) collects them for you). The game does not let you pick up anything useless: a health potion at full health or ammo for a full gun stays on the ground. See [Items](/docs/artefacts-items/items).
2. **Artefacts** - you have **one slot** for them. You pick up an artefact with **E** and use it with **F**. Every artefact is single-use - after that the slot is empty. Every run starts with the **Reveal Map** artefact. See [Artefacts](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts).
3. **Guns** - they lie on the ground and are picked up with **E**. Details in the [Guns](/docs/guns) section.

### Where loot comes from

- **Killing an enemy** - the longer nothing has dropped, the higher the drop chance; at the latest, every fifth kill drops something (a coin or ammo).
- **Breaking a flowerpot or barrel** - always drops one item.
- **Chest** - opens on touch and throws out 5 items plus a gun (weapon chest) or an artefact (chest after a boss).
- **Shop** - three tables with goods for coins, and a Sacrifice pentagram room where you pay with your own health.
- **Slot machine** - stands in the shop only with the Gamble Machine talent; 5 coins per spin, roughly a 40% chance of some win.
- **The Midas Coin and Blood Chalice artefacts** bring coins and healing.

You can find the exact probabilities on the page [Where and how things drop](/docs/artefacts-items/drops).

### Controls

| Action | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Interact | E | pick up an artefact or gun, buy goods, spin the slot machine |
| Use artefact | F | activates the artefact in the slot |
| (touch) | - | picks up a pickup, opens a chest |

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - you start with 10 health, 5 armor, 0 coins and the Reveal Map artefact.
- [Guns](/docs/guns) - ammo boxes refill all guns that take that ammo type; chests, the shop and the slot machine only offer guns you do not have yet.
- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - King Slime and summoned minions drop nothing.
- [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon) - loot is concentrated in the Shop, Treasure, Sacrifice, Ambush and Boss rooms.
- [Progression](/docs/progression) - talents like Discount, Gamble Machine, Pet or Additional Ammo change loot and the shop.

## Known inconsistencies

- **Potions never drop from enemies or crates** - you get them only in the shop, at the pentagram and from the slot machine. Apart from those, the only way to heal is the Blood Chalice artefact.

## Ideas for expansion

- **Keys and bombs** - locked chests or doors would fit naturally with the Treasure room.
- **A rare small potion drop** from enemies, so healing is not only a matter of the shop.
- **Rechargeable artefacts or passive relics** - today there is only one slot and everything is single-use.
- **More merchants / gods and a choice economy** - re-rolling the offer for coins, removing an unwanted item from the pool.
