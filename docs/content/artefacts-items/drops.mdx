This page summarizes where loot comes from in Bane and with what chance. All loot follows the run seed - with the same seed you find the same shops and chests, and with the same play you get the same drops.

## How it works

### 1. Enemies

The drop is decided when the enemy finishes its death animation. The game remembers how many enemies you have killed since the last drop, and the chance grows with each further kill:

| Kills since last drop | Drop chance |
|---|---|
| 1st | 33% |
| 2nd | 50% |
| 3rd | 67% |
| 4th | 83% |
| 5th | 100% (guaranteed) |

- After a drop, the counter resets.
- What drops: **25% coin, 50% pistol ammo, 25% shotgun ammo**. Ammo that none of your guns can use turns into the box your neediest gun takes (rifle, rocket or battery cell). The item disappears after 10 s.
- **King Slime and all summoned minions drop nothing.** Minions do not even count toward the kill statistics.

### 2. Breakable objects

- Flowerpots and barrels (plain, explosive, poison and frost) have 4 hit points.
- When broken, they **always drop 1 item** (coin or ammo, same chances as for enemies), which disappears after 10 s. Special barrels also explode, freeze or spill poison.
- More about objects: [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).

### 3. Chests

- They open on **touch**, not with a key.
- After the opening animation the chest disappears and **5 regular items** (coins / ammo) shoot out in a circle - they **do not disappear**.
- In addition, depending on the chest type:

| Chest | Where it appears | Extra contents |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon | in the middle of the Treasure room (1 per floor) and after clearing every Ambush room | a random gun you do not have yet (if any remain) |
| Artefact | after defeating a boss, above the center of the arena | a random artefact other than the one you hold |

### 4. Shop and Sacrifice

See [Shop](/docs/artefacts-items/shop) - three tables (large potion / universal ammo / gun or artefact) and a pentagram where you pay with health. The shop also has 2 flowerpots.

### 5. Slot machine

Only with the Gamble Machine talent. See [Slot machine](/docs/artefacts-items/slot-machine) - coins, a burst of 5 items, or a rare prize (potion, universal ammo, gun, artefact).

### 6. Artefacts

- [Midas Coin](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/midas-coin) - turns enemy projectiles into coins and gives +2 coins per kill for 6 s.
- [Blood Chalice](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts/blood-chalice) - healing instead of items.

### 7. Swapping

When you pick up a new artefact, the old one drops to the ground. The same goes for a gun when your arsenal is full (see [Guns](/docs/guns)).

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - who drops and who does not.
- [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon) - every floor has 1 Treasure, 1 shop (except in the Merchant's Strike challenge), 0-1 Sacrifice, 1-2 Ambush and a boss.
- [Pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) - collects drops for you.
- The guaranteed drop after the fifth kill means that on average something drops roughly every third kill.

## Known inconsistencies

- **The "kills without a drop" counter does not reset between floors** - you carry a running streak to the next floor.
- **Coins are rare** - on average roughly 1 coin drops per 12 kills, while shop prices grow with the floor.
- **The Treasure room always has a weapon chest** - never an artefact chest or a locked one.

## Ideas for expansion

- A wider drop pool: a small potion (e.g. 5%), a rare artefact from elite enemies, boss drops of their own outside the chest.
- A "luck" stat or a talent that raises the drop chance.
- Balancing the coin supply against prices on later floors.
