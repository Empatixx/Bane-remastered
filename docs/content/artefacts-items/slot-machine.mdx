If you have the **Gamble Machine** talent, a slot machine stands at the top right of every shop. Without the talent it is not in the shop. For 5 coins you try your luck - you can win coins, a burst of items, or a rare prize like an artefact or a gun.

## How it works

- Stand next to the machine and press **E** ("Press E to spin"). You need at least **5 coins**.
- The machine spins for **2 seconds**; during that time you cannot spin again and the price tag is hidden.
- Then the result is shown and the winnings fly out of the machine.

### Results

| Result | Chance | Winnings |
|---|---|---|
| Rare prize | 6.7% | one rare item (see below) |
| Cashout | 6.7% | 10 coins |
| Item burst | 6.7% | 5 regular items (coins / ammo) |
| Triple | 6.7% | 15 coins |
| Pair | 13.3% | 10 coins |
| Loss | 60% | nothing |

Coins and items fly out in a circle around the machine and **disappear after 10 seconds** - do not wait to pick them up.

### Rare prize

| Winnings | Chance within the rare prize |
|---|---|
| small potion (half health, half armor) | 50% |
| a gun you do not have yet (if none remain, nothing) | 10% |
| an artefact other than the one you hold | 10% |
| universal ammo | 10% |
| large health potion | 10% |
| large armor potion | 10% |

### Is it worth it?

For 5 coins the machine returns on average **3 coins**, plus roughly a third of an item from the burst and, in 6.7% of cases, a rare prize. So it loses coins - it makes sense mainly as a source of potions and a chance at an artefact (roughly 1 in 150 per spin).

## Tips and connections

- [Shop](/docs/artefacts-items/shop) - the machine stands in the same room and shares your coins with it. Buy what you need first, then you can spin with the rest.
- [Perks](/docs/artefacts-items/perks) - the **Gamble Machine** talent adds the machine to the shop; without it, it is not there.
- [Guns](/docs/guns), [Artefacts](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts) - the rare prizes.

## Known inconsistencies

- **Cashout pays the same as Pair** (10 coins), although the name promises a jackpot.
- **The item burst ignores the Pistol Only challenge** - shotgun ammo can drop, which you cannot use.
- **The rare gun from the machine does not disappear, but the rare potions do** (after 10 s).
- You can spin again right away with no limit.

## Ideas for expansion

- Cashout as a real jackpot, e.g. 25 coins.
- Special machines (more expensive spins, better prizes) on later floors.
