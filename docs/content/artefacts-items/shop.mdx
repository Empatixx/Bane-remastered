---
title: Shop and Sacrifice
description: A shop room with three tables, prices that grow with the floor, discounts from the Discount talent, and a pentagram where you pay with your own health.
---

On every floor you find one shop where you buy a potion, ammo, or a new gun or artefact for coins. Some floors also hide a **Sacrifice** room with a pentagram, where you pay not with coins but with your own blood.

## How it works

### Shop room

In the middle of the upper part stands the merchant, in the middle of the room are **three tables with goods**, at the bottom are two breakable flowerpots and - only with the **Gamble Machine** talent - the [slot machine](/docs/artefacts-items/slot-machine) at the top right. The offer is generated once when the floor is created and does not change.

| Table | Category | Goods | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| left | Defense | large potion - half health, half armor (+4) | x1.5 |
| middle | Ammo | universal ammo (+50% reserve of all guns) | x1 |
| right | Attack | 50%: a gun you do not have yet (if any remain), otherwise an artefact other than the one you hold | x2 |

### Prices

The base price is a random 1-4 coins times the floor number, multiplied by the table coefficient (rounded down). So prices grow with each floor:

| Floor | Ammo | Defense | Attack |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-4 | 1, 3, 4, 6 | 2, 4, 6, 8 |
| 2 | 2-8 | 3-12 | 4-16 |
| 3 | 3-12 | 4, 9, 13, 18 | 6-24 |
| 4 | 4-16 | 6-24 | 8-32 |
| 5 | 5-20 | 7, 15, 22, 30 | 10-40 |

### Discounts (Discount talent)

With the **Discount** talent ([Perks](/docs/artefacts-items/perks)), every shop has a chance of a discount on **one random table**:

| Result | Label | New price | Chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| small discount | -25% | 75% of original | 23% |
| medium discount | -50% | 50% of original | 5% |
| large discount | -75% | 25% of original | 5% |
| no discount | - | 100% | 67% |

The price after a discount is always at least 1 coin. Discounted goods have a green price tag and a label above it showing how much is taken off the price (e.g. "-75%").

### Buying

- Stand next to the goods and press **E** ("Press E to buy"). If you have enough coins, you pay and the merchant thanks you: "Enjoy your purchase!"
- A potion or ammo is used right away, if possible. Otherwise the goods stay on the ground as a regular pickup and you collect them later by touch.
- A gun or artefact is put straight into your gear; whatever it replaces drops to the ground.
- The merchant is silent: he says nothing on entry or after a purchase.
- Goods in the shop never disappear.

### Sacrifice (pentagram)

- A Sacrifice room appears on roughly every second floor (0 or 1). It has an angel statue, five candles, books, chains and a **pentagram** in the middle.
- On the pentagram lies one item of a random category (defense, ammo or attack - each with a one-third chance), chosen the same way as on the tables.
- Price: **1-3 health**. It is paid from health, not from armor.
- You can buy only when you have **more** health than the price - you cannot kill yourself with the sacrifice.
- After the sacrifice a ritual sound plays and **all candles in the room go out**.

## Contents

| Place | Goods | Currency | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defense table | large health / armor potion | coins | 1 |
| Ammo table | universal ammo | coins | 1 |
| Attack table | gun or artefact | coins | 1 |
| Pentagram | anything from the above | 1-3 health | 0-1 |
| Slot machine | gambling | 5 coins | 1 (only with the Gamble Machine talent) |

## Tips and connections

- [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon) - the shop is on every floor, except in the **Merchant's Strike** challenge, where it is not generated at all. Sacrifice does not depend on the shop.
- [Guns](/docs/guns) - a gun from the shop already has the gun upgrades applied. In the Pistol Only challenge guns are not offered, so the Attack table always has an artefact.
- [Artefacts](/docs/artefacts-items/artefacts) - the Attack table is the most reliable source of artefacts.
- The pentagram pays off when you have full health and a lot of armor - you then refill health with a potion or Blood Chalice.
- Do not buy a potion at full health - you pay the coins and the potion just stays on the ground.

## Known inconsistencies

- **Buying unusable goods** (a potion at full health) takes the coins without a warning.
- **The merchant knows only two sentences** - the planned cycle of lines by number of visits is not in the game yet. The altar in the Sacrifice room does not speak at all.
- The original version of the game had 3-5 items in the shop and a different price calculation; the current version always has 3 tables.

## Ideas for expansion

- Re-rolling the offer for coins.
- A voice for the altar - according to the story it "speaks in capital letters".
- A warning or a blocked purchase when you cannot use the goods right away.
- More tables or categories of goods (e.g. artefacts only, potions only).
