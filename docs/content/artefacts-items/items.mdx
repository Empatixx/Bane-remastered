---
title: Items and pickups
description: Coins, ammo boxes and potions - what they do, when you can pick them up and how long they stay on the ground.
---

Pickups are small items that drop in the dungeon from enemies, crates and chests, or can be bought. They are collected automatically - just step on them.

## How it works

### Collecting

- You pick up a pickup by touching it. If you have the [Pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) perk, the wolf collects them for you too.
- The game does not let you pick up an item you cannot use - it stays on the ground until you need it (or until it disappears):

| Item | When you can pick it up |
|---|---|
| Coin | always |
| Ammo | you have a gun that takes this ammo type and its reserve is not full |
| Health potion | your health is not full |
| Armor potion | your armor is not full |

- Goods displayed in the shop are not picked up by touch - you have to buy them.
- Every collected item counts toward the run statistics (items collected, gold earned).

### Effects

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| Coin | +1 coin |
| Ammo | refills part of the maximum reserve of each of your guns that takes the type (up to full) |
| Small potion | +1 health or armor |
| Large potion | +4 health or armor |

### Ammo

For ammo, guns are split into two groups: **Shotgun** takes shotgun ammo, **all other guns** take pistol ammo.

| Box | Which guns it helps | Refills from max reserve |
|---|---|---|
| Pistol | all except Shotgun | 10% |
| SMG | all except Shotgun | 10% |
| Shotgun | Shotgun only | 25% |
| Universal | all guns | 50% |

The refill is calculated from the maximum reserve of each gun separately (with the Additional Ammo talent it is 10% bigger), and **all** suitable guns you carry get it at once.

In the **Pistol Only** challenge, a random ammo drop you cannot use is automatically changed to pistol ammo.

### How long items stay

- Items from enemies, crates, the slot machine and the Midas Coin artefact **disappear after 10 seconds**. They blink for the last 2 seconds as a warning.
- Items from chests and goods in the shop **never disappear**.
- Everything lying on the ground (pickups, guns, artefacts) hovers gently so it is easy to see.
- Items shot out of a chest, the slot machine or Midas Coin fly out to the sides and slowly stop.

## Contents

| Item | Look | Value | Drops randomly? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coin | animated gold coin | +1 coin | yes |
| Pistol ammo | small box | 10% of reserve | yes |
| SMG ammo | same box as pistol | 10% of reserve | yes |
| Shotgun ammo | box with shells | 25% of reserve | yes |
| Rifle ammo | box with long rounds | 25% of reserve (Sniper) | only as a replacement, see below |
| Rocket ammo | box with rockets | 34% of reserve (RPG) | only as a replacement, see below |
| Battery cell | battery | 25% of the battery (energy guns) | only as a replacement, see below |
| Universal ammo | large box | 50% of reserve | no (shop, slot machine) |
| Small health potion | small bottle | +1 health | no (slot machine) |
| Small armor potion | small bottle | +1 armor | no (slot machine) |
| Large health potion | large bottle | +4 health | no (shop, slot machine) |
| Large armor potion | large bottle | +4 armor | no (shop, slot machine) |

A random drop (from enemies, crates, chests) chooses evenly from four items: coin, pistol, shotgun and SMG ammo. If the rolled ammo is useless to the guns you carry, it changes into the box the neediest gun takes - so rifle ammo, rockets and battery cells drop only when you carry a Sniper, an RPG or an energy gun. For where things drop, see [Drops](/docs/artefacts-items/drops).

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - you start with 10 health and 5 armor; the Hero's Heart, Titan's Heart and Improved Armor talents raise the maximums.
- [Guns](/docs/guns) - the reserve size of each gun.
- [Pet](/docs/artefacts-items/pet) - the wolf runs to pickups on its own and collects them for you.
- Leave a potion you do not need right now - it will not disappear from a chest and you can come back for it after the fight.

## Known inconsistencies

- **SMG ammo is the same as pistol ammo** - same effect, same look, taken by the same group of guns. In practice, pistol ammo drops twice as often as the others.
- **"Pistol" ammo also refills the Crossbow, Minigun and Flamethrower** - they share the pistol ammo group. The Sniper, the RPG, the Shotgun and the energy guns have boxes of their own.
- **Potions never drop from enemies or crates.**

## Ideas for expansion

- Own ammo for the Crossbow and the Flamethrower, so that the pistol box is not shared with so many guns.
- A bigger coin or a bag of coins - a green coin variant already exists in the art, but it is not used in the game.
- A magnet or pickup attraction - today you have to walk up to every coin.
- A rare small potion drop from enemies.
