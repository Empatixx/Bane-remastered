description: Barrels and flowerpots, chests, gates, the ladder, the shop, the slot machine, the altar and the lobby furnishings - what they do and what drops from them.

The dungeon is not just scenery - a lot of things can be broken, opened or used. Most objects
are used with the **E** key: when the player stands next to an object, a prompt appears ("Press E to ...").
Chests open on a simple touch, and barrels and flowerpots are broken by shooting.

## How it works

- **Breakable objects** (barrels, flowerpots) have 4 health. Player bullets and explosions break them;
  traps and enemies do not damage them. They can be pushed - the player can shove them around the room.
- Every broken object drops **1 regular item** (coins or ammo, preferably the kind
  the player actually uses). The item disappears after 10 s.
- **Chests** open when the player walks into them: they spill 5 regular items in a circle
  and, on top of that, a weapon or an artefact. Items from chests **do not disappear**.

## Contents

### Breakable objects

| Object | Where | Effect when broken |
|---|---|---|
| Flowerpot | combat rooms depending on the theme, 2 in the shop | – |
| Barrel | combat rooms depending on the theme | – |
| Explosive barrel | combat rooms depending on the theme | an explosion for 3 damage - hurts the player, enemies and other barrels (**chain reactions**) |
| Poison barrel | combat rooms depending on the theme | a poison puddle for 8 s |
| Frost barrel | combat rooms depending on the theme | freezes the surroundings (enemies 4 s, boss 2 s, player 1.5 s) |

All of them have 4 health and drop 1 regular item. The detailed effect numbers are in
[Traps and hazards](/docs/dungeon/hazards), and how many barrels each theme has is in
[Floors and themes](/docs/dungeon/themes).

### Tables and carpets

- In about 2 of 5 combat rooms there is one, sometimes two tables (2 tiles long),
  either across the room or turned along it. A standing table is in everyone's way, but bullets from both
  sides fly over it.
- When the player **bumps into** a table (walking or rolling), the table **flips** to the opposite side:
  a table across the room falls up or down, a turned table falls left or right. A bump from the end the
  table cannot fall toward flips it away from the player along its axis. Even pushing against a table the
  player is already leaning on is enough. The top ends up facing away from the player and the legs point at them, as when a table
  is tipped over to make cover. A flipped table is cover: it stops enemy
  bullets, but player bullets keep flying over it. It can be flipped only once.
- A table has **6 hits**. They are taken off by enemy bullets (only for a flipped table, bullets pass over standing ones)
  and by enemies that lean on it or push into it, standing or flipped: each such
  enemy takes off a hit roughly once every half second. Then the table bursts into pieces (a short
  animation like a barrel or a pot) and leaves scrap on the floor that no longer blocks anyone.
- Each theme has its own table: Stone steel with rust, Sewer verdigris with brass rivets and slime,
  Moss planks overgrown with moss, Spider dark wood with cobwebs, Ice frosted steel, Lava
  sooty iron with smoldering seams and Temple red wood with gold studs.
- In about a third of combat rooms a carpet lies on the floor. It is not a rectangle but a shape: an oval
  6-10 x 5-7 tiles broken up by noise into lobes and bays, like a puddle. It keeps at least 2 tiles away from water, lava and all
  other liquids. Then it is smoothed: no strips
  one tile wide (every tile is in some 2 x 2 square), one piece and no holes.
  The border runs around the whole shape and around inner corners, and the tiles are a set of 47 "blob" pieces
  (`tools/make_carpets.py`, the same order as for puddles). Each theme has
  its own: Stone red with a gold border, Sewer verdigris green with brass, Moss a straw mat
  with a green pattern, Spider black-purple with silver thread, Ice dark blue with frost, Lava
  black with a glowing pattern and Temple royal blue with gold. In the shop and the treasure room it is always
  red. Rails avoid both tables and carpets.

### Cart on rails

- In about every third or fourth combat room, rails (8-20 tiles) run across free floor,
  with a buffer at each end, and a mine cart stands on them. The track is straight, or turns once or
  twice at a right angle (an L or U shape); the cart goes through a turn along an arc and rotates
  to match the direction of travel. The sleepers, rails and cart have their own look in each theme, and the cart has its own
  cargo: Stone coal, Sewer sludge, Moss mushrooms, Spider a cocoon, Ice ice, Lava glowing ore, Temple
  gold.
- When the player bumps into the cart or shoots it along the rails, it sets off in the opposite direction (about 10 tiles per
  second) and always rolls all the way to the buffer at the end, where it stops. The next bump or
  a shot from the other side sends it back. Sparks fly behind its wheels.
- A moving cart runs over enemies for **20 damage** (each one once per pass). It pushes the player
  and enemies out of the way, and stops bullets.
- Only the player can set it moving - enemies can bump into it, but cannot move it.

### Chests

| Chest | Where | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon | Treasure room (in the middle), reward for Ambush | 5 regular items + a random weapon the player does not have |
| Artefact | after defeating the boss | 5 regular items + an artefact the player does not have |

If the player already owns all weapons (or a challenge limits their gear), the weapon chest
gives only 5 items.

### Gates

- They drop over all the doors of a combat room as soon as the player enters it, and act as a
  wall. When the room is cleared, they rise with an animation.

### Ladder

- It appears after defeating the boss on floors 1-4, a little left of the center of the arena. Its look depends on the theme.
- "Press E to enter next floor" - go down to the next floor.

### Shop

- The **merchant** stands silently behind the counter between two shelves of goods. Crates and barrels lie in the corners, and two brass lamps
  glow between the tables. The counter, shelves, crates and lamps are solid and cannot be walked through.
- **3 tables** under a velvet cloth with goods, with a price tag above each (on sale also with a label showing how much is taken off, e.g. "−75%"). Buying with the E key
  takes the coins; a potion or ammo is used right away, and a weapon or artefact is picked up right away.
  For goods and prices see [Room types](/docs/dungeon/rooms).
- **Slot machine** (only with the *Gamble Machine* talent): "Press E to spin", one spin
  costs **5 coins** and takes 2 s. While spinning the lever is pulled down and lights run around the sign.
  After a win the lights flash and coins pour into the tray, and after a loss the lights dim for a moment.

| Result | Chance | Prize |
|---|---|---|
| Rare item | 6.7% | 50%: a small health or armor potion; otherwise one of: a weapon, an artefact, universal ammo, a large health potion, a large armor potion |
| Cashout | 6.7% | 10 coins |
| Item shower | 6.7% | 5 regular items |
| Triple | 6.7% | 15 coins |
| Double | 13.3% | 10 coins |
| Lose | 60% | nothing |

Items from the slot machine disappear after 10 s. On average it returns roughly 3 out of every 5 coins put in
(not counting items) - it does not pay off in pure coins, you play for the chance at rare things.

### Altar (Sacrifice)

- A pentagram with one item, which costs **1-3 lives**. The altar will not kill you - you can buy
  only with more lives than the price.
- Around it are an angel statue, 5 candelabras with red light, books and chains. After the sacrifice the candles go out.

### Portal (lobby)

- A glowing purple portal in the lobby: "Press E to enter the portal" starts a new run.
- With an active challenge, the portal and its light take on the color of the challenge.
- There is no portal in the dungeon - the player returns from a run to the lobby only by death or victory.

### Other objects in the lobby

| Object | What it does | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Scientist | talent menu | "Press E to upgrade" |
| Notice board | missions and challenges | "Press E to read the board" |
| Weapon table | weapon upgrades | "Press E to upgrade weapons" |

## Tips and connections

- [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items) - what exactly drops from chests, barrels and the slot machine.
- [Guns](/docs/guns) - weapon chests offer only weapons you do not have yet.
- [Progression](/docs/progression) - the *Gamble Machine* talent (the slot machine in the shop),
  *Discount*, challenge colors.
- Barrels can be pushed toward enemies - an explosive barrel next to a group of enemies is a cheap grenade.
- Barrels and flowerpots are a reliable source of ammo when you run low: something always drops from them.

## Known inconsistencies

- "Cashout" pays the same as "Double" (10 coins), even though by its name it should be a jackpot.
- Traps do not damage barrels and enemies do not break them - only the player or an explosion breaks them.

## Ideas for expansion

- Keys and locked chests, a mimic chest as a trap in the Treasure room.
- Levers, buttons, secret doors, healing fountains, more NPCs in the dungeon besides the merchant.
- A new breakable object, e.g. a crate with coins.
- Use the lobby furnishings (weapon and armor racks, bookcases, a map table, crates, stones)
  as decor on Stone and Temple floors.
