---
title: Surfaces and liquids
description: Ice, lava, water, swamp, sand and blood - where they appear, what they look like and how they change the player's movement.
---

Six themes have their own **liquid** (or loose surface) on the floor: Ice has ice, Lava has lava, Sewer
water, Moss swamp, Temple sand and Stone blood. In every Normal and Ambush room of such a floor, several irregular
puddles spread out. They change the player's movement - or burn them outright.

## How it works

- Puddles appear only in combat rooms (Normal, Ambush), never in the Start, shop,
  altar, treasure room or boss arena.
- They have irregular, "organic" shapes. They never cover doors (they stay at least 3 tiles away from
  them), enemy spawn spots, or decor and traps.
- **Lava never splits a room** - a dry path always remains to every part of the room.
- Whether a liquid affects the player is decided by the player's center; ripples in water come from the feet.
- When walking through a liquid, particles fly off the feet depending on the surface: water drops and foam, clumps of mud,
  soot and sparks from lava, ice shards, grains of sand, drops of blood. They fly in an arc and fall back
  (sparks and shards just fade out). They are only decoration.
- Puddles have a shore drawn around them and a shadow under the wall. Lava and water are animated, and lava
  also glows orange.
- **On their own** (`stir_surfaces` in `systems/surfaces.rs`), puddles in view make tiny
  particles even without the player: black embers and white sparks rise from lava, glints shimmer
  on water and ice, bubbles rise and pop in swamp and blood, and grains blow across sand. Each
  tile produces roughly one every 2-8 s.

## Stats

| Surface | Theme | How much of the room it covers | Puddles | Effect on the player |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ice** | Ice | 22-40% | 1-5 | slides - speeds up and brakes slowly |
| **Lava** | Lava | 8-14% | 1-3 | 2 damage straight to health, at most 2 per second; does not burn during a dodge |
| **Water** | Sewer | 16-30% | 1-4 | speed to 55%; makes ripples when walking |
| **Swamp** | Moss | 14-26% | 1-4 | speed to 45%; makes muddy rings when walking |
| **Blood** | Stone | 6-14% | 1-4 | speed to 70% (sticky); makes red rings when walking |
| **Sand** | Temple | 10-20% | 1-4 | you sink: the slowdown grows from 1% to 80% over 3 s in the sand; after climbing out it fades 4x faster |

The player normally covers about 6.4 tiles per second, about 3.5 in water and about 2.9 in swamp.
The slowdown multiplies with other speed bonuses and penalties. Water and swamp do not stack with the cobweb
slowdown - only one of them applies. In sand the slowdown depends on how
long the player has been in it (standing and walking are the same) - a short dash across barely slows you,
and anyone who lingers in the sand nearly stops. A dodge goes through sand at full speed.

### Sewer furnishings

Puddles on a Sewer floor are not just water:

- **Drain pipes:** in 1-2 places on a wall right above the water there is a round pipe in the colors of the wall.
  Water flows from it, splashes into the puddle and makes rings on the surface.
- **Walkways:** in about 7 of 10 rooms with water, a wooden walkway with ropes runs across a straight stretch of water
  (2-6 tiles), across or vertically. Its ends rest on the edge of the shore. Water does not slow you on the walkway.
- **Trash:** 1-2 pieces of trash float on larger puddles (at least 6 tiles): a plank, a crate,
  a bottle, a boot and occasionally a rat on a plank. They bob, turn away from the shore and do not float under a walkway.
  They are only decoration and block nothing.
- **Floating barrels:** in about a third of rooms with water, a barrel slowly drifts along the surface, plain,
  explosive or poison. It blocks like any barrel, a bullet nudges it in the direction it flies, and then
  it slows back down to drifting. It bounces off the shore, and if pushed onto dry land it stays put.

### Lava furnishings

A Lava floor has the same as the sewer, just in its own colors and without barrels:

- **Lavafalls:** 1-2 glowing cracks in a wall right above the lava. Lava runs down from them, splashes into
  a pool, sparks, and both glow.
- **Stone paths:** in about 7 of 10 rooms with lava, a row of flat basalt stones runs across a straight stretch of lava
  (2-6 tiles), with lava glowing between them. Lava does not burn on the stones.
- **Crusts:** 1-2 pieces of hardened black rock with glowing seams float on larger pools
  (at least 6 tiles), only for decoration.

### Swamp furnishings

Swamp on a Moss floor has the same in its own style, without barrels:

- **Root trickles:** murky water runs down from 1-2 gaps between roots in a wall above the swamp.
- **Fallen logs:** in about 7 of 10 rooms with swamp, a moss-covered log lies across a straight stretch (2-6 tiles).
  The swamp does not slow you on the log.
- **Lily pads and leaves:** lily pads (some in bloom) float on larger swamps, along with fallen leaves
  and occasionally a frog on a lily pad that jumps about every 3 s and makes a ring on the surface.

### Ice furnishings

Ice on an Ice floor has its own version, without bridges (you walk on the ice as it is):

- **Frozen waterfalls:** from 1-2 cracks with icicles in a wall above the ice hangs a frozen cascade.
  A glint slides down it, and at the bottom is an icy pile that sparkles.
- **Under the ice:** in larger ice patches you can see 1-2 frozen things: a fish, a skeleton hand,
  a sword or a few coins. Only decoration, they cannot be picked up.
- **Cracks and drifts:** 1-2 cracks in the ice and up to 3 snow drifts by the walls.

### Blood

Blood puddles on a Stone floor are dark, slowly ripple and a glint passes over them. When walking they make
dark red rings.

### Sand furnishings

Sand in Temple has only trickles: from 1-2 cracks in the face of a wall above the sand, a stream of sand falls
straight down and vanishes into the sand, with only a few grains bouncing off. Nothing floats on it and no bridges lead across it.

The Hellhound boss's magma looks the same as lava, but works differently - see
[Traps and hazards](/docs/dungeon/hazards).

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - a dodge across lava is safe, and across ice it is the best way to quickly
  change direction.
- [Floors and themes](/docs/dungeon/themes) - which floor has which liquid.
- [Decor](/docs/dungeon/decor) - liquids spread only after the decor is placed and avoid it.
- [Lighting](/docs/dungeon/lighting) - lava is a light source.
- On an Ice floor the ice covers up to 40% of the room - expect stopping to take a while, and enemies
  that attack on contact can easily catch you.

## Known inconsistencies

- Liquids affect **only the player** - enemies walk through water, swamp, ice and lava without
  penalty and do not avoid them.
- Water and swamp differ by only 10% of slowdown.

## Ideas for expansion

- Interaction between elements: a frost barrel freezes water into ice, an explosion dries up swamp, lava sets things on fire.
- Swamp could "stick" or poison, not just slow more.
- A liquid for Spider (e.g. cobweb mush).
- The player could visibly sink into sand (the sprite goes down).
- Puddles in boss arenas.
