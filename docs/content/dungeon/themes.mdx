Every floor has one of seven **themes** (biomes): Spider, Stone, Sewer, Moss, Ice, Lava
and Temple. The theme changes the look of the walls and floor, the color of the darkness around the map, the look of the ladder, the liquid
on the floor, the mix of traps, barrels and decor, and also which enemies and bosses can
appear.

## How it works

### Which floor gets which theme

- At the start of a run the themes are **shuffled**, and floors 1-5 get five of the seven themes. No
  theme is tied to a specific floor - even Spider can come as late as floor 5.
- Within one run no theme **repeats**, and for the same seed the themes are always the same.

### What floors look like

- Walls and floors are built from tiles that adapt to their surroundings - corners, wall edges,
  the floor right under a wall and next to it all have their own artwork.
- **Stone** uses a simpler set of tiles with fewer variants - the floor is
  uniform, with only an occasional decorated tile.
- The other themes have a richer set: roughly a third of the floor is "decorated" (cracks, pebbles,
  themed details), and the rest is clean areas that blend into continuous patches instead of a
  checkerboard pattern. Edges along the walls have more variants.
- Around the map the darkness is in the theme's color, and it shows through wherever there is only deep wall.
- The look of every tile is determined by its position only - the same spot always looks the same for the same seed.

## Contents

### Theme overview

| Theme | Where | Darkness color | Liquid | Typical traps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spider | any floor | almost black with a purple tint | – | cobweb patches |
| Stone | any floor | dark burgundy | blood | spikes, flamethrower, explosive barrels, pendulum |
| Sewer | any floor | dark teal | water | poison barrels |
| Moss | any floor | dark olive | swamp | spikes |
| Ice | any floor | dark blue | ice | frost barrels |
| Lava | any floor | dark red | lava | 3 flamethrowers, explosive barrels |
| Temple | any floor | dark brown | sand | the most spikes, arrow traps, flamethrowers |

Each theme also has its own look of the ladder that appears after the boss is defeated.

### Bosses by theme

Each theme has **exactly one boss of its own**. The boss travels with its theme, so whichever
floor the theme lands on, its boss waits there.

| Theme | Boss |
|---|---|
| Spider | Spider king |
| Stone | Stone golem |
| Sewer | Sewer ogre |
| Moss | King slime |
| Ice | Argus |
| Lava | Hellhound |
| Temple | Mimic |

Spider enemies (Red, Green and Purple spiderling, Bloated spider, Stilt spider) appear
only in the Spider theme - on any floor. The Ice theme has its own Frost rat, Icicle bat and Snow slime, and the Lava theme its own Ember rat, Flame bat, Magma slime and Lava worm - see [Ice and Lava creatures](/docs/enemies/ice-lava). In these two themes the plain Rat, Bat and Slime do not appear. Details in [Enemies](/docs/enemies).

### Character of the themes

- **Spider** - cobwebs everywhere (5 on walls, 1-3 sticky patches on the floor, cocoons), poison
  barrels, no liquid. The darkest floor.
- **Stone** - a classic dungeon with a balanced mix of everything and simpler tile art.
- **Sewer** - water slows you down, lots of poison barrels, no flamethrowers.
- **Moss** - swamp (the strongest slowdown), glowing mushrooms, lots of spikes and flowerpots.
- **Ice** - slippery ice, frost barrels, icicles, crystals; chilling Frost rats and Snow slimes and Icicle bats that drop icicles.
- **Lava** - lava burns (2 damage straight to health), 3 flamethrowers per room, up to 4 explosive
  barrels, no poison or frost barrels; Ember rats, Flame bats, Magma slimes and Lava worms; the Hellhound boss floods the arena with magma.
- **Temple** - the most spikes, arrow traps in the walls, flamethrowers, sand that the player sinks into.

  on Spider, Sewer, Moss and Ice floors you will find none.

## Known inconsistencies

- On a Spider floor no explosive, plain or frost barrel ever appears, nor a flowerpot;
  Sewer, Moss and Ice lack explosive barrels, and Ice, Lava and Temple lack plain barrels. It looks
  more like an unintended result of the settings than a design choice.
- Ice patches are ready in the game, but they do not appear in any theme. They would naturally
  belong in Ice.
- According to the story, floor 5 should always be Temple with the final boss The Heart - in the game the theme
  of the fifth floor is random.

## Ideas for expansion

- The theme could change the music and the overall brightness (darker Spider and Lava, a lighter bluish Ice)
  (the game already has particles and fog by theme - see [Lighting](/docs/dungeon/lighting)).
- Temple has no unique enemies.
- Themed decor could come alive: cocoons hatch spiders, icicles fall.
- A new theme, e.g. Crypt, with its own liquid and boss.
