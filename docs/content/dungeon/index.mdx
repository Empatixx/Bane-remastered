title: Dungeon and world
description: What a Bane floor looks like and how it is built - rooms, corridors, themes, traps and the way to the boss.

Every run in Bane leads through **5 floors** of the dungeon. Each floor is generated fresh:
a network of rooms connected by short straight corridors. A floor has its own **theme** (biome). It sets
the look of the walls and floor, the color of the darkness around the map, the liquid on the floor, the kinds of traps, the decor,
and which enemies and which boss appear there. A boss waits at the end of every floor.
After the player defeats it, they go down a ladder to the next floor. After the boss on floor 5, the run ends
in victory.

## How it works

### Floor layout

The makeup of a floor is **rolled again for every floor**, so no two floors are the same size
or equally rich. A floor has **8-15 rooms** (one fewer with the *Merchant's Strike* challenge):

| Room | Count | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Start | 1 | the player begins here |
| Normal | 4-7 | forms the backbone of the floor, branches out |
| Shop | 1 | dead-end branch (none with the *Merchant's Strike* challenge) |
| Treasure | 0-2 | dead-end branch - a floor may have no treasure room, or it may have two |
| Sacrifice | 0-1 (50%) | dead-end branch |
| Ambush | 1-2 | dead-end branch |
| Boss | 1 | always as far from the start as possible |

- The Start and Normal rooms form a branching backbone. The generator prefers
  "lonelier" rooms, so the floor spreads out to the sides and does not merge into one block.
- All special rooms (Shop, Treasure, Sacrifice, Ambush, Boss) are **dead ends**
  attached to some Normal room. The path never continues through them.
- The Boss room is attached to one of the Normal rooms that are farthest from the start.
- A floor has no loops: there is exactly one path to every room. To go back, the player has to
  return through the same corridor.
- Corridors are always straight, 2 tiles wide and 4 tiles long. The doors of rooms that face each other
  lie on one axis.

Example floor layout: from the Start a corridor leads to a Normal room, where the path branches
up (Normal -> Shop, a branch to Treasure) and down (Normal -> Sacrifice). The main branch
continues through two more Normal rooms, one of which has a branch to Ambush, all the way to the Boss
room at the very end.

### Moving through the floor

- When the player steps further into a combat room (at least 2 tiles from the edge), **gates**
  fall over all the doors and enemies appear. The gates rise when the last of them falls.
- Start, Shop, Treasure and Sacrifice are peaceful - the gates do not close in them.
- Ambush has 3 waves, Normal has one, and the Boss room has only the boss.
- Inside a room the camera stays within its bounds. In a corridor it glides smoothly into the next room.
- After the boss, a chest with an artefact appears and (on floors 1-4) a ladder to the next floor.
  The player carries health, armor, coins, weapons and the artefact into the new floor.

### Randomness and seed

Every run has its own **seed**. The floor layout, the choice of rooms, decor, traps and liquids are
always the same for the same seed. Which enemy types land in which room also depends on the
order in which the player opens the rooms, though. The look of individual tiles and floor shades is determined
by position only - the same spot always looks the same.

## Stats

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Floors per run | 5 |
| Rooms per floor | 8-15 (random for each floor) |
| Room variants | 23 (15 Normal, 3 Ambush, one each of Start, Shop, Treasure, Sacrifice, Boss) |
| Themes | 7 (Stone, Sewer, Moss, Spider, Ice, Lava, Temple) |
| Corridor | 4 tiles long, 2 wide |
| Enemies per room | 5-9 per wave depending on room size |

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - who appears depends on the floor number and its theme;
  the Hellhound spreads magma over the boss arena.
- [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items) - barrels, flowerpots and chests drop coins
  and ammo; the *Reveal Map* artefact reveals the whole floor map.
- [Player](/docs/player) - liquids change movement (ice slides, water and swamp slow you down, lava burns).
- [Progression](/docs/progression) - the talents *Trap Master*, *Discount*, *Gamble Machine*,
  *Armor Regeneration*; the *Merchant's Strike* challenge removes shops.
- Special rooms are always dead ends. When you find a branch off a Normal room,
  it almost certainly leads to a reward, a shop or an ambush.

## Known inconsistencies

- According to the story, floor 5 should always be a temple (Temple) with the final boss The Heart. In the game the
  theme of the fifth floor is random and the boss on floor 5 is a regular boss.
- On floors 2 and up, the shop and the altar are filled before the game "gives back" the player's gear from the
  previous floor. Because of this they may offer a weapon the player already has.
- You cannot return from the dungeon to the lobby except by death or victory.

## Ideas for expansion

- A floor could grow with depth - today its size is purely random regardless of the floor number.
- Loops and shortcuts in the layout, curved corridors or corridors with their own content.
- A reward after clearing a room (a choice of three "boons"), secret rooms, rooms with a curse
  or elite enemies.
- A fixed finale: floor 5 is always a Temple with the boss The Heart.
