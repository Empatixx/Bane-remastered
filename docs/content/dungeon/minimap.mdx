Bane has two floor maps: a small **minimap** at the top right that is always visible, and a large
**map** across the whole screen on the **Tab** key. Both show rooms as icons by
type, connected by corridors, and they reveal as the player explores the floor.

## How it works

- Every room is one icon on the map, by type. On its edges the passages are marked
  where a corridor leads.
- A room is **visited** as soon as the player steps into it. The current room is the one the player
  last stood in - it does not change in a corridor.
- The view glides smoothly to the current room.
- Unvisited rooms are covered by a moving **fog**.
- The **Reveal Map** artefact marks all rooms of the floor as visited.

### Small minimap

- Always visible in the top right corner.
- It shows the current room at full strength, neighboring rooms a little faded, and visited
  rooms one step further only faintly. Unvisited rooms farther than the neighbors are almost
  invisible.

| Distance from the current room | Visited | Unvisited |
|---|---|---|
| current | 100% | – |
| neighbor | 75% | 75% |
| 2 rooms away | 41% | 6% |
| 3 rooms away | almost invisible | – |

### Large map

- **Tab** opens and closes it, **Esc** closes it. The key can be remapped.
- It shows the whole explored part of the floor plus the neighboring rooms the player has not yet
  entered (the "frontier" of exploration).
- The map can be moved by dragging with the mouse; it is centered when opened and when the room changes.
- While the large map is open, the player does not shoot.

| Room | Visibility |
|---|---|
| current | 100% |
| visited | 75% |
| unvisited, but next to an explored one | 50% (in fog) |
| others | hidden |

## Contents

Room icons: **Start**, **Normal**, **Treasure**, **Shop**, **Boss**, **Sacrifice**,
**Ambush** - each type has its own icon. A description of the rooms is in [Room types](/docs/dungeon/rooms).

## Tips and connections

- [Room types](/docs/dungeon/rooms) - what hides behind which icon.
- [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items) - *Reveal Map*.
- [Player](/docs/player) - controls and key remapping.
- Just step into a Normal room next to a branch, and the large map will tell you whether there is a shop,
  treasure room, ambush or boss behind it.

## Known inconsistencies

- The large map shows the type even for unvisited neighboring rooms - so the player sees where the Boss
  or Shop is as soon as they stand next to it. If that is not intended, they could have a neutral icon.
- The small minimap cannot be enlarged, shrunk or turned off in the settings.

## Ideas for expansion

- Content markers on the map: an unopened chest, the ladder, a shop with unbought goods, cleared
  vs. uncleared room.
- A setting for the size and transparency of the minimap.
