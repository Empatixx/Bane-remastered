---
title: Traps and hazards
description: Spikes, flamethrowers, arrow traps, cobwebs, barrel effects, lava and the Hellhound's magma - how much they hurt and how to avoid them.
---

Besides enemies, combat rooms are full of traps. Most of them work in a regular rhythm
- dangerous for a while, then not - and they hurt **both the player and enemies**. Which traps appear on a
floor is decided by its [theme](/docs/dungeon/themes).

## How it works

- A trap hurts only in its active phase (spikes extended, flame blasting). Outside it, you can
  walk across safely.
- After a trap hit, the player loses armor first, then health, and gets **1 s of invulnerability**.
- An enemy hurt by a trap is then resistant to traps for 1 s. Enemies can be lured into traps.
- Traps, barrels and decor never appear closer than 3 tiles to a door or on spots
  where enemies spawn.
- The **Trap Master** talent: at the start of a floor, every trap has roughly a 30% chance of being
  switched off (spikes stay retracted, the flame does not burn).

## Stats

### Traps

| Trap | Where | Cycle | Dangerous | Damage | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Spikes** | floor of Normal rooms, always away from walls | 0.7 s | 0.35 s of the cycle | 1 | 1-4 per room depending on the theme |
| **Flamethrower** | in a wall, blasts downward | 0.7 s | 0.175 s of the cycle | 1 | glows red and flickers with the flame's rhythm |
| **Arrow trap** | in a wall, Temple only | 2.4 s | – | 1 | fires an arrow downward, only when the player is within 10 tiles |
| **Pendulum** | Stone only, at most 1 per floor (on about 7 of 10 floors) | 2.5 s there and back | always | 1 | a blade on a chain swings across the gap between blocks |

**Pendulum** in detail:

- It hangs on a chain from the eye of an iron bracket that clamps planks laid across a 3-6 tile gap
  between two blocks in a combat room, and swings
  from one side of the gap to the other without going into the blocks. It hangs lowest in the middle and rises
  at the edges.
- It hits **only the player** for 1 damage, at most once per pass (then it will not cut for 0.8 s).
  It does no harm during a dodge.
- It is drawn above the player, and its shadow is on the floor beneath it.

**Arrow trap** in detail:

- Traps in one row do not fire all at once - the first arrow flies after 0.8-2.2 s depending on position, then every 2.4 s.
- If the player is farther than 10 tiles, the trap waits.
- The arrow flies straight down at about 8 tiles per second, "sliding out" of a slot in the wall.
- It hits **only the player** (1 damage through armor) and shatters on a wall.

### Slowing patches

| Patch | Where | Size | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cobweb patch** | Spider, 1-3 per room | 2 × 2 tiles | the player moves at half speed; **bullets** (the player's and enemies') quickly slow down in it, and finally get caught and vanish |
| **Ice patch** | does not appear in the game yet | 2 × 2 tiles | the player slides |

The cobweb spun by the Spider king boss (the boss of the Spider theme on any floor) slows the player the same way (to half) and disappears
when it dies.

### Barrel effects

| Barrel | Effect | Range | Numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explosive** | an explosion, screen shake | about 5 × 4 tiles | 3 damage to the player (armor protects), to enemies and to other barrels -> chain explosions |
| **Frost** | freezing | about 8 × 6 tiles | enemies 4 s, boss 2 s, player 1.5 s (not during a dodge); a frozen one cannot walk and does not hurt on touch |
| **Poison** | a poison puddle under the barrel | puddle about 2 × 1.5 tiles | lasts 8 s (fades during the last second); player 1 damage, enemies 1 damage per second; the poisoned glow green |

The Snake enemy also leaves a poison trail behind it (the drops last 5 s and hurt only the player).
Puddles from the player's sources hurt only enemies.

### Lava and magma

| Hazard | Where | Damage | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lava** | puddles on Lava floors | 2, **straight to health** | burns when you stand in the middle of the lava; not during a dodge; at most 2 damage per second |
| **Magma** | the Hellhound boss arena | 2, **straight to health** | see below |

**The Hellhound's magma:**

- 3 s after the fight begins, the hound starts flooding the arena with magma.
- The magma creeps **from the walls toward the center** - it gradually fills a strip up to 3 tiles from the walls.
- A new magma tile is added every 0.45 s, and the enraged hound adds one every 0.28 s.
- Each tile heats up for 1 s (gray -> orange, no damage yet), then glows for 16 s
  and cools for the last 1.2 s.
- It burns when the player's **feet** are on a glowing tile.
- When the boss dies, all the magma disappears.

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player) - a dodge protects against lava, magma and freezing; *Phantom* does not protect
  against traps.
- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - traps hurt enemies too, so it pays to lure them into them.
- [Progression](/docs/progression) - the *Trap Master* talent.
- [Surfaces and liquids](/docs/dungeon/surfaces) - lava, water, swamp, ice.
- Spikes and flamethrowers share the same 0.7 s rhythm - just wait for them to retract and walk through.
- In the Hellhound fight, hold the center of the arena; the longer the fight lasts, the smaller the safe area.
- Cobweb patches swallow enemy bullets too - they can serve as a shield.

## Known inconsistencies

- Ice patches are finished in the game, but they do not appear in any theme.
- Arrow traps do not hurt enemies, and the *Trap Master* talent does not switch them off.
- Lava, water and swamp affect **only the player** - enemies walk through lava unharmed and are not slowed
  in water.
- Lava takes health through armor with no warning - the player gives no sign that they are burning.

## Ideas for expansion

- Icicles in Ice could fall as a trap.
- Pressure plates, trapdoors, rotating flames, traps triggered by the player.
- A new "falling rock" trap for Stone or Temple.
- A visual warning when standing in lava.
