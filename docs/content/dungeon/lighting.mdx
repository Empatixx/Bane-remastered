title: Lighting and atmosphere
description: How the dungeon is lit - torches and other light sources, shadows from walls, the color of darkness by theme, dust and fireflies in the lobby.
---

The dungeon is plunged into dimness. There is a faint warm glow around the player, and rooms are lit by
torches, candelabras, lava, glowing mushrooms or flamethrowers. Lights cast soft shadows from walls,
so pillars and low walls create darker corners.

## How it works

- The whole world has the same base brightness (roughly 60%) in all themes. Lights add color
  and brightness to it and add up with each other.
- **Shadows**: a wall blocks the light that is behind it. The walls themselves are lit from their own side,
  though, and a torch set into a wall shines into the room. The edges of shadows are soft.
- At most 64 lights shine on the screen at once - when there are too many, the ones closest to the
  center of the screen are shown. That is why lava glows only from every fourth tile.
- **The darkness around the map** has the theme's color (burgundy in Stone, blue in Ice, red in Lava...) and shows through
  wherever there is deep wall.
- **Music**: the game soundtrack plays in the dungeon, switches to boss music in the boss room
  and goes back after the boss is defeated. The floor's theme does not change the music.

### Air in the dungeon

Every floor has something in the air based on its theme. Particles and fog stay around the camera,
slowly appearing and fading away, so there are always about the same number on screen.

| Theme | Fog | Particles |
|---|---|---|
| Stone | – | fine dust, visible only in light |
| Temple | – | fine golden dust |
| Moss | green ground fog + wisps overhead | glowing spores rise upward |
| Sewer | teal ground fog | – |
| Spider | – | fine dust |
| Ice | – | snow falls at a slant |
| Lava | – | a spark occasionally flies off a lava puddle and flickers |

- Fog is only on damp floors (Moss, Sewer). **Ground fog** is below the characters and is lit by
  torches. **Wisps overhead** (Moss) are above everything and drift faster than the floor when the camera moves,
  which gives a sense of depth.
- Sparks and spores glow on their own, while dust and snow are visible only where light falls. Sparks
  rise only from lava puddles in view (at most a few per second), and elsewhere on a Lava floor there are none.

### Atmosphere of the lobby and menu

- **Dust**: about 14 fine translucent motes slowly float in view, each lasting 6-12 s.
- **Fireflies**: 4 fireflies circling the portal (in the lobby) or the altar (in the menu) and around the
  middle of the map. They wander along smooth curves, blink yellow-green and **run away from the player**
  when the player gets close.
- The look is the same every time.

## Stats

Range is approximate, in tiles.

| Source | Color | Range | Strength | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Player | warm orange | ~2.5 | weak | always shines around the player |
| Torch | warm yellow | ~1 | medium | 10% of torches are extinguished |
| Candelabra (altar) | red | small | strong | goes out after the sacrifice |
| Glowing mushrooms (Moss) | green | ~1 | weak | |
| Flamethrower | red | ~1 | flickers | lights up with the rhythm of the flame |
| Lava | orange | ~1.25 | medium | every fourth tile glows |
| Magma (Hellhound) | orange | ~1 | grows and fades | gets stronger while heating, weaker while cooling |
| Portal (lobby) | purple, or the challenge color | ~1 | very strong | |
| Firefly (lobby, menu) | yellow-green | ~1.5 | blinks | |

Other lights - explosions, lightning, bullets, the golem, summoned creatures - belong to weapons
and enemies, see [Guns](/docs/guns) and [Enemies](/docs/enemies).

## Tips and connections

- [Decor](/docs/dungeon/decor) - most static lights are torches and mushrooms.

- When paused, the image blurs and darkens; on a hit and at low health the edges of the screen
  change color - see [Player](/docs/player).

## Known inconsistencies

- All themes have the same base brightness - Spider and Lava are not darker and Ice is not
  lighter, even though that would suit the atmosphere.

## Ideas for expansion

- A custom brightness and tint for each theme (darker Spider and Lava, a lighter bluish Ice).
- A "lantern" artefact or talent that enlarges the light around the player.
- A soundscape by theme (dripping water, crackling lava).
