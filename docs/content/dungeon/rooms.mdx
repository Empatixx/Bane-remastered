title: Room types
description: Seven kinds of rooms - what you find in them, how many enemies to expect, how combat behind closed gates works and what the rewards are.

Every floor is made of seven kinds of rooms. Normal and Ambush are combat rooms, Start, Shop,
Treasure and Sacrifice are peaceful, and Boss is the finale of the floor. Every room is built from a hand-drawn
template. Normal rooms have 15 different shapes, Ambush has 3, and the other types have one
each. How many rooms of each kind a floor gets is rolled again for every floor - see
[Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).

## How it works

### Combat behind gates

- A room "comes alive" only when the player gets at least 2 tiles from the edge, so the gates do not
  fall on the player's head in the doorway. On the minimap the room is marked as visited right after entering.
- In a combat room (Normal, Ambush, Boss) **gates** drop over all the doors and
  act as a wall.
- When the last enemy falls, either the next wave comes after **1.5 s** (Ambush only), or the gates
  rise and the room is cleared. Slowing time also slows the pause between waves.
- A cleared room stays empty even if the player returns to it.

### How many enemies

- The number of enemies in one wave grows with the room size: roughly **1 enemy per 100 tiles**
  of area, at least 5 (the cap is 12, but no room reaches it today - the maximum is 9).
- Ambush has **3 waves**, each about 30% smaller than a Normal room of the same size would have
  (at least 4 enemies per wave).
- Enemies appear at preset spots in the room, never closer than 3 tiles
  to a door. Which enemy type spawns where is rolled based on the floor and theme.
- The Boss room contains only the boss, who appears in the middle.

## Contents

### Start
- A peaceful 20 x 16 tile room, with no enemies and no decor.
- Animated **key hints** glow on the floor: Q "Drop gun", E "Interact", F "Use artefact",
  1 "Switch slot 1", 2 "Switch slot 2", WASD "Movement", space "Dodge".
- The player appears on a free spot as close to the center as possible.

### Normal
- 4-7 per floor (rolled for every floor), 15 different shapes. The main source of combat, coins and ammo.
- 1 wave, 5-9 enemies depending on size.
- Furnishings: 2-5 torches (more in larger rooms), 2 banners, cobwebs, bones, spikes
  and other traps, barrels and flowerpots depending on the floor theme; on themed floors also puddles
  of water, swamp, ice or lava.

### Ambush
- 1-2 per floor, always a dead-end branch, 3 large arenas.
- **3 waves** of 5-7 enemies; before each wave the text "wave X/3" is shown.
- Reward: a **weapon chest** as close to the middle of the room as possible.
- Furnishings like Normal, but **without spikes and cobwebs**.

### Treasure
- 0-2 per floor (rolled for every floor - some floors have no treasure room, others have two),
  a dead-end branch, a 20 x 16 room with 4 pillars.
- In the middle, on a carpet, stands a **weapon chest**, with 4 torches around, bricks, 3 cobwebs
  and 3 piles of bones. No traps or enemies.
- The chest opens on touch and spills 5 regular items and a weapon the player does not have yet.

### Shop
- 1 per floor (none with the *Merchant's Strike* challenge), a dead-end branch, a 20 x 16 room.
- The **merchant** says nothing.
- **3 tables** with goods, a **slot machine** (only with the *Gamble Machine* talent), 2 breakable
  flowerpots and a large carpet.

| Table | Goods | Price on floor 1 |
|---|---|---|
| left | large health potion or armor (50/50) | 1-6 coins |
| middle | universal ammo | 1-4 coins |
| right | 50%: a weapon the player does not have; otherwise an artefact | 2-8 coins |

- Prices grow with the floor: base 1-4 coins x floor number x table multiplier (defense 1.5x,
  ammo 1x, offense 2x).
- *Discount* talent: one table may be on sale - 23% chance of -25%, 5% for -50%,
  5% for -75%.
- *Gamble Machine* talent: without it the shop has no slot machine at all.
- A potion or ammo is used right after purchase, and a weapon or artefact is picked up right away.
- For the slot machine and its prizes, see [Objects](/docs/dungeon/objects).

### Sacrifice (altar)
- 0-1 per floor (50%), a dead-end branch, a 20 x 16 room with 4 pillars.
- An **angel statue**, a **pentagram** in the middle, 5 **candelabras** with red light, books and chains.
- One item lies on the pentagram (a potion, ammo, or a weapon or artefact) and it costs
  **1-3 lives**. The altar will not kill you - you can buy only if you have more lives than the price.
- After the sacrifice, all the candles go out.

### Boss
- 1 per floor, at the end of the longest branch of the floor. An empty 20 x 16 arena with no cover.
- On entry the music changes and the boss health bar with its name appears.
- After victory: screen shake, an **artefact chest** a little above the middle and, on floors 1-4, a
  **ladder** to the next floor. On floor 5 the victory screen follows.
- The Hellhound floods the arena with **magma** during the fight (see [Traps](/docs/dungeon/hazards)).

Rules that all rooms follow: doors are always in the middle of each wall
(2 tiles wide, open only where a corridor leads), there is no passage only
1 tile wide, and Normal rooms have at least 300 tiles of area.

### Vestibules

Corridors between rooms are at least 7 tiles long. On about 2 of 5 straight corridors the middle
widens into a small vestibule: 3-5 tiles along the corridor and 4 or 6 across, with a tile
of plain corridor at each end. The way through stays clear, and the furnishings stand in side
alcoves: 1-2 breakable flowerpots or barrels (they drop 1 regular item, as elsewhere),
sometimes a bit of biome (Moss mushrooms, a Spider cocoon, Ice crystals, bones elsewhere), occasionally a torch
on the wall or a cobweb in the corner. In a vestibule the camera shows both connected rooms equally,
as it does in a corridor.

## Tips and connections

- [Enemies](/docs/enemies) - which enemies and bosses appear on which floor.
- [Objects](/docs/dungeon/objects) - chests, gates, ladder, slot machine.
- [Progression](/docs/progression) - the talents *Discount*, *Gamble Machine*, *Armor Regeneration*
  (+1 armor after every cleared combat room); the *Merchant's Strike* challenge.
- [Minimap](/docs/dungeon/minimap) - every room type has its own icon.
- Rooms with many obstacles (normal_13, normal_16) are tight and safer against shooters,
  while large open arenas (normal_19, ambush_02) give room to dodge.
- Ambush pays off - the weapon chest is a second source of new weapons next to Treasure and the shop.

## Known inconsistencies

- Large Normal rooms have up to 24 enemy spawn spots ready, but only
  5-9 are used; the cap of 12 enemies per wave is never reached.
- The Start room shows fixed keys (Q/E/F/1/2/WASD/space) even though the controls can be
  remapped; there is no hint for the map (Tab).
- The "Cashout" win on the slot machine pays the same as "Double" (10 coins).
- Shop, Treasure, Sacrifice, Start and Boss each have only one form - they look the same on every floor,
  just in a different theme.

## Ideas for expansion

- The altar could speak - the story plans curses on entry ("YOU ARE MINE ALREADY"...).
- Treasure variants: a locked chest, a mimic chest, a choice of 1 of 3.
- Custom boss arenas with different mechanics (Golem, Ogre, Hellhound), possibly larger
  and with cover.
- Themed room shapes (e.g. water channels in Sewer), spots for flying
  or elite enemies only, fixed trap placement.
- A reward for clearing a Normal room, elite, curse or secret rooms, or a healing fountain.
