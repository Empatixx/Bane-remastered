title: Decor
description: Torches, banners, bones, cobwebs and themed extras - where they appear in rooms and what they do.
---

Decor gives every floor atmosphere: torches on walls, banners, piles of bones,
cobwebs and themed extras like icicles, glowing mushrooms or spider cocoons. Most
of it is purely visual, but some of it glows and so changes the lighting of the room. It is placed randomly
together with traps and barrels.

## How it works

- Decor never overlaps, does not lie on enemy spawn spots, and stays at least
  3 tiles from doors.
- Each kind has its typical spots: torches, banners and icicles hang on the face of a wall, cobwebs
  in corners by the side walls, bones lie loose on the floor, and mushrooms, crystals
  and cocoons press against the walls.
- A torch has a 10% chance of being **extinguished** and gives no light. On a front wall it faces forward, and on a side
  wall it is seen from the side.
- Bones, mushrooms, crystals, cocoons and cobwebs have 2 forms each, icicles have 3.

### How much decor where

| Decor | Normal | Ambush | Treasure | Start, Shop, Sacrifice, Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Torches | 2-5 (more in larger rooms) | 2-5 | 4 fixed | – |
| Banners | 2 | 2 | – | – |
| Cobwebs on walls | 2 (Spider 5) | – | 3 | – |
| Bones | 2 | 2 | 3 | – |
| Themed extras | by theme | by theme | – | – |

Start, Shop, Sacrifice and Boss have only their fixed furnishings (the merchant and tables, the altar, key
hints...). See the overview in [Room types](/docs/dungeon/rooms). The counts of themed extras
are in [Floors and themes](/docs/dungeon/themes).

### Overhead

In Normal and Ambush rooms (about 3 of 4), one or two objects lie across the gap between two wall blocks
(3-9 tiles), either across or vertically from the block to the block below it.
Two objects in one room do not cross. They are at the height of the wall tops and only rest against their
sides, without sticking out over the wall: a chain is hooked into an eye bolt in a plate on the wall, and beams
lie in an iron collar. They are drawn above the player, enemies and bullets, and cast a shadow on the floor
one wall height lower. When the player stands under them, they turn translucent.

| Theme | What lies between the walls |
|---|---|
| Stone | a grate, wooden beams, a chain |
| Temple | a stone arch with a keystone, a chain, a garland of red and gold pennants |
| Moss | a vine, a chain |
| Sewer | a pipe, a grate |
| Spider | thick cobweb, taut threads, beams |
| Ice | a chain, a grate |
| Lava | a metal walkway, a chain |

The chain, vine, cobweb and garland sag in the middle, and the arch rises. Treasure, shop, altar, Start
and boss arena have nothing overhead.

In addition, 1-3 things hang from the ceiling over free floor of a combat room (at least 2 tiles from walls
and from each other). They sway slightly on a chain or thread that vanishes into the darkness above, have a shadow
right beneath them, and turn translucent when the player walks under them.

| Theme | What hangs from the ceiling |
|---|---|
| Stone | a chandelier, a cage with a skeleton, a rusty hook under a wooden pulley |
| Temple | a golden censer, a bronze bell, a red pennant, a chandelier |
| Moss | a vine, roots with glowing mushrooms, a wasp nest, an overgrown cage, a swarm of fireflies |
| Sewer | a lantern, a cage |
| Spider | a cocoon on a thread, a cage |
| Ice | an ice chandelier, a skeleton frozen in a block of ice, a large icicle, a lantern |
| Lava | a glowing basket, a charred skeleton on hooks, an obsidian stalactite, a cage; a smelting cauldron over lava |

The chandelier and lantern flicker and give warm light, and the vine sways. On a Lava floor the glowing basket blazes
and glows orange, and the cauldron (it hangs only over lava, in about half the cases where it has somewhere to go) glows
and roughly every 2 s a glowing drop falls from it and makes a ring in the lava. The stalactite has pulsing glowing
veins and the skeleton on hooks swings. On a Moss floor the mushrooms on the roots pulse greenish, three wasps circle
the wasp nest, and the firefly swarm hangs from nothing, just slowly floats and blinks. On an Ice
floor the ice chandelier and the icicle glow a cold blue and sparkle. On a Temple floor smoke rises from the censer
and it glows, the pennant on its golden cord waves, and the bell, when the player shoots it, swings strongly for a few
seconds (along with its chain).

### Lobby

The lobby is a fixed, furnished room: 2 weapon racks, 4 torches, bones, cobwebs,
2 armor racks, a crate, chains, 4 stones, 2 bookcases and a map table. In addition a portal,
a notice board, a weapon table, 3 flowerpots and the Scientist. The lobby and the main menu both use the look of the
Stone theme.

## Tips and connections

- [Lighting](/docs/dungeon/lighting) - torches, mushrooms and candelabras are light sources.
- [Surfaces and liquids](/docs/dungeon/surfaces) - puddles avoid decor.
- [Traps and hazards](/docs/dungeon/hazards) - traps are placed in the same step.
- Extinguished torches make the corners of a room darker - on a Spider floor visibility tends to be worse.

## Known inconsistencies

- Start, Shop, Sacrifice and Boss rooms have no random decor - the boss arena
  looks bare.
- A cobweb on a wall is purely visual, it neither slows nor catches bullets.
- Cocoons, icicles and crystals have no mechanics.
- The banner looks the same in all themes.

## Ideas for expansion

- Skulls on stakes for Temple, themed banners on walls.
- The bell could also ring when hit (for now it only swings).
- Decor in boss arenas.
- The lobby could change its look depending on the player's progress.
