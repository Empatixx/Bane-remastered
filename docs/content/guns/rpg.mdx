The **RPG** is an area damage gun. The rocket explodes on contact with anything (an enemy, a wall or a breakable object) and hurts everything around it.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun with a single rocket in the magazine, so you have to reload (R) after every shot. The rocket flies slowly. On impact it explodes in an area of about 2.75 × 2.75 tiles and deals **full** damage to all enemies and breakable objects in it. The explosion never hurts the player.

## Tips and connections

- The **Magazine +1** node doubles the magazine (1 → 2). It is the strongest use of this node in the game.
- Against groups, the DPS is many times higher than the listed 6.4, because every enemy in the explosion takes the full hit.
- The explosion breaks barrels and pots nearby. See [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).
- An enemy hit directly takes no extra hit, only the one from the explosion.
- Of the mutations, only **Deadeye** works (the rocket explodes before any other mutation could take effect), so the RPG's tree always offers it.

## Known inconsistencies

- Explosions do not count as hits, so the RPG spoils the accuracy statistic and does not charge abilities that count hits.
- The weapon table shows damage 8–11, but 11 never lands.

## Ideas for expansion

- Mutations that make sense on a rocket, for example a poison pool after the explosion.
- An explosion that also hurts the player (risk and reward), or throws enemies back.
- Count the explosion in the hit statistics.
