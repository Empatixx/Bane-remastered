A mutation is the strongest gun upgrade. It sits at the very end of the main branch of the [weapon tree](/docs/guns/weapon-trees), a chain of three upgrades leads to it, and it costs **800 gold**. Each tree has exactly one mutation, and chance decides which one when the tree is created. The tree offers only mutations that do something on that gun.

## How it works

A mutation changes the behavior of every shot from the gun. Piercing and Ricochet take effect right at the shot, Deadeye counts shots, and the other mutations trigger when the projectile hits an enemy (Explosive on a wall too).

## Contents

| Mutation | In-game description | What it does exactly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piercing Rounds | Bullets pierce the first enemy | The projectile passes through one more enemy. |
| Explosive Rounds | Hits explode and hurt enemies nearby | Every hit on an enemy or a wall causes a small explosion (about 1.5 × 1.5 tiles) that deals half damage (min. 1) to nearby enemies and breakable objects. It does not hurt the target hit directly or the player. |
| Venom Rounds | Hits poison and leave a toxic pool | A hit on an enemy leaves a poison pool for 3 s that takes 1 HP per second from enemies inside. It does not hurt the player. No new pool appears if another one is already within 1 tile. |
| Frost Rounds | Hits chill and slow enemies | Every hit on an enemy slows it to half speed for 2.5 s. The fourth hit in a row freezes it for 1.2 s. Bosses do not freeze, they only slow down. |
| Ricochet | Bullets bounce off walls once | The projectile bounces off a wall once, and after the bounce it can hit the same enemy again. |
| Deadeye | Every fifth shot is a critical hit | Every fifth shot of the gun is a critical hit (×2). It is counted separately for each gun. |

## Tips and connections

- **Deadeye** works on every gun. On the Shotgun it makes all 4 pellets critical at once, on the M4 it crits every fifth shot (not every burst). On the RPG it is the only mutation that works, so its tree always offers it.
- **Piercing** is not offered for the Railgun or Flamethrower, because their projectiles already pass through everything.
- **Frost** suits fast-firing guns best (Uzi, Minigun, Flamethrower), because four hits come in a moment.
- **Explosive** and **Venom** improve guns that have no area damage against groups.
- **Piercing** adds to the projectile's own pierce. With it, the Sniper shoots through 3 enemies and stops in the fourth.
- If a mutation does not suit you, the tree can be rerolled for 100 gold (all spent gold is returned). See [Weapon trees](/docs/guns/weapon-trees).
- Slowing and freezing enemies: [Enemies](/docs/enemies). Enemy poison pools work the same way but hurt the player: [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).

## Known inconsistencies

- **Ricochet** on the Flamethrower does almost nothing. The flame goes out before it reaches a wall, unless the player stands right next to it.
- The Piercing description "pierce the first enemy" fits only guns that are not piercing already.
- The game never shows the mutation numbers (explosion size, poison duration, how many hits freeze).

## Ideas for expansion

- **Chain**: every hit jumps with lightning like on the Tesla Gun.
- **Vampiric**: part of the damage heals the player.
- **Homing**: projectiles curve toward enemies.
- **Split**: on a hit, the projectile splits into two smaller ones.
- **Burn**: a hit sets the enemy on fire for a few seconds.
- **Midas**: a kill gives extra coins (in the spirit of the Midas Coin artefact).
- The option to choose from two mutations instead of one fixed one.
