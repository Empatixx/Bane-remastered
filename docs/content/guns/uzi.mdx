The **Uzi** is an automatic "sprayer": it pours out 10 shots per second, but each does only 1 and the shots spread a lot.

## How it works

An automatic gun: it fires as long as the player holds the button. Each shot is a plain bullet with a spread of up to ±11.5°.

## Tips and connections

- Many shots = many chances for mutations that trigger on a hit: **Frost** freezes an enemy in under half a second, and **Venom** and **Explosive** trigger constantly. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- The Scientist's damage upgrades add an extra point on only some of its shots (with *Sharpened Bullets*, every fifth). The weapon tree strengthens it more.
- A magazine of 20 empties in 2 seconds, so **Magazine** and **Reload** nodes pay off.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 1–2, but the Uzi always does 1.
- With many **Fire rate** nodes, the screen shakes almost constantly while the trigger is held.
