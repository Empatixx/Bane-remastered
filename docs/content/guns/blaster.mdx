The **Blaster** is a sci-fi semi-automatic. For a semi-automatic gun it fires quickly (4.5 shots per second), it is accurate and every laser does 2–3.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun: one laser per click, with a spread of only ±1.4°. The laser flies very fast and leaves red glowing particles behind it in flight.

## Tips and connections

- The best semi-automatic for DPS after the Shotgun. The fast laser also hits easily at range.
- The fire rate depends on how fast the player clicks. **Fire rate** nodes help only if the player can keep up.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 2–4, but 4 never lands.
- The laser is refilled with ordinary ammo boxes.
