The **Crossbow** is an accurate mid-range gun. A bolt does 5–7 and passes through one enemy.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun with no spread. The bolt flies fast and has **pierce 1**: it passes through the first enemy and stops in the second.

## Tips and connections

- The magazine is small (3), so **Magazine** nodes are relatively strongest here (+3 magazine doubles it).
- The **Piercing** mutation raises pierce to 2. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- The crossbow's tree does not offer **Accuracy** nodes, because the spread is already zero.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 5–8, but 8 never lands.

## Ideas for expansion

- Bolts stuck in a wall that can be picked up again (returns ammo).
- Poison or fire bolts as variants of their own.
