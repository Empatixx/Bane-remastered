Each gun has its own **upgrade tree**, bought with gold at the [weapon table](/docs/guns/weapon-table) in the lobby. Purchased upgrades are permanent. They apply in every later run to every copy of that gun, whether the player gets it at the start, finds it in a chest or buys it in the shop.

## How it works

### Shape of the tree

A tree has 4 tiers and up to 5 columns. It looks different for each gun, but it is fixed for that gun until the player rerolls it.

- **Main branch**: three upgrades in a row (Common → Rare → Epic) and a **mutation** at the end, on tier 4.
- **Side branches**: 1 to 2, 1–3 nodes deep, sometimes branching further.
- Each node gives one of 6 upgrades at random, but only one that does something for that gun. Perfectly accurate guns (M4, Sniper, Crossbow, Railgun) do not get **Accuracy**, and guns with a magazine of 40 (Minigun, Flamethrower) do not get **Magazine**.
- The same goes for mutations: the Railgun and Flamethrower, whose projectiles already pass through everything, do not get **Piercing**, and the RPG, whose rocket explodes before a mutation can take effect, always gets **Deadeye**.
- You can only buy a node that hangs directly on the root or on an already purchased node.

### Cost and strength by tier

| Tier | Quality | Bonus strength | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Common | ×1 | 100 gold |
| 2 | Rare | ×2 | 200 gold |
| 3 | Epic | ×3 | 300 gold |
| 4 (mutation) | Epic | none | 800 gold |

So the whole main branch including the mutation always costs **1,400 gold**.

### Reroll

The dice below the tree rerolls the tree. The player gets back **all** the gold spent on this tree, pays a fee of **100 gold** and gets a completely new tree: a different shape, different nodes and a different mutation. Purchased nodes are lost. You can reroll only if the player has at least 100 (including the returned gold).

When the shape of the trees changes in a new version of the game, the game automatically refunds the spent gold and resets the tree. This last happened when trees stopped offering useless nodes, and the old trees were refunded then.

## Stats

| Upgrade | Common | Rare | Epic | What it does | In-game description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage | +5% | +10% | +15% | higher damage on every shot; fractions of a point turn into a chance of an extra point | "+N% damage — on every shot" |
| Fire rate | +4% | +8% | +12% | shorter pause between shots | "+N% fire rate — less time between shots" |
| Magazine | +1 | +2 | +3 | bigger magazine, 40 at most | "+N magazine size — more rounds before reload" |
| Reload | +6% | +12% | +18% | faster reload | "+N% reload speed — back in the fight sooner" |
| Accuracy | +8% | +16% | +24% | smaller spread | "+N% less spread — bullets fly straighter" |
| Crit | +4% | +8% | +12% | higher chance of a critical hit (×2) | "+N% crit chance — crits deal double damage" |

The same upgrades in a tree are numbered with Roman numerals in order: Damage I, Damage II, Damage III.

## Contents

The exact shape of a tree differs from gun to gun and changes with every reroll, so it cannot be listed. But every tree has:

- 1 mutation on tier 4 and 3 upgrades on the way to it,
- 1–2 side branches, so at least 5 nodes in total,
- a main branch for 1,400 gold and side nodes for 100–300 gold.

## Tips and connections

- **Magazine +1** on the RPG doubles the magazine (1 → 2). It is the strongest use of this node in the game.
- **Damage** works on weak guns with damage 1 too. A fraction of a point turns into a chance of an extra point, just like with the Scientist upgrades.
- If a tree offers weak nodes or a mutation that does not suit you, it is worth rerolling before the player invests a lot. The gold comes back, but the 100 fee does not.
- What the final node does: [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- Gold is earned from runs and is a different currency from the points for Scientist upgrades: [Progression](/docs/progression).

## Known inconsistencies

- Fire rate has no upper limit. With many Fire rate nodes, the screen on the Uzi and Minigun can shake almost constantly.
- The tree does not show the total cost or how much gold is left to the mutation.

## Ideas for expansion

- Gun-specific nodes: +1 pellet for the Shotgun, +1 lightning jump for the Tesla, a bigger explosion for the RPG.
- A fork at the end of the tree: a choice of two mutations.
- Expensive "prestige" nodes for players who have bought out the tree.
- Node weights per gun, so that what fits it is offered more often.
