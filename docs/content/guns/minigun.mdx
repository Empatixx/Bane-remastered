The **Minigun** is a gun for continuous fire: almost 17 shots per second, the biggest magazine in the game (40) and the longest reload (2.2 s).

## How it works

An automatic gun: it fires as long as the player holds the button. It fires yellow tracers that fly faster than plain bullets, with a spread of about ±8.6°.

## Tips and connections

- The highest DPS after the Shotgun, and at any distance.
- The magazine lasts only 2.4 s of fire and the reload takes 2.2 s, so **Reload** nodes are the most valuable upgrade.
- An ideal carrier for the **Frost** mutation: the four hits needed to freeze come within a quarter of a second. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- The Minigun's tree does not offer **Magazine** nodes, because the magazine is already at the maximum of 40.
- The Scientist's damage upgrades add an extra point on only some of its shots (with *Sharpened Bullets*, every fifth). The weapon tree strengthens it more.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 1–2, but the Minigun always does 1.
- With **Fire rate** nodes, the screen shakes almost constantly while the trigger is held.

## Ideas for expansion

- Barrel spin-up: the fire rate grows as the player holds the trigger.
- Slowing the player while shooting, as a counterweight to its power.
