title: Projectiles

Each gun fires one kind of projectile. Most guns use a plain bullet, but special guns have their own shots: a rocket, flame, laser, plasma, spark, arc or rail shot. Each projectile has its own light color, so it is easy to see in the dark of the dungeon.

## How it works

- **Speed**: how fast the projectile flies. For comparison, the player runs about 6.4 tiles per second.
- **Pierce**: how many enemies the projectile passes through before it stops. The Piercing mutation adds +1.
- **Lifetime**: flame goes out on its own after 0.45 s. Other projectiles fly until they hit something.
- **Explosion** (rocket): on contact with anything it explodes and hurts all enemies and breakable objects in an area of about 2.75 × 2.75 tiles for **full** damage. It does not hurt the player.
- **Splash** (plasma): after a hit, enemies and breakable objects within about 1.25 × 1.25 tiles take half the damage (min. 1). The target hit directly gets no splash.
- **Chain lightning** (spark): after a hit, lightning jumps twice to the nearest other enemy within about 3.4 tiles, each jump for full damage.
- **Arc**: like chain lightning, but it jumps **four** times and every jump keeps only 75 % of the damage before it (at least 1).
- **Trail**: the laser, spark and arc leave glowing particles behind.
- Elongated projectiles (tracer, bolt, rocket, laser, rail) rotate in the direction of flight. Round ones do not.

Speed is in tiles per second (t/s). After impact, each projectile plays a short hit animation.

## Tips and connections

- Piercing projectiles (Sniper, Crossbow, Railgun, Flamethrower) are strongest against enemies in a line, in corridors and doorways.
- The rocket and plasma also break barrels and pots nearby. See [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon).
- [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations) add pierce, ricochet, explosion, venom or frost.
- The order in which effects are evaluated on impact: [Shooting](/docs/guns/shooting).

## Known inconsistencies

- After exploding, the rocket ignores mutations. Explosive, Venom, Frost, Piercing and Ricochet do nothing on the RPG.
- Flame does not set things on fire. It is just a short piercing projectile.
- Explosions, splashes and the Explosive mutation do not count toward accuracy or toward abilities that count hits.
- The Sniper ejects shotgun shells instead of rifle casings.
