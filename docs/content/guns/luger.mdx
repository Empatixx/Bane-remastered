The **Luger** is an alternative pistol that can be found in a chest, the shop or the slot machine. It has a bigger magazine and reserve than the starting Pistol, but every shot always does just 1 damage and it spreads a lot.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun: one plain bullet per click, with a large spread of about ±10°.

## Tips and connections

- **Damage** and **Crit** nodes in the [weapon tree](/docs/guns/weapon-trees) and the Scientist's damage upgrades strengthen the Luger. *Sharpened Bullets* gives it an extra point on every fifth shot.
- **Accuracy** nodes help it more than most guns.
- It sounds the same as the Pistol.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 1–2, but 2 never lands. The Luger always does 1.
- It is a Pistol that is worse in every way (DPS 4 against 6).

## Ideas for expansion

- Give the Luger its own identity: a higher crit chance, a faster fire rate or better accuracy.
- Its own shot sound.
