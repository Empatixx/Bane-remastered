Bane has no locked guns. The player can find all 17 right in the first run. Guns are only upgraded permanently through the [weapon tree](/docs/guns/weapon-trees).

## How it works

### Where to find guns

| Source | Where | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting loadout | start of the run | Pistol |
| Gun chest | in the middle of the treasure room, once per floor | 1 gun + 5 items |
| Gun chest after an ambush | appears after clearing an ambush room | 1 gun + 5 items |
| Shop | the "Offensive" table, 50% chance it offers a gun (otherwise an artefact) | a gun for coins |
| Slot machine | one of the possible wins | a gun thrown out of the machine |
| Own dropped gun | anywhere | whatever the player dropped (Q) or swapped out |

A chest opens on touch. After a short animation it disappears, scatters items and throws out a gun. A chest after a boss gives an artefact instead of a gun.

### Which gun drops

Always a random gun the player is **not holding** right now, all with equal chance. Floor and gun strength do not matter. If the player holds all allowed guns (or plays the Pistol Only challenge), nothing drops.

A new gun has a full magazine, a full reserve (including the *Additional Ammo* upgrade) and all upgrades from its tree. It lies on the ground and is picked up with **E**.

## Tips and connections

- Per floor the player finds one chest in the treasure room and another for each ambush room. With the shop and the slot machine, the slots usually fill up quickly, so you have to choose what to keep.
- The same gun never drops twice while the player carries it, but a dropped gun goes back into the draw.
- [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon): the treasure room, ambush rooms, the shop, the slot machine.
- [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items): what the chests scatter.
- [Slots and ammo](/docs/guns/loadout): what happens after pickup.

## Known inconsistencies

- Guns have no rarity. The Railgun can drop in the first room as easily as the Luger.
- Enemies and bosses do not drop guns.
- An older game plan counted on unlocking guns for meta currency. It is not in the game yet.

## Ideas for expansion

- Gun tiers by floor, or weights by strength.
- Unlocking guns by first bringing them out of a run (it fits the story of the weapon table).
- Unique legendary gun variants with a fixed mutation, dropping only from bosses.
- Dismantling an unneeded gun into ammo or coins.
