title: Sniper
description: A sniper rifle with a piercing shot, a built-in 20% crit chance and zero spread.

The **Sniper** is a slow but precise gun. Every shot does 9–12, flies very fast and shoots through two enemies. It is the only gun with its own crit chance.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun with no spread. The shot flies very fast and has **pierce 2**: it passes through two enemies and stops in the third, so it hits up to three targets.

## Tips and connections

- Its own 20% crit adds to the *Critical Hit* upgrade (+10%) and **Crit** nodes in the tree. With everything together it crits almost every second shot.
- The **Piercing** mutation raises pierce to 3 (up to 4 enemies hit), and **Deadeye** guarantees a crit on every fifth shot. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- Strongest against enemies in a line, in corridors and in doorways.
- The Sniper's tree does not offer **Accuracy** nodes, because the spread is already zero.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 9–13, but 13 never lands.
- It ejects shotgun shells instead of long rifle casings.
