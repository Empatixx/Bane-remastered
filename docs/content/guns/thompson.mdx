The **Thompson** is an easygoing automatic gun: 5 shots per second, almost no spread and a magazine of 30 rounds.

## How it works

An automatic gun: it fires as long as the player holds the button. Plain bullets fly almost exactly where the player aims (±1.4°).

## Tips and connections

- Thanks to its accuracy, it is reliable at longer range, where the Uzi and Minigun scatter.
- The big reserve (300) means you almost never run out of ammo.
- **Accuracy** nodes are almost useless on it. **Damage** and **Fire rate** are better. See [Weapon trees](/docs/guns/weapon-trees).

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 1–3, but 3 never lands.
- It sounds the same as the Pistol. It has no sound of its own.
