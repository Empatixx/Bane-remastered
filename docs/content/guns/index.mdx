Bane is a top-down shooter, so the gun is the main thing the player holds. There are **16 guns** in the game, from the starting pistol through the SMGs and the shotgun to the rocket launcher, flamethrower, tesla gun and railgun. Each gun fires one of **10 projectile types**, has its own permanent **upgrade tree**, and ends that tree with one of **6 mutations**.

## How it works

1. **In the lobby**, the player uses gold from earlier runs to buy upgrades for specific guns at the [weapon table](/docs/guns/weapon-table). These last forever.
2. **At the start of a run**, the player always gets a **Pistol** and a **Shotgun**. Tree upgrades apply right away, and so do the Scientist upgrades (a third gun slot, a bigger ammo reserve, higher damage and crit chance).
3. **During a run**, the player aims with the mouse, shoots with the left button, reloads with R, switches guns with keys 1-9 or the scroll wheel, drops with Q and picks up with E.
4. **New guns** drop from gun chests (the treasure room and a cleared ambush room), can be bought in the shop and won at the slot machine. It is always a gun the player does not have right now, with a full magazine and all purchased upgrades.
5. **Between floors**, the player keeps the whole loadout, including magazine states. When the run ends, the loadout is lost. Only the upgrade trees are permanent.

## Tips and connections

- [Player](/docs/player): the player cannot shoot during a dodge or while frozen.
- [Progression](/docs/progression): gold from runs pays for the weapon trees. The Scientist upgrades give *Sharpened Bullets* and *Dangerous Bullets* (damage), *Critical Hit* (+10% crit), *Additional Ammo* (reserve ×1.1) and *Additional Gun* (+1 slot). The *Pistol Only* challenge allows only the pistol.
- [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items): ammo boxes, Infinity Quiver (8 s of infinite ammo), Phantom (the first sneak attack for double damage), Ring of Death (50 bullets of 2), Bone Turret (shards of 2), Thunder Idol (chain lightning).
- [Dungeon](/docs/dungeon): gun chests, the shop and the slot machine.
- [Enemies](/docs/enemies): the player's shots do not collide with enemy shots.

## Known inconsistencies

- Pistol and Luger (DPS 6 and 4) are almost useless next to the starting **Shotgun** (DPS 20).
- There is no **rarity or gun progression**. All 14 non-starting guns drop equally often from floor 1, so the Railgun can drop in the very first chest.
- Hits do **not push enemies back** (no knockback).
- The gun is **not visible in the player's hand**. Its image is only in the HUD and on the ground, and there is no muzzle flash either.
- There are only two kinds of ammo: shotgun shells for the Shotgun and "bullets" for everything else. The rocket launcher, flamethrower, laser and railgun are all refilled by the same box as the pistol.
- The older design document lists 7 guns and a Shotgun with damage 222–333. The game has 16 guns and the Shotgun does 2–3 per pellet.

## Ideas for expansion

- Unlock guns through meta progression, for example a gun appears on the table only after the player first brings it out of a run.
- Gun tiers by floor, so that strong pieces (Railgun, Minigun) come later.
- New gun types: a charged gun (hold for a stronger shot), a beam, a boomerang, a grenade launcher with bounce, a gun that summons helpers.
