The **Revolver** is a stronger version of the pistol: every shot does 4–5, at the cost of a slower fire rate and a smaller reserve.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun: one plain bullet per click, with a spread of about ±5.7°. It has its own shot sound and reloads with the pistol sound.

## Stats

| --- | --- |
| Damage per shot | 4–5 (average 4.5) |

| DPS | 10 |

| Ammo | ammo box, universal box |

Values are without tree upgrades and without player upgrades.

## Tips and connections

- Percentage damage upgrades (*Sharpened Bullets*, *Dangerous Bullets*) add whole points more often here than on weak guns. On average, though, they strengthen every gun by the same share.
- It fits well with the **Deadeye** and **Explosive** mutations. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 4–6, but 6 never lands.

## Ideas for expansion

- Reloading one round at a time, which can be interrupted.
- "Fan the hammer": holding the button quickly empties the cylinder with a larger spread.
