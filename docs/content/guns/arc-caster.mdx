The **Arc Caster** is the newest energy gun. It is an automatic like the Plasma Rifle, but its violet bolt does not splash: after a hit it **arcs on to four more enemies**, and every jump is weaker than the last. Like all energy guns it runs on a **battery** and never reloads.

## How it works

Hold the button and it fires about 4.5 bolts per second with a small spread (±2.3°). The bolt flies at medium speed, leaves violet sparks and lights its surroundings violet. After it hits an enemy, the lightning jumps up to **4 times**, each time to the nearest enemy not yet hit within about 3.4 tiles of the last target. Each jump keeps **75 %** of the damage of the jump before it (never less than 1).

With the base damage of 2 the arc deals 2, 2, 1, 1 and 1 to five enemies in a row, 7 in total.

## Tips and connections

- Weak against a single enemy, but the best gun for a crowd in a small room: five enemies in a chain take more than the Tesla Gun's three.
- Compared with the [Tesla Gun](/docs/guns/tesla-gun): the Arc Caster is automatic and has a bigger battery, but its jumps lose strength. The Tesla's jumps do full damage.
- Because the arc loses 25 % per jump, damage nodes in the [weapon tree](/docs/guns/weapon-trees) help the later jumps most.
- Lightning jumps count as hits, so they charge abilities that count hits.
- The lightning does not jump to barrels and pots, or to enemies that are only just appearing.
- How the battery works: [Slots and ammo](/docs/guns/loadout#batteries).

## Known inconsistencies

- With the damage of 2 the falloff is invisible: the first two targets take the same damage, because 1.5 rounds up to 2.

## Ideas for expansion

- A tree node that adds a fifth jump or removes the falloff.
- A mutation that makes the arc return to the first target.
