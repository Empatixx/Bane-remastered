The **M4** is the only gun in the game that fires **bursts**. It is perfectly accurate and every shot does a steady 2 damage.

## How it works

Just hold the button: the M4 fires burst after burst. Each burst has **4 shots** 0.05 s apart and aims where the player aimed at the moment of the first shot. Moving the mouse during the burst does not turn it. After a burst there is a 0.75 s pause, and the whole cycle takes about 0.9 s.

## Tips and connections

- You cannot reload during a burst, so reload between bursts.
- The **Deadeye** mutation counts each shot of a burst separately, so it crits every fifth shot, not every burst.
- The [weapon tree](/docs/guns/weapon-trees) does not offer the M4 **Accuracy** nodes, because its spread is already zero.
- Only the first shot of a burst gets the sneak attack bonus (the Phantom artefact).
- Careful against fast enemies: the locked direction means the late shots of a burst can miss.

## Known inconsistencies

- Only the first shot of a burst is audible.

## Ideas for expansion

- A sound for every shot of the burst, or one longer burst sound.
