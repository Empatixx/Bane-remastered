The **Railgun** is a heavy precision gun. Every shot does 14–19, flies the fastest of all projectiles and passes through all enemies in its way. In return it fires slowly and its battery holds only 24 shots, the smallest of all energy guns.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun with no spread. The shot has a larger hit area than the others and **unlimited pierce**. Only a wall stops it.

## Tips and connections

- Strongest against enemies in a line: in a corridor it hits all of them at once.
- With the Phantom artefact (the first sneak attack ×2) and a critical hit, one shot does over 70.
- It fits well with the **Deadeye** mutation and **Crit** nodes. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- The Railgun's tree offers neither the **Piercing** mutation (the shot already passes through everything) nor **Accuracy** nodes (the spread is zero).
- The reserve is small (24), so save it for strong enemies and bosses.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 14–20, but 20 never lands.
- The Railgun can drop in the very first chest on floor 1.

## Ideas for expansion

- Charging before the shot, which would set the Railgun apart from the Sniper.
- A visual beam across the whole path instead of a flying projectile.
