The **Pistol** is the first gun the player holds. The player gets it in the first slot at the start of every run. It is accurate and reloads quickly, but its damage is very low: in the game it only lands 1 or 2.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun: one shot per click, holding the button does not shoot. It fires one plain bullet with a small spread. When the magazine is empty, you have to reload with R.

## Tips and connections

- The only gun allowed in the **Pistol Only** challenge. See [Slots and ammo](/docs/guns/loadout).
- Because of its low damage, the pistol is mainly a backup gun. **Damage** nodes in the [weapon tree](/docs/guns/weapon-trees) and the Scientist's damage upgrades help it. With *Sharpened Bullets*, a shot worth 1 does 2 every fifth shot.
- The **Deadeye** or **Frost** mutation keeps it useful on later floors. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 1–3, but 3 never lands.
- The starting pistol has DPS 6 while a found Shotgun has 20, so the Pistol is dropped as soon as a better gun turns up.

## Ideas for expansion

- Raise the damage to 2–3 or give the pistol its own crit bonus.
- According to the story, the player's mirror boss shoots a pistol, which leaves room for a unique "Scientist's Pistol" variant.
