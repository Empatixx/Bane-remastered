The **Plasma Rifle** is an automatic gun with an area effect: over 6 shots per second, and every hit hurts the enemies around too.

## How it works

An automatic gun: it fires as long as the player holds the button. Green plasma balls **splash** on impact (on a wall too): all enemies and breakable objects within about 1.25 × 1.25 tiles take half damage (at least 1). The enemy hit directly takes only the full hit, not the splash.

## Tips and connections

- Strong against clusters of enemies. Even a shot that misses can splash enemies by a wall.
- The splash breaks barrels and pots nearby.
- **Damage** nodes raise the splash too: from damage 4, the surroundings take 2.

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 2–3, but 3 never lands.
- Splashes do not count as hits for accuracy or for abilities that count hits.

## Ideas for expansion
