The **Tesla Gun** is a chain damage gun. After a hit, the blue spark jumps with lightning to two more enemies, and each takes a full hit.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun with a small spread (±2.9°). The spark flies at medium speed and leaves blue sparkles behind it. After hitting an enemy, the **lightning jumps twice**, each time to the nearest enemy not yet hit within about 3.4 tiles of the last target. Each jump deals **full** damage and crackles.

## Tips and connections

- It is weak against a single enemy, but against a group it is one of the strongest guns.
- Lightning jumps count as hits, so they charge abilities that count hits.
- The lightning does not jump to barrels and pots, or to enemies that are only just appearing.
- The **Thunder Idol** artefact has its own, stronger chain lightning. See [Artefacts and items](/docs/artefacts-items).

## Known inconsistencies

- The weapon table shows damage 3–5, but 5 never lands.
- Tesla's lightning and the Thunder Idol's lightning each work differently (different range, number of targets and look).
