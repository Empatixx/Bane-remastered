The **Flamethrower** is an automatic close-range gun. It pours out over 14 flames per second, each passes through any number of enemies and goes out after 0.45 s.

## How it works

An automatic gun: it fires as long as the player holds the button. The flames fly slowly and with the largest spread in the game (±14.3°). They travel only about **2.7 tiles** and then go out. Only a wall stops them. They pass through enemies.

## Tips and connections

- Against a cluster of enemies up close, the damage is much higher than the listed DPS, because every flame hits everyone in its path.
- It fits very well with the **Frost** (fast freezing) and **Venom** mutations. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- The Scientist's damage upgrades add an extra point on only some of the flames (with *Sharpened Bullets*, every fifth). The weapon tree strengthens it more.

## Known inconsistencies

- The flame does **not set things on fire**. There is no burning, it is just a short piercing projectile.
- The **Ricochet** mutation is almost useless, because the flame usually goes out before it reaches a wall. (The Flamethrower's tree offers neither **Piercing** nor **Magazine** nodes, because the flame already passes through everything and the magazine is at the maximum of 40.)
- The weapon table shows damage 1–2, but the flame always does 1.
- A flame touching two enemies at once can apparently hit them alternately and repeatedly, so the damage may be higher than expected.

## Ideas for expansion

- Burning: a hit enemy loses HP for a few seconds. The dungeon already has fire traps whose look could be reused.
- Igniting flammable objects in the room.
