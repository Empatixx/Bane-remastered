The **Shotgun** is not a starting gun: it is found in a chest, bought in the shop or won in the slot machine. One shell fires four pellets, and up close it has no rival. It is the only gun in the game that uses its own shotgun ammo.

## How it works

A semi-automatic gun. One shell fires **4 pellets** in a fixed fan: straight, −2.9°, +5.7° and −8.6°. The fan is always the same, with no randomness. Each pellet has its own damage roll and its own crit roll, so from one shot only one pellet may be critical.

## Tips and connections

- It is strongest up close, where all four pellets hit. At range the fan opens up and some pellets miss.
- **Accuracy** nodes in the [weapon tree](/docs/guns/weapon-trees) narrow the pellet fan too.
- The **Deadeye** mutation makes all 4 pellets critical at once, so every fifth shot is devastating. See [Mutations](/docs/guns/mutations).
- The fired-projectiles statistic counts pellets, not shells: one shot = 4 projectiles.
- Ammo boxes do not refill the Shotgun. It needs a shotgun shell box or a universal one.

## Known inconsistencies

- The fan is not symmetrical: the pellets fly more to one side than the other.
- The weapon table shows damage 2–4, but 4 never lands.
- The older design document lists damage 222–333 (a leftover from tuning). The game has 2–3.

## Ideas for expansion

- A symmetrical pellet fan.
- A "+1 pellet" tree node as a shotgun-specific upgrade.
